Bind its stated coverage immediately; continue only when the original request, that record, or other already-observed current authoritative evidence identifies a material uncovered rule, dependency, or authority conflict, not merely to find a separately named guideline or policy. Preserve genuinely unresolved source facts. Do not mutate any external state.
