# Phase-one presentation review

Inspect the actual PNG rendered from every slide of the final PPTX.

For each slide, check:

- the intended title and all required content are present;
- no text, picture, chart, label, or shape is clipped or outside the canvas;
- body type is readable and the hierarchy is obvious;
- alignment, margins, and spacing are consistent;
- contrast is sufficient and color carries no unsupported meaning;
- charts use the correct categories, series, units, labels, and scale;
- images are not stretched, broken, or used without useful alt text;
- the slide contains no unresolved placeholder or accidental blank region; and
- sources or notes appear where the content contract requires them.

Across the deck, check:

- the narrative order answers the user's question;
- repeated elements use one visual system;
- slide density varies intentionally instead of monotonously;
- the last slide gives the requested conclusion or action; and
- the deliverable filename and title match the request.

An independent child review is advisory evidence, not a boolean gate. The
parent Work conversation owns corrections, the final validation call, and the
final delivery call.
