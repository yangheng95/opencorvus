# Presentation review

Inspect every fresh PNG: each PPTX slide, DOCX page, or XLSX worksheet.
For DOCX/XLSX also open the derived PDF preview. Verify complete paragraphs,
rows, columns, numeric totals and page boundaries against the requested source.

For every slide, verify required content, clipping, readability, hierarchy,
alignment, spacing, contrast, chart categories/series/units/scale, image aspect
ratio and alt text, placeholders, blank regions, and required sources.

Do not put heterogeneous units such as currency, counts, and percentages on a
single raw-value axis. Use separate charts, a clearly labelled and justified
secondary axis, or an explicit normalized-index comparison. Keep source and
provenance notes as text, never as dummy chart categories or series. Repair a
chart when scale dominance makes any meaningful series illegible.

Across the deck, verify narrative order, visual consistency, intentional
density, requested conclusion/action, filename, and title.

An independent child review is advisory evidence. The parent Work conversation
owns corrections, final validation, exact receipt selection, and delivery.
