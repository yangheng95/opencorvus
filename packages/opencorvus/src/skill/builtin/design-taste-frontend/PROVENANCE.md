# Provenance

`design-taste-frontend` is an OpenCorvus adaptation of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) licensed Taste Skill by Leonxlnx.

Sources reviewed on 2026-08-03:

- Repository and installation documentation: <https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill>
- Default v2 Skill: <https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/blob/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md>
- Generative Pre-trained Transformer (GPT) oriented variant: <https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/blob/main/skills/gpt-tasteskill/SKILL.md>
- Upstream MIT license: <https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/blob/main/LICENSE>

The adaptation retains the upstream ideas of brief inference, contextual design dials, anti-template bias correction, mature design-system selection, intentional hierarchy and motion, redesign audit, and rendered pre-flight inspection.

It replaces upstream framework, marketing-page, mobile, dark-mode, asset-placeholder, fallback, and simulated-randomization defaults with OpenCorvus contracts. The resulting Skill supports product interfaces such as Prism's dense financial surfaces while preserving the active Expert Squad, Product Requirements, evidence, platform, and design-system authority.
