# Code of Conduct

OpenCorvus adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) for all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Critique ideas and changes rather than people. Welcome participants with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Do not harass others, publish private information, use sexualized language or imagery, or engage in personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory conduct.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, limit participation, or permanently ban participants when behavior is inconsistent with this policy. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, recurrence, and the safety of affected participants.

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner through the contact method published on their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue when a report contains sensitive or identifying information. Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as the available communication channel permits.
