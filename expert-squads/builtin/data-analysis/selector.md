# Data Analysis & Business Insights selection

Select when the operator supplies or identifies business data, metric definitions, a time boundary, and decision questions. Use Deep Research for broad public-source questions and Equity Research for public-company valuation.

Use only `operating-insight-report`. Before the first dispatch, visibly state the exact workflow and full dependency graph. Every node is mandatory and executes once. Dispatch the two analysis branches together after their shared evidence predecessor reaches terminal success.

Treat correlation as observation, not causation. Never invent missing values, silently change metric definitions, or present an estimate as a source fact.
