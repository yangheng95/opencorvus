# Selection instructions

Select this package for bounded operational finance work such as source reconciliation, close readiness, variance investigation, receivables or payables workflow review, management-report preparation, evidence retention, or control remediation.

Require the reporting entity or operating unit, period, currency and units, accounting basis or management definition, source systems, requested decision, and accountable human reviewer. If the request asks for investment, financial, tax, audit, or legal advice, an official filing, an accounting conclusion, a payment, a journal posting, a certification, or an external transaction, keep those outcomes outside this package and require the appropriate qualified professional and operator approval.
