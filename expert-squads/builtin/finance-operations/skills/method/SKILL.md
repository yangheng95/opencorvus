# Finance operations method

This Skill was authored clean-room for OpenCorvus.

## Method

1. Fix the entity or operating unit, period, as-of date, currency, units, accounting basis or management definition, materiality context supplied by the operator, source systems, requested decision, and accountable reviewer.
2. Preserve source lineage. Every balance, movement, variance, assumption, exception, and proposed adjustment must identify its source, period, definition, units, transformation, and reconciliation status.
3. Analyze records and controls independently. Record analysis reconciles opening, activity, closing, subsidiary schedules, statements, and unexplained differences. Control analysis maps preparer, reviewer, approver, segregation, evidence, cutoff, exception, and retention responsibilities.
4. Never net unexplained differences, silently change definitions, invent a missing source, infer approval, or convert a proposed adjustment into a posted fact.
5. Join both analyses into one sequenced close or remediation plan. Each step names its source evidence, dependency, accountable owner, reviewer, due condition, completion evidence, unresolved exception, and required human approval.
6. Keep actuals, estimates, forecasts, scenarios, and recommendations visibly distinct. State precision and materiality limits supplied by the operator rather than inventing universal thresholds.
7. Independently reproduce material reconciliations and verify that the plan cannot imply a filing, posting, payment, certification, or professional conclusion.

## Non-advisory and human-review boundary

This package does not provide financial, investment, accounting, audit, legal, or tax advice. It does not determine compliance, sign an opinion, file a return, post a journal entry, release or route a payment, move funds, open or close an account, certify a control, approve a close, or bind an organization. All material classifications, adjustments, filings, tax positions, payments, control conclusions, and external communications require review and approval by the accountable controller, finance leader, qualified accountant, auditor, tax professional, legal professional, or other authorized human as applicable.
