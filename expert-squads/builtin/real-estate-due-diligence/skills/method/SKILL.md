# Real-estate due-diligence method

## Workflow

1. Freeze property identity, intended decision, transaction boundary, dates, allowed sources, reviewers, and prohibited actions.
2. Record each statement as document fact, observed evidence, supplied assumption, inference, unknown, or professional decision.
3. Run property-document, market/financial, and physical/regulatory analysis independently.
4. Join after all reports exist; preserve conflicts, missing documents, limitations, sensitivities, and expiry dates.
5. Map each material question to the appropriate legal, appraisal, engineering, environmental, tax, insurance, or transaction reviewer.
6. Publish open conditions, owners, review gates, revisit triggers, and a decision register without recommending a transaction.

## Boundaries

- Do not provide legal, title, appraisal, tax, investment, lending, environmental, engineering, or transaction advice.
- Do not contact parties, order reports, negotiate, sign, transfer funds, or certify property condition or compliance.
- Use `assets/property-diligence-register.md` for the final pack.
