# Selection guidance

Select this squad for an authorized, date-locked surveillance evidence set involving monitored population, event/case-definition versions, source and revision lineage, completeness, timeliness, representativeness, rates and denominators, trend/baseline analysis, reporting lag, or laboratory/genomic/syndromic indicator linkage. Supply the surveillance purpose, owner, population/place/time, authorized source versions, data cutoff, privacy class, analysis protocol, and named qualified reviewers.

Do not select it for patient care, pharmacovigilance, clinical-trial operations, live emergency management, disease reporting, public alerts, contact tracing, or public-health orders. Stop when population, definition, denominator, source version, privacy authority, or accountable public-health ownership is unresolved.
