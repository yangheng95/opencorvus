# Public Health Surveillance

This package runs three evidence-only branches in parallel: surveillance-system/case-definition/data quality, descriptive measure/trend/signal analysis, and laboratory/genomic/syndromic indicator integration. `public-health-surveillance-review-owner` joins their versioned artifacts without hiding differences in population, definition, denominator, reporting lag, or data cutoff.

Use it to prepare reproducible surveillance evidence. It never diagnoses; classifies a person as a case; declares a signal, cluster, outbreak, epidemic, or emergency; issues an alert; starts contact tracing; changes a surveillance system; submits a report; contacts a person, institution, media, or public; or makes isolation, quarantine, testing, treatment, vaccination, resource, or other public-health decisions. Public-health authorities, epidemiologists, biostatisticians, surveillance informaticians and data stewards, laboratory/genomic specialists, privacy/legal staff, and communications leaders retain those responsibilities.

The unique package Skill is `public-health-surveillance/shared/method`. It is a bounded adaptation of the pinned K-Dense MIT pathogen-variant surveillance Skill plus clean-room general surveillance governance. Exact provenance, full license closure, retained/excluded concepts, and primary sources are under `skills/method/references/`.
