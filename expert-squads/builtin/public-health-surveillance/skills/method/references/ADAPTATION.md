# Bounded adaptation notice

The local Skill is a modified, non-verbatim adaptation under MIT plus clean-room general surveillance methodology.

## Retained concepts

- derive current-status statements only from date- and version-stamped sources rather than model memory;
- preserve reporting lag and collection/submission date distinctions;
- state numerator, denominator, filters, source-field definitions, and data version;
- distinguish sequence submissions from cases and population incidence;
- discover source fields from authoritative metadata instead of guessing a universal schema.

## Excluded concepts

- all network access, live GenSpectrum/LAPIS/Pathoplexus queries, scripts, endpoints, command examples, and dependencies;
- pathogen, lineage, clade, mutation, assay-target, nomenclature, correction, or current-circulation defaults;
- real-time detection, clinical interpretation, assay suitability, outbreak conclusion, public-health guidance, alerts, reports, or interventions.

## Local modifications

The method is rewritten for authorized local evidence across infectious, chronic, injury, exposure, mortality, syndromic, laboratory, genomic, and environmental surveillance. It adds case-definition and revision lineage, denominator governance, completeness/timeliness/representativeness evidence, general descriptive measures, multi-indicator joining, privacy constraints, three parallel roots, explicit join, and named public-health authority boundaries.
