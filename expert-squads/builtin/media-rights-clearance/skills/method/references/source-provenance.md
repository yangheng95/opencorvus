## Clean-room primary sources

- U.S. Copyright Office, Circular 16A, `How to Obtain Permission`: `https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ16a.pdf`. Bounded concepts: identify the exact work and intended use, research copyright status and owner evidence, and provide precise use details when permission is needed. It does not authorize this Skill to decide whether permission or a limitation applies.
- W3C Recommendation, ODRL Information Model 2.2, versioned 2018-02-15: `https://www.w3.org/TR/2018/REC-odrl-model-20180215/`. Bounded concepts: assets, parties, permissions, prohibitions, duties and constraints as separate traceable entities.
- IPTC RightsML 2.0 specification: `https://www.iptc.org/std/RightsML/2.0/RightsML_2.0-specification.html`. Bounded concepts: a media-oriented ODRL profile and explicit permissions, restrictions and duties. The standard is referenced, not copied into the package.
