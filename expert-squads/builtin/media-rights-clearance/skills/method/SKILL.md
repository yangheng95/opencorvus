# Media Rights Clearance Method

## Freeze production and intended use

1. Record production/title/campaign/catalog ID, exact cut and asset versions, languages, launch dates, platforms/media, audience, paid or organic context, territories, term/windows, edits/excerpts/promotion, distribution parties, evidence cutoff, confidentiality and accountable owners.
2. Assign stable asset, component, use, agreement, rule, evidence, obligation and decision IDs. Never reuse an ID across versions.
3. Keep source text, claimant or supplier assertion, analyst extraction, counsel interpretation and authorized decision separate.

## Inventory components and provenance

1. Decompose the frozen cut into footage, stills, artwork, text, scripts, performances, people, locations, trademarks, products, archives, user-generated content, composition, lyrics, recording, voice, font and other embedded material.
2. Record exact timecode/location, parent asset, creator or claimant assertion, acquisition route, document IDs, source/version/date and uncertainty in [the component inventory](assets/media-component-rights-inventory.md).
3. Trace creator/claimant → assignment/license/release/supplier delivery → production entity in [the chain-of-title ledger](assets/chain-of-title-and-release-ledger.md). Preserve breaks and conflicts.

## Normalize supplied terms

1. Read complete agreements, schedules and amendments. Extract atomic permission, prohibition, duty, condition, reservation, approval and ambiguity rows with exact page/section.
2. Normalize only for comparison: asset/party, action, platform/media, territory, language, term/window, audience, edition/cut, edit/adaptation, promotion, exclusivity, sublicensing, credit, fee/royalty, reporting, audit, notice, approval, renewal, revocation and survival.
3. Populate [the terms matrix](assets/license-release-terms-matrix.md) and [the exploitation calendar](assets/territory-window-edition-calendar.md). Do not resolve precedence, validity or enforceability.

## Compare uses and join evidence

1. Compare every use × component across all normalized dimensions. Keep composition and sound-recording rights separate; keep talent/likeness, privacy, trademark, artwork, location, archive and user-generated material as distinct review domains.
2. Record only evidence posture: supported for qualified review, conditional, conflicting, restricted by supplied text, or unverified. Absence is never permission or public-domain evidence.
3. Trace required credits, fees, royalties, reports, notices, approvals and expiry monitoring to owner and due date in [the decision register](assets/clearance-decision-obligation-expiry-register.md).

## Authority and stop boundary

Never decide fair use, public domain, ownership, infringement, consent, privacy/publicity, trademark, contract validity, enforceability, breach or legal clearance. Never contact, negotiate, sign, pay, edit, publish, upload, remove or issue a takedown. Stop on ambiguous cut/component/use, incomplete agreement chain, claimant conflict, missing release, unsafe personal data or expired review. Require qualified media counsel, business affairs, rights/licensing, privacy, production, finance, brand and accountable distribution-owner review.
