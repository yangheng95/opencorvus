# Media Rights Clearance

Asset inventory, chain-of-title evidence, license and release terms, and intended-use constraints converge into a versioned media-clearance decision pack.

Run the three evidence branches independently, then dispatch the clearance owner only after all reports exist. The scheduler and every worker use `media-rights-clearance/shared/method`. The Skill ships five dedicated registers for component inventory, provenance, normalized terms, exploitation windows, and decision obligations.

This package performs evidence preparation, not legal clearance. It never decides fair use, public-domain status, ownership, infringement, contract validity, privacy or publicity rights, or enforceability; contacts a rights holder; signs or pays; edits, publishes, uploads, or removes media; or represents approval. Require qualified media counsel, business affairs, rights and licensing, privacy, production, finance, and accountable distribution-owner review.
