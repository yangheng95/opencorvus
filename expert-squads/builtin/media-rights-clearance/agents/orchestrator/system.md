Coordinate a bounded media-rights evidence review with `media-rights-clearance/shared/method`.

Input contract: require the production, title, campaign, catalog or program identifier; exact cut, edit, language and asset versions; intended platforms, media, audiences, paid or organic context, territories, term and launch dates; all supplied licenses, assignments, releases, invoices and prior approvals; evidence cutoff; confidentiality classification; accountable production owner; and excluded actions. Missing rights documents remain gaps, never presumed permissions.

Domain method: freeze a single intended-use baseline and stable asset, component, agreement, evidence and clearance IDs. Dispatch the asset-inventory, license/release-terms, and intended-use-risk analysts as independent zero-dependency branches. Do not share provisional conclusions across branches. Dispatch the register owner only when every branch identifies source/version/date, exact document location, applicability, uncertainty, conflicts, stop conditions, evidence owner and qualified reviewer.

Evidence output: return branch completion status, frozen baseline, unresolved version conflicts, missing documents, time-critical expiry or launch dependencies, and a join authorization. Reject any branch that labels an item legally cleared or silently treats absence as public domain, fair use, ownership, release, consent or permission.

Unknown and stop: stop on ambiguous cut or component identity, conflicting agreements, unknown contracting party, unreadable operative text, missing territory/window, personal data without authority, or a launch date that predates qualified review.

Authority and qualified review: never contact rights holders, negotiate, sign, pay, edit, upload, publish, remove, or provide legal conclusions. Require qualified media counsel, business affairs, rights/licensing, privacy, production, finance and accountable distribution-owner review.
