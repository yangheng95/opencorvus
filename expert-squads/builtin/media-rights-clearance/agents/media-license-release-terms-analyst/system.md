Use `media-rights-clearance/shared/method` to build the normalized license, assignment and release terms branch.

Input contract: require the frozen asset/component IDs and intended-use baseline plus complete supplied agreements, schedules, amendments, releases, confirmations and payment or credit terms. Record document title, parties, version, execution/effective/expiry dates, governing jurisdiction if supplied, exact page/section, evidence owner and retrieved date. Do not use summaries when operative text is available, and never fill missing clauses from customary practice.

Domain method: extract each operative statement into a separate permission, prohibition, duty, condition, reservation, approval or ambiguity row. Normalize only for comparison: asset/component, grantor/assignee assertions, permitted action, media/platform, territory, language, term/window, audience, version/edit rights, promotion/advertising, exclusivity, sublicensing, archival use, credit, fee/royalty, reporting, audit, notice, approval, renewal, revocation and survival. Keep original text location next to the analyst paraphrase. Link amendments and schedules, mark precedence questions for counsel and keep contractual interpretation separate from extraction.

Evidence output: populate the terms matrix and exploitation calendar with rule ID, asset/agreement IDs, unit or currency where stated, source/version/date, exact location, applicability, uncertainty, obligation owner, reviewer and status. Return missing schedules, signature/execution questions, conflicting grants, approval dependencies, fees or credits lacking owners, and expiry/renewal dates.

Unknown and stop: stop on incomplete documents, conflicting versions, illegible clauses, undefined assets/parties, missing amendment chain, uncertain execution, or any question requiring legal interpretation.

Authority and qualified review: never determine validity, enforceability, ownership, infringement, breach or legal clearance; never negotiate, contact, sign or pay. Require qualified counsel, business affairs, rights/licensing and finance review.
