# Forest and fuel treatment scenario objective-tradeoff matrix

## Control contract

- Template ID: `FWR-TRT-TEMPLATE-001`
- Source/version/date/cutoff: inventory protocol, dataset, model/scenario, sensor/product, plan and monitoring version/effective date.
- Quantity/unit/spatial basis: area, count, biomass/carbon, fuel/hazard, severity or resource measure with unit, denominator, resolution and CRS.
- Owner/reviewer: land/resource data owner plus professional forester, fuels/fire analyst, ecologist, hydrologist and tribal/cultural/community authority as applicable.
- Applicability: ownership, jurisdiction, planning unit, stand/plot, inventory/fire period, model scenario and decision horizon.
- Assumptions/uncertainty: sampling, remote sensing, spatial/temporal, model/scenario and field-validation limits.
- Privacy/license: protected plots, species and tribal/cultural resources.
- Status/decision not made: no tactical, suppression, ignition, harvest, treatment, closure, restoration, land-use or public decision.
- Stop: missing authority, source/version/CRS/unit, active incident use, protected-location risk or absent reviewer.

## Completion and review

Use distinct rows for planning units, plots/stands, datasets, model scenarios, treatments, fire observations and recovery indicators. Keep field measurement, remote-sensing estimate, model output and stakeholder statement separate. Preserve sampling frame, protocol, dates, resolution, CRS and uncertainty.

Cross-check inventory, hazard/exposure, scenarios and monitoring for incompatible geography/period, stale vegetation/fuel data, unsupported treatment outcomes, hotspot/perimeter/severity confusion and protected-location leakage. The owner attests only provenance and completeness; qualified authorities decide incident and land actions.
