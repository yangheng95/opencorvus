# Selector

Select for a named planning unit and authority requiring forest inventory/condition/trend, fuel and wildfire-risk data, treatment scenario tradeoffs, burn-severity or post-fire resource-monitoring evidence.

Require ownership/authority, jurisdiction, planning unit/CRS, source versions, period/horizon, protected-location controls and qualified forestry/fire/ecology reviewers.

Do not select for live incident response, dispatch, evacuation, suppression, prescribed-fire ignition, harvest/treatment approval, drone operation or public warning.
