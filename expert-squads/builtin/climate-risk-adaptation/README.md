# Climate Risk Adaptation

Climate hazard/scenario evidence, exposure-vulnerability-consequence analysis, and adaptation options/pathways converge into a scenario-explicit pack for qualified human review.

Run the hazard/scenario, exposure/vulnerability/consequence, and adaptation/pathway branches concurrently. Dispatch the join owner only after all three versioned reports exist. The scheduler and every worker use climate-risk-adaptation/shared/method.

The method is a bounded MIT adaptation of climate-risk-assessment from mohitagw15856/pm-claude-skills at pinned commit 04fb3213980dbb711e422757efac509b26438358. The source path, full license, retained concepts, and exclusions are saved in the package. The adaptation retains physical/transition separation, hazard-exposure-vulnerability framing, explicit scenarios/horizons, ranges, confidence, gaps, options, and monitoring triggers. It excludes upstream default years/scenarios/locations, invented scores and losses, compliance claims, external acquisition, and professional decisions.

Never invent climate projections, return periods, damage functions, financial loss, disclosure compliance, community consent, or engineering adequacy. Never issue emergency, land-use, relocation, insurance, legal, actuarial, investment, procurement, or capital decisions. Require qualified climate science, engineering, actuarial, emergency, regulatory, finance, public/community, and accountable asset-owner review.
