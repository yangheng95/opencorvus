- Primary method cross-check: IPCC AR6 Working Group II, Climate Change 2022: Impacts, Adaptation and Vulnerability, https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg2/

Bounded concepts adapted: separate physical and transition risk; frame risk through hazard, exposure and vulnerability; state scenario and horizon; report ranges, confidence and gaps; structure adaptation options; record monitoring triggers.

Explicitly excluded: upstream default years, scenario labels, assumed locations, sample risk scores, invented revenue/financial ranges, automatic external data acquisition, current TCFD/ESRS/ISSB compliance claims, capital allocation, and engineering, actuarial, insurance, legal or emergency conclusions. Examples are not factual defaults. The local Skill and assets own the OpenCorvus execution contract.
