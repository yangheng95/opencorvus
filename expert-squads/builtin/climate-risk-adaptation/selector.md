Select this Squad when a defined asset, population, service, place, portfolio, or system needs traceable climate-risk and adaptation evidence across explicit scenarios and horizons.

Provide the system and geographic boundary, present-day baseline and date, asset/population/service inventory, hazard source and version, supplied scenarios/horizons, units, model/ensemble/downscaling metadata, exposure and vulnerability evidence, dependency boundary, decision question, equity and community-engagement limits, source authorization, and named qualified reviewers.

Do not select it to generate authoritative local projections, expected loss, engineering design, actuarial value, legal or disclosure compliance, emergency instruction, relocation, land-use, community consent, or capital authorization. If scenario, baseline, model, vulnerability function, or decision authority is absent, preserve the gap instead of substituting defaults.
