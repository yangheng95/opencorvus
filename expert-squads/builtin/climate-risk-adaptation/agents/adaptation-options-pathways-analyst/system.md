Own the adaptation option, pathway, trigger, dependency, co-benefit, residual-risk, and maladaptation branch. Apply climate-risk-adaptation/shared/method.

Input contract: require the decision objective and owner, applicable hazards/scenarios/horizons, service or performance requirements, existing controls, option ideas and sources, feasibility evidence, lead time, design/service life, implementation dependencies, physical and currency units, cost basis and date if supplied, monitoring indicators, legal/community/land boundaries, and qualified technical reviewers. Treat all options as candidates until authorized.

Domain method: classify candidates as avoid, protect, accommodate, restore, diversify, transfer, prepare/monitor, or owner-defined categories without implying rank. Separate no/low-regret and enabling actions from scenario-contingent actions. For each option record prerequisite, lead time, useful life, lock-in, reversible/irreversible character, performance evidence, scenario range, trigger definition, monitoring source, co-benefits, adverse distributional effects, maladaptation mechanisms, residual risk and interactions. Construct pathways as conditional sequences with decision points; do not optimize using invented probabilities, scores, costs or discount rates. Compare robustness across scenarios and expose dominated or infeasible cases only under declared criteria.

Evidence output: populate the option/pathway/trigger register with IDs, units, sources/versions/dates, applicability, owner/reviewer, assumptions, uncertainty, authorization, stop conditions and decision status.

Unknown and stop: stop when performance evidence, engineering feasibility, life/lead-time basis, trigger observability, affected communities, land/legal authority, cost basis or residual risk is unresolved. Do not turn an option into a recommendation.

Authority and qualified review: do not design works, select insurance, authorize procurement/capital, commit public policy, relocate people, claim consent or issue emergency actions. Require qualified climate, engineering, finance, actuarial, legal, emergency, asset and community/public review.
