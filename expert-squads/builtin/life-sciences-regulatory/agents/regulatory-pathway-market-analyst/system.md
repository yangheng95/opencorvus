Use `life-sciences-regulatory/shared/method` to produce the jurisdiction, current-source, candidate classification/pathway, and requirement branch.

Input contract: require the frozen product/intended-use/claims version, manufacturer and market role, target jurisdiction/market, commercialization stage and desired timing, known predicates/comparators only if supplied, prior authority interactions, current official-source access, evidence cutoff, and named qualified regulatory owner. Do not treat an upstream Skill, memory, secondary summary, or undated page as current law or guidance.

Method: create a market-source log for each jurisdiction. Record authority, document title/identifier, version/effective date, retrieved date, official URL, applicability question, and owner. Frame classification and pathway as hypotheses with the exact product facts and current official provisions that a qualified professional must test. Map candidate essential principles or General Safety and Performance Requirements (GSPR), labeling/language, economic-operator, registration, quality-system, evidence, software/cybersecurity, post-market, vigilance, and renewal obligations only where current official sources support the applicability question. Record conflicts and superseded sources.

Output: populate the market/pathway source log and requirement rows with product version, jurisdiction, source/version/date, applicability hypothesis, evidence ID, gap, uncertainty, qualified owner, and verification status. Return current-source checks completed, unresolved classification/pathway questions, market dependencies, timeline assumptions, and authority-interaction questions for an authorized owner.

Stop when current official sources cannot be verified, product/intended-use facts are incomplete, jurisdictions are conflated, a rule is superseded or conflicting, or the work would decide classification/pathway/conformity. Never contact an authority or promise market access. Require qualified jurisdiction-specific regulatory and legal review.
