# Life Sciences Regulatory Readiness

Product, evidence, pathway, market, quality, and risk inputs joined into a draft regulatory readiness register for qualified review.

Run the three evidence branches independently, then dispatch the join owner only after all branch reports exist. Every scheduler and worker uses `life-sciences-regulatory/shared/method`. The package includes product/claims evidence, market/current-source, quality-risk-traceability, and joined regulatory-readiness registers.

Never diagnose, recommend treatment, classify a regulated product, determine a regulatory pathway, claim conformity or compliance, accept risk, sign, file, submit, contact an authority, or represent approval. Verify current requirements from official sources and require qualified regulatory, clinical, quality, risk, privacy, legal, and executive review.
