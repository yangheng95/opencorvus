# SEO & Generative Engine Optimization method

Define the exact site, market, language, audience, conversion goal, competitors, source date, and accessible evidence. Search Engine Optimization (SEO) observations distinguish crawlability, indexability, relevance, authority, usability, and measurement. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) observations distinguish factual entity clarity, source quality, extractability, corroboration, and current model evidence. Never infer ranking from a single query or guarantee future indexing, citation, or mention. Every recommendation names evidence, owner, expected mechanism, verification method, and priority.
