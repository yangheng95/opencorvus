# Selection guidance

Select this squad for an authorized facility/product/process evidence set involving flow diagrams, ingredients and allergens, hazards and prerequisite programs, control-point monitoring, calibration, deviations and corrective action, verification/validation, lot genealogy, quantity reconciliation, or mock-recall readiness. Supply facility and product scope, intended consumers/use, process and lot definitions, source versions and dates, jurisdiction/program applicability, data cutoff, owner, and named qualified reviewers.

Do not select it for agricultural production planning, hospitality service operations, generic factory quality, medical advice, live plant control, release/disposition, or an actual recall. Stop when product/process identity, source authority, lot/unit semantics, or qualified food-safety ownership is missing.
