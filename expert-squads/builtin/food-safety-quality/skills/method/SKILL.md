# Food Safety Quality Method

## Freeze the food system boundary

1. Record facility/site, product and product-family identity, intended consumers and use, formulation/ingredient/allergen versions, packaging, storage, distribution and shelf-life conditions, process-flow version, rework and outsourced steps, lot/event definitions, unit-conversion sources, jurisdictions/programs, source inventory, data cutoff, owner, and qualified reviewers.
2. Separate supplied facts, observations, calculations, existing controlled decisions, and unresolved questions. Keep Good Hygiene Practices and prerequisite programs distinct from Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point (HACCP) plan controls.
3. State `decision_not_made`: no declaration of safety/compliance; significant-hazard, preventive-control, Critical Control Point (CCP), or critical-limit decision; process change; deviation closure; product release, hold, quarantine, destruction, or rework; recall; or external notification.
4. Do not hard-code temperatures, times, counts, thresholds, legal deadlines, or jurisdiction rules. Every requirement and scale must be supplied from an authorized current source with version, effective date, applicability, and owner.

## Map product, process, and hazard evidence

Walk each ingredient and process step, including receipt, storage, transfer, delay, preparation, transformation, packaging, rework, waste, outsourced steps, finished storage, and distribution interface. Record biological, chemical, physical, and allergen hazard hypotheses with source, cause/condition, population or product applicability, and existing prerequisite/control evidence.

Use only the qualified team's supplied severity and likelihood method. Preserve its scale source and do not create a score when an input or scale is absent. Keep hazard identification, significance determination, control selection, CCP determination, and critical-limit approval as different records owned by humans. A flow diagram is not verified merely because it exists; retain the on-site verification record and version.

## Trace monitoring, deviation, verification, and validation

For an already-authorized control, trace control-plan ID/version/effective date; monitored parameter; supplied limit source; what/how/frequency/who record; instrument/calibration evidence; observation and unit; deviation; immediate correction; affected-product control evidence; investigation and corrective action; implementation evidence; effectiveness check; verification; and scientific/technical validation evidence.

Keep monitoring, verification, and validation separate. Do not infer product impact, control adequacy, closure, or disposition. Check timestamps, lot/product/line identity, unit compatibility, expected frequency evidence, reviewer independence, and version alignment. Preserve missing records and contradictory results.

## Reconstruct genealogy and mock-recall readiness

Build stable one-step-back, internal transformation, and one-step-forward relations. Preserve lot splits, merges, commingling, rework, carryover, waste, yield, samples, inventory adjustments, unit conversions, finished lots, shipments, and authorized locations. Never infer a relation from time or location alone.

After unit reconciliation, calculate only within an owner-defined population:

- `accounted_quantity = output + rework + waste + samples + ending_inventory + other_authorized_categories`
- `mass_balance = accounted_quantity / input_quantity`

Record each category, input denominator, conversion source, rounding, uncertainty, and unexplained amount. A ratio is not proof of traceability or safety. For an authorized mock exercise, capture scenario version, target lots/population supplied by the recall coordinator, evidence-retrieval elapsed time, located and unmatched lots/shipments, coverage numerator/denominator, duplicate links, source latency, and privacy constraints. Do not initiate or simulate external communications.

## Join the branches

Require compatible facility, product/formulation, process-flow, lot, control-plan, source, date, jurisdiction, and unit scope or document mismatch. Cross-link hazard/control records to monitoring and validation evidence by stable IDs. Cross-link deviations and affected-lot records to genealogy and distribution. Keep actual and mock-recall records distinct. Retain conflicting quantities and source versions rather than choosing one.

Classify each branch and joined claim as evidence-complete, qualified-review-required, stopped, or superseded. Populate exactly the five assets in `assets/`. Every material row includes stable identity, source locator/version/date, cutoff/effective date, quantity with unit/denominator, owner, qualified reviewer, applicability, assumptions, uncertainty, privacy/license boundary, status, decision_not_made, and stop/escalation reason.

## Stop and escalate

Stop on unauthorized data, unknown facility/product/process/lot identity, unresolved allergen or rework paths, missing units, unverifiable source/control versions, absent qualified authority, unexplained genealogy, or requests for operational, disposition, recall, notification, medical, or legal decisions. Do not reproduce protected Codex standards or proprietary facility procedures; store only authorized locators and versions.

Route hazard and control decisions to the HACCP/food-safety team and Preventive Controls Qualified Individual where applicable; technical evidence to process engineers, microbiologists, toxicologists, allergen specialists and laboratories; product status to quality and disposition authority; disclosure to privacy/legal/regulatory staff; actual recall decisions and communication to the recall coordinator and accountable executive. Read `references/SOURCES.md` and `references/REJECTED-CANDIDATES.md`. This Skill is clean-room and retains no candidate text.
