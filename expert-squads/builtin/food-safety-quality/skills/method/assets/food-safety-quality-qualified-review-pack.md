## Joined claim table

For each claim record `claim_id`, branch row IDs, source locators/versions/dates, effective date/cutoff, product/process/lot/control-plan compatibility, value with unit and denominator, owner/reviewer, applicability, assumptions, uncertainty, status, decision_not_made, and escalation. Preserve incompatible formulations, flows, lots, units, plans, and jurisdictions. Do not generate a compliance score.

## Controlled human decisions

Human reviewers record their decision in controlled systems and link its locator, identity, role, reviewed artifact versions, date, disposition, conditions, and signature mechanism. A placeholder is not approval. Stop the join when a branch is absent, authority is unclear, or the request would change product, process, inventory, recall, or external communications.
