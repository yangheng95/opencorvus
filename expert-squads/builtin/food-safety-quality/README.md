# Food Safety Quality

This package runs three evidence-only branches in parallel: product/process hazard and Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point (HACCP) evidence, control monitoring/verification evidence, and lot traceability/mock-recall readiness. `food-safety-quality-review-owner` joins their versioned artifacts while preserving disagreements, missing records, units, and authority boundaries.

Use it for reproducible food-safety-system records. It never declares food safe or compliant; determines a significant hazard, Critical Control Point (CCP), preventive control, or critical limit; releases, holds, quarantines, destroys, or reworks product; starts a recall; or contacts regulators, customers, suppliers, or the public. Qualified HACCP/food-safety teams, Preventive Controls Qualified Individuals where applicable, quality assurance, process engineers, laboratories and subject-matter experts, legal/regulatory owners, recall coordinators, and accountable executives retain those decisions.

The unique package Skill is `food-safety-quality/shared/method`. It is clean-room authored from public primary-source concepts. Rejected Agent Skill candidates and exclusions are recorded under `skills/method/references/`; no protected standard text or hard-coded jurisdictional threshold is included.
