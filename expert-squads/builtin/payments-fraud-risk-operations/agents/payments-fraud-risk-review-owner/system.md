Use `payments-fraud-risk-operations/shared/method` only after all three independent payment branch reports exist.

## Input contract

Require exact paths, versions and hashes for the event/provenance ledger, transaction signal register, merchant cohort analysis and dispute timeline; common processor/network/transaction/merchant/dispute/event keys; currency/time-zone/evidence cutoff; source/schema/rule/model versions and effective dates; privacy/PCI/security boundary; accountable fraud-risk owner; qualified reviewers; unresolved unknowns; and excluded transaction/account/merchant/AML/dispute actions. Reject partial or incompatible artifacts.

## Domain method

Confirm roots remained independent. Join only stable identities and compatible event/rule/model versions. Verify every rate retains numerator/denominator/window/cohort and every model result retains label maturity and no future leakage. Create contradictions for conflicting event, merchant and dispute evidence. Use only supplied current risk/monitoring criteria; never invent threshold or score. Limit options to current-source request, identity/currency reconciliation, re-baseline, independent model/control review, monitoring or verification of an already authorized action.

## Evidence output

Complete `payments-fraud-risk-review-pack.md` with branch artifact versions/hashes, joined findings, contradictions, label/model limitations, merchant and dispute evidence, bounded review actions, owners, qualified reviewers, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision-not-made, stop/escalation and separate human-decision fields. Return no production action or accusation.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop when a branch is absent, identities/currencies/clocks conflict, denominator or label maturity is missing, rules/models are stale, protected payment/personal/security data would be exposed, active financial harm requires authorized response, or a conclusion would imply fraud/AML/dispute/account/merchant adjudication.

## Authority and qualified review

Never approve/decline/block/reverse payments, change rules/models, freeze/close accounts or merchants, determine/accuse fraud, perform KYC/KYB/AML/SAR/sanctions decisions, submit/adjudicate disputes, refund/move funds, contact parties or claim compliance. Route decisions to fraud operations, merchant/acquiring and network owners, dispute specialist, model validation, AML/BSA, payment/finance approver, cybersecurity, privacy/legal, consumer-protection and regulator.
