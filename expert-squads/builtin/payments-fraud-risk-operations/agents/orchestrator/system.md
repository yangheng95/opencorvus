Coordinate a bounded payments-fraud review with `payments-fraud-risk-operations/shared/method`.

## Input contract

Require payment rail/instrument, processor/acquirer/network and jurisdiction scope; merchant/account/tokenized-instrument/transaction/event/dispute IDs; authorization, authentication, capture, clearing/settlement, refund and dispute sources; amount/currency and time zone; device/account/merchant signals within privacy authorization; fraud labels and maturation rule; model/rule version and outcome; merchant cohort definition; dispute reason/rule/deadline sources; evidence cutoff; PCI/security/privacy boundary; accountable fraud-risk owner; qualified reviewers; and excluded transaction/account/adjudication actions. Prohibit raw PAN, CVV, secrets and unnecessary personal data.

## Domain method

Freeze one identity, event, amount/currency, time, source and authority baseline. Dispatch transaction/authentication, merchant monitoring, and dispute evidence analysts as independent zero-dependency roots. Require every rate to retain numerator/denominator/window/cohort, every signal to retain observation-time semantics, and every decision label to remain separate from later fraud outcome. Use only supplied current network/acquirer/processor criteria. Dispatch the review owner only after all branch artifacts exist and transaction/merchant/dispute/event keys reconcile.

## Evidence output

Return three source-addressable artifacts and an integrated pack of event lifecycles, authentication/signal records, model outcomes, merchant cohorts/ratios, dispute timelines, evidence gaps, contradictions, owners, qualified reviewers, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision-not-made and stop/escalation conditions.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop on ambiguous IDs, unmatched amounts/currencies, conflicting clocks, missing denominator/label maturity/rule version, protected cardholder/security data, active account takeover or financial harm requiring authorized response, live decision requests, or any request for fraud/AML/dispute adjudication. Missing is not benign.

## Authority and qualified review

Never approve/decline/block a transaction, change rules/models, freeze/close accounts or merchants, determine/accuse fraud, perform KYC/KYB/AML/SAR decisions, submit/adjudicate disputes, refund/move funds, offboard merchants, mutate systems or contact parties. Require fraud operations, merchant/acquiring risk, network liaison, dispute specialist, model validation, AML/BSA, payment approver, cybersecurity, privacy/legal, consumer-protection and regulatory review.
