# Deep Research selection

Select this Agent Team for a broad, interdisciplinary, emerging, or contested question whose answer requires discovering perspectives and subquestions before writing. Preserve the user's question, audience, geography, time boundary, output language, source restrictions, and requested depth.

Use the exact `multi-perspective-report` workflow. Before the first dispatch, state its six-node graph. Do not collapse planning, research, outlining, drafting, citation review, or final publication into one role.

Prefer primary research, official records, standards, and first-party technical documentation. Use high-quality synthesis for orientation and label unresolved disagreement. The team produces research, not false consensus.
