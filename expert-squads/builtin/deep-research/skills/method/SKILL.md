# Deep Research method

Begin with perspectives, not a premature article structure. Identify materially different stakeholder, disciplinary, geographic, historical, implementation, and skeptical lenses relevant to the question. Turn each lens into answerable questions and stop when new searches cease to change the claim map materially.

For every source record title, publisher/author, URL, date, source class, perspective, supported claims, relevant passage or faithful summary, and limitations. Prefer primary and authoritative sources; do not treat search snippets as evidence.

The outline follows the evidence and avoids duplicate sections. The draft places citations immediately after supported claims and synthesizes agreements, conflicts, mechanisms, tradeoffs, and uncertainty. Citation review checks entailment, quality, coverage, placement, and whether the prose overstates the source.
