# Primary method sources

Evidence cutoff: 2026-08-11. Recheck current status and operator-controlled protocol/SAP/charter for every task. Official sources anchor method only.

- [ICH E9 Statistical Principles for Clinical Trials](https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E9_Guideline.pdf) — statistical planning, prespecification and qualified-statistician context.
- [ICH E9(R1) Estimands and Sensitivity Analysis](https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E9-R1_Step4_Guideline_2019_1203.pdf) — estimand attributes, intercurrent events and sensitivity-analysis framework.
- [ICH E6(R3) Annex 2 Step 4](https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/ICH_E6%28R3%29_Annex%202_Guideline_Step%204_2026_0603_0.pdf) — current GCP/data-governance source identity.
- [FDA Establishment and Operation of Clinical Trial DMCs](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/establishment-and-operation-clinical-trial-data-monitoring-committees) — final DMC role and operation guidance.
- [FDA Multiple Endpoints in Clinical Trials](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/multiple-endpoints-clinical-trials) — final multiplicity guidance.
- [FDA Adjusting for Covariates](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/adjusting-covariates-randomized-clinical-trials-drugs-and-biological-products) — final prespecified covariate-adjustment guidance.
- [CDISC ADaM Traceability Examples](https://www.cdisc.org/standards/foundational/adam/adam-examples-traceability-v1-0) and [CDISC SDTM](https://www.cdisc.org/standards/foundational/sdtm) — analysis/source traceability and tabulation model sources.

ICH E20 Adaptive Designs remains Step 2 draft at the cutoff; label it draft and never treat it as an approved stopping/adaptation rule.
