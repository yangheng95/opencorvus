# Clinical Biostatistics and Data Monitoring

Estimand, Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP), analysis-population, source/Study Data Tabulation Model (SDTM)/Analysis Data Model (ADaM) traceability, model, missing-data, multiplicity, sensitivity, interim and monitoring evidence without unblinding or stop/continue authority.

The scheduler dispatches four independent professional branches, then one explicit all-roots join preserves conflicts and produces a qualified-review pack. Every scheduler and worker projects only `clinical-biostatistics-data-monitoring/shared/method`.

- Do not choose unapproved endpoints, estimands, methods, alpha, margins, sample sizes or stopping boundaries; do not randomize, unblind, alter or lock data.
- Do not execute unauthorized interim analysis, recommend stop/continue/adapt, sign an analysis, certify results or make clinical/regulatory decisions.
- Qualified trial statisticians, statistical programmers/data managers, independent DMC statisticians, medical monitors, sponsors, ethics/privacy and regulatory owners retain decisions.

The method is a bounded MIT adaptation. Exact upstream paths, license, exclusions and current primary sources are saved under `skills/method/references/`; the five assets are evidence structures, not completed decisions.
