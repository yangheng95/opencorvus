# Selection guidance

Select for estimand and SAP trace, analysis populations, source-to-SDTM-to-ADaM derivations, statistical model specifications, missing-data or multiplicity controls, sensitivity analyses, interim evidence, blinding boundaries or Data Monitoring Committee packet readiness.

Do not select to choose unapproved methods or thresholds, randomize or unblind, modify or lock data, sign results, recommend stop/continue/adapt or make a clinical/regulatory decision. Use only for source-bound evidence preparation before qualified review.
