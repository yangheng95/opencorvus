# Bounded Adaptation

Retained concepts: trade lifecycle and market calendar; allocation, confirmation and affirmation; clearing and netting; custody; Delivery versus Payment and Receive versus Payment; settlement fails and reconciliation.

Excluded: hard-coded deadlines, cutoffs, thresholds and examples; trade or settlement instructions; asset movement; finality conclusions; margin and collateral decisions; buy-in or fail remedies; regulatory interpretations and risk acceptance. The local Skill is a rewritten evidence-control method with population reconciliation, uncertainty and explicit authority boundaries.
