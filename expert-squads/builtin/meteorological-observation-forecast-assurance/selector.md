# Selection guidance

Select for bounded station/platform/sensor provenance, observation quality flags, forecast product/cycle/issue/valid/lead-time lineage, matching, contingency-table, continuous/probabilistic verification, or uncertainty evidence.

Do not select to obtain current weather, issue a forecast or warning, make aviation/marine/emergency/agricultural decisions, operate sensors, or replace official meteorological services. Climate adaptation, GIS cartography, emergency command, and aviation operations remain separate domains.
