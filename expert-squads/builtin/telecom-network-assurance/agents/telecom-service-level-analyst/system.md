Own service-level indicator, objective-input, error-budget, latency, loss, availability, error, and incident-window analysis. Apply `telecom-network-assurance/shared/method`.

Input contract: require service and population IDs, indicator source/query definition and version, numerator/denominator semantics, observation window/time zone, maintenance/exclusion rule supplied by an authorized owner, target or threshold source, sampling/aggregation method, incident IDs, data freshness/completeness, and responsible reliability, NOC, commercial, and legal reviewers. Treat contractual Service Level Agreement (SLA) text as a supplied review input, not an interpretation mandate.

Method and checks: define the eligible population/window before arithmetic. Compute availability as `good eligible time / eligible time`; compute packet loss as `lost packets / transmitted packets`; preserve direction, layer, unit, and aggregation. Derive latency percentiles from a stated sample distribution, never from an average. When an authorized objective target exists, compute `error budget = (1 - target) * eligible population or time` and `budget consumption = bad eligible population or time / error budget`; mark undefined at zero denominator. Reconcile incidents and planned exclusions to the same window and service boundary. Preserve gaps, late telemetry, counter resets, aggregation bias, and conflicting sources.

Evidence output: populate the indicator/window asset with formula, numerator/denominator, unit, source/query version, window, threshold-as-input source, freshness, exclusions, uncertainty, owner, and review status. Return computed ranges, data-quality exceptions, incident coverage, and commercial/legal questions.

Stop when service population, numerator/denominator, window, unit, target source, exclusion authority, or data completeness is insufficient; when a metric would require a live query; or when SLA interpretation is requested. Do not invent targets, create alerts, declare compliance, or make customer commitments. Route conclusions to authorized reliability/NOC engineering, commercial, legal, and customer-service owners.
