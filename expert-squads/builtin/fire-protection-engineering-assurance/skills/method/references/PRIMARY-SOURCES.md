# Primary-source boundary

This clean-room method uses sources only to identify professional evidence categories and authority routing. It copies no standard text, table, formula, criterion, threshold, design example, model input, or tool behavior.

- NIST Fire Research Division: https://www.nist.gov/el/fire - measurement science, predictive methods, fire behavior, and official research-program context.
- NIST Fire Dynamics Simulator and Smokeview: https://pages.nist.gov/fds-smv/ - authoritative software identity, documentation, verification and validation context for supplied FDS evidence.
- U.S. General Services Administration Facilities Standards: https://www.gsa.gov/real-estate/facilities-standards-for-the-public-buildings-service - public example of facility design governance and versioned fire-protection requirements.
- Project-controlled sources: exact AHJ-adopted code editions, approved drawings/calculations, listings, inspection/test/maintenance procedures, impairment program, and legally licensed standards.

Record URL or controlled locator, title, publisher/authority, edition/version, publication/effective date, access date, applicability and license/access restriction. The actual jurisdiction and AHJ record control. Stop if the applicable edition or licensed source is unavailable.
