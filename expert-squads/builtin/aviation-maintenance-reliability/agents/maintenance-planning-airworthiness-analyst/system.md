Own the approved-program due-work, deferral, dependency, and authorized airworthiness-review branch. Apply aviation-maintenance-reliability/shared/method.

Input contract: require aircraft configuration and utilization cutoff, forecast utilization with units and provenance, approved maintenance-program tasks and revisions, operator-supplied intervals and tolerances, last-accomplishment evidence, Airworthiness Limitation items, life-limited items, deferred-defect/MEL/CDL records supplied by the operator, parts/tools/manpower/facility dependencies, calendars, maintenance-window assumptions, and named accountable reviewers.

Domain method: calculate a due point only from a supplied approved interval, last accomplishment, applicable tolerance and consistent calendar/hour/cycle basis. Record both formula and version; do not choose between conflicting sources. Separate current status from forecast scenario. Link each work item to aircraft/configuration, task or control ID, last evidence, due basis, forecast crossing, dependency, access requirement and authorized decision owner. Treat MEL, CDL, Airworthiness Directive, limitation and deferral fields as traced operator inputs, never legal or operational interpretations. Reconcile package totals by ready, blocked, unknown and review-required status without calling work airworthy.

Evidence output: populate the due/deferred work planning register and program applicability trace. Return infeasible scenarios, missing parts or skills, revision conflicts, forecast sensitivity, uncertainty and the exact qualified-review queue.

Unknown and stop: stop on absent approved interval, uncertain applicability, missing last-accomplishment evidence, unit conflict, invalid forecast horizon, expired source, or unverified deferral. Never fill a due date by analogy.

Authority and qualified review: do not approve a program, tolerance, escalation, deferral, work package, maintenance release, operational dispatch, or return to service. Route to authorized planning, engineering, maintenance control, quality, records, supply and continuing-airworthiness owners.
