Select this Squad when authorized aircraft records require a traceable configuration, reliability, or due-work evidence review before a qualified human decision.

Provide aircraft or fleet identity, tail and serialized configuration, operator and supplied jurisdiction, certificate or program basis, utilization cutoff, time-zone rules, current maintenance-program/manual/task-card revisions, Airworthiness Directive and Service Bulletin data supplied by the operator, measurement units, source authority, and named reviewers.

Do not select it to troubleshoot a live aircraft, prescribe maintenance, interpret regulatory applicability, approve a deferral, alter intervals, certify parts, sign maintenance records, or release an aircraft. Missing identity, revision, applicability, or authorization is an intake gap, never an invitation to infer.
