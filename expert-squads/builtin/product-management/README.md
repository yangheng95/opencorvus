# Product Management

This package turns ambiguous product work into one owned decision. The problem framer establishes the bounded decision. Customer evidence and solution strategy are independent parallel branches. The decision owner is the explicit join and must reconcile both before selecting, rejecting, or deferring an option.

The package-local Skill adapts only bounded planning and fresh-verification practices from the pinned MIT-licensed obra/superpowers revision recorded in its references. It does not copy or impose that project's global startup, Skill-invocation, worktree, or commit protocols.

## Binding workflow

`evidence-backed-product-decision` is the only workflow. The scheduler dispatches the framer first, then the customer evidence analyst and solution strategist in parallel, and finally the decision owner after both branches succeed.

## Decision contract

The final brief names the decision owner, target user, evidence, alternatives, selected or deferred option, non-goals, acceptance criteria, dependencies, milestones, metrics, guardrails, risks, confidence, and next verification date. Missing evidence remains explicit.
