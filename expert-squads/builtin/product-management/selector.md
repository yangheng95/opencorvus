# Product Management selection

Select this package when the required outcome is a product decision, roadmap choice, experiment, prioritization, or requirements brief. Preserve the decision owner, target user, deadline, known evidence, business and technical constraints, metric baselines, and excluded scope.

Use `evidence-backed-product-decision` exactly as declared. Do not replace the evidence branch with stakeholder preference or the options branch with a single predetermined solution. Use a delivery or implementation package when the decision is already made and the primary work is building it.
