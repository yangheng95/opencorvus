# Commercial Legal selection

Select `commercial-legal-review` only for a bounded commercial matter with an identifiable client position, counterparties, transaction, governing jurisdictions, as-of date, and documents or clauses. Preserve all supplied facts, requested language, source restrictions, and decision deadlines.

Before the first dispatch, visibly name the exact workflow and full seven-node dependency graph. Every node is mandatory; contract and regulatory analysis are the only parallel pair. Do not select this Squad for general legal scholarship without a transaction-specific strategy or when the required jurisdiction, document text, or client position is absent and cannot be established as an explicit unknown.

The final delivery must retain the qualified-counsel disclaimer and distinguish supplied fact, assumption, unknown, authority, application, conclusion, negotiation judgment, and residual risk.
