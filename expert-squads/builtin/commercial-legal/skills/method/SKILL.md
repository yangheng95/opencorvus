# Commercial Legal method

Fix the client position, counterparties, governing jurisdictions, transaction, document set, decision context, and as-of date before research. Separate supplied facts, assumptions, unknowns, legal rules, applications, conclusions, and negotiation judgments.

Prefer official legislation, regulator publications, judicial interpretations, court or tribunal material where authoritative, and official treaty or registry material. Record issuer, authority level, legal status, jurisdiction, effective date, source Uniform Resource Locator (URL), supported proposition, and limitation. Search snippets and undated summaries are leads rather than authority.

Analyze each material clause with Issue, Rule, Application, and Conclusion (IRAC) reasoning. Cover obligations, conditions, payment, warranties, intellectual property, confidentiality, data, liability, indemnity, termination, remedies, governing law, dispute resolution, approvals, and regulatory applicability only to the extent supported by the matter facts. A domain that is not applicable still receives an explicit evidence-based applicability determination.

Every recommendation names the affected clause or risk, legal basis, proposed language or action, negotiation rationale, and residual exposure. Never invent contract text, facts, authority, dates, outcomes, or legal certainty. The finished work is legal research and risk analysis, not a substitute for advice from qualified counsel in the relevant jurisdiction.
