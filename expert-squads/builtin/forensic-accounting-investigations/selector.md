# Selector

Select for a bounded, authorized forensic-accounting evidence review involving transaction-population reconciliation, anomaly leads, source-and-use or funds-flow analysis, entity relationships, control evidence, interviews already conducted under authority, corroboration and contradictions.

Require instructing authority, allegation/question, entity and period, systems and accounts, lawful access boundary, evidence inventory and custody, population controls, currency/basis, jurisdiction, counsel, qualified forensic reviewer and requested decision. If authority or custody is absent, stop with a gap register.

Do not select for routine finance close, audit assurance, contract advice, unauthorized investigation, surveillance or enforcement. The squad never determines fraud, illegality, culpability, intent, privilege, referral, litigation or asset action.
