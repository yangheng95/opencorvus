# Allegation, hypothesis, corroboration and contradiction matrix

## Control header

- Template ID: `FAI-HYP-TEMPLATE-001`
- Purpose: Keep allegations, competing hypotheses, support and contradictions explicitly separated.
- Source/version/date: immutable evidence locator, source-system or document version, acquisition/effective date, time zone and analysis cutoff are mandatory.
- Quantity/unit: record count, amount, currency, conversion, denominator and accounting basis where applicable.
- Owner: authorized investigation evidence owner.
- Qualified reviewer: instructing counsel and named CFE/CPA or authorized investigator.
- Applicability: entity, jurisdiction, engagement, allegation/workstream, period and evidence population.
- Assumptions: state only sourced assumptions; never assume intent, ownership, privilege or illegality.
- Uncertainty: state custody, completeness, reconciliation, matching, false-positive, translation and interpretation limits.
- Privacy/license boundary: classification, access limit and third-party terms.
- Status: draft, evidence-present, contradicted, unresolved, or qualified-review-complete.
- Decision not made: no fraud, illegality, culpability, intent, suspect, privilege, loss, SAR, referral, litigation or audit-opinion decision.
- Stop/escalation: missing authority, custody, population control, evidence, permission or qualified reviewer stops analysis.

## Completion procedure

Create one row per evidence item, reproducible analytic result, flow link, proposition or human-decision item. Never merge separate entities, currencies, periods or evidence classes. Link all derived results to custody-controlled inputs and retain exact test/filter versions. Reconcile counts and amounts to authorized controls before interpreting exceptions.

Label every material statement fact, observation, inference or hypothesis. Record corroboration and contradiction separately and keep plausible alternative explanations visible. An anomaly remains a lead. A shared name, address, account attribute or timing pattern is not sufficient to establish identity, control, intent or misconduct.

## Review questions

1. Is engagement and source access explicitly authorized?
2. Is original evidence preserved and every derived artifact linked through custody?
3. Is the transaction population reproducible and reconciled?
4. Are currency, conversion, debit/credit and amount categories explicit?
5. Are false-positive limits, contradictory evidence and alternative explanations recorded?
6. Are personal data, privilege markings and confidential sources handled only as instructed?
7. Is every prohibited accusation or external action reserved to the named human authority?

## Controlled closeout

The asset owner may attest only to provenance and record completeness. Counsel and qualified forensic professionals determine further collection, interview, disclosure, referral and reporting. Never alter evidence, contact an external party, reveal credentials, paste restricted personal data or act on an account. Preserve superseded analysis and corrections rather than overwriting history.
