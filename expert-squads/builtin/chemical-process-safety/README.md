# Chemical Process Safety

This squad prepares a bounded chemical-process-safety evidence pack for a named facility, unit, process boundary and review date. Four independent roots examine process safety information; process hazard analysis and supplied Layer of Protection Analysis assumptions; mechanical integrity, Management of Change and Pre-Startup Safety Review readiness; and incident-to-barrier learning. One explicit join preserves disagreement and routes decisions to qualified authorities.

It is separate from manufacturing quality: product defects and nonconformance remain with Manufacturing Quality, while this package addresses catastrophic toxic, reactive, flammable or explosive release scenarios and their prevention barriers. It does not set operating limits, design relief or Safety Instrumented Systems, credit an Independent Protection Layer, accept risk, approve a change or startup, issue an emergency instruction, or certify legal compliance.
