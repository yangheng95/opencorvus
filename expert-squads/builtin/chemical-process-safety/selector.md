# Selection guidance

Select for source-bound reviews of process safety information, Process Hazard Analysis, Hazard and Operability Study, supplied Layer of Protection Analysis, safeguards, mechanical integrity, Management of Change, Pre-Startup Safety Review, incident barriers and recommendation closure.

Require facility/unit/process/node boundary, chemicals and inventories, operating modes, process and instrumentation diagram versions, data cutoff, units, decision question, applicable jurisdiction and named process-safety reviewers. Missing boundaries or incompatible diagram and equipment revisions are stop conditions.

Do not select for ordinary manufacturing defects, occupational-safety checklists without catastrophic-process scope, live emergency command, equipment operation, engineering design, regulatory certification or startup approval.
