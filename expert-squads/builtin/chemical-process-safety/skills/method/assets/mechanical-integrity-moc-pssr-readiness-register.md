This register records evidence but never calculates remaining life, changes an inspection interval, decides fitness for service, treats a change as replacement in kind, closes an MOC or authorizes startup. An administrative signature without the referenced technical evidence remains a gap. Decisions require authorized integrity/inspection/materials/process/control/operations/training/EHS and startup authorities under the current procedure.
