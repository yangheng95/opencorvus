Keep observations separate from causal hypotheses and authorized findings. Ticket closure, document revision or completion percentage does not prove risk reduction; require effectiveness or revalidation evidence. This register cannot assign blame, declare root cause, close a PHA/MOC action, accept risk, issue emergency instructions or change a live process. Investigation leadership, barrier owners, process safety/engineering/operations/EHS, site authority and legal/privacy reviewers decide.
