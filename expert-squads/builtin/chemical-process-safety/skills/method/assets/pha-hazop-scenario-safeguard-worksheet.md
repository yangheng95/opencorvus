Keep prevention and mitigation distinct, and retain every scenario even when another row has the same consequence. A listed alarm, procedure, relief device, SIS or response action is evidence of a claimed safeguard, not proof of availability or effectiveness. This worksheet cannot select the PHA method, approve causes/consequences, close recommendations, change operations or certify compliance. Qualified facilitators and process safety, process, operations, control, relief, human-factor, siting and EHS reviewers decide.
