Use only authorized numeric inputs and retain every factor, exponent, unit and source. Do not derive a missing initiating frequency from generic industry data, treat a HAZOP safeguard as an IPL, assign probability of failure on demand, select a risk criterion, or conclude that calculated frequency is acceptable. Missing independence or current testing prevents displayed credit. Only the approved LOPA team and site risk authority may decide credit, SIL allocation and tolerability.
