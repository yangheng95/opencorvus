# Authority and source currency

This package is an OpenCorvus clean-room method authored from public-service analysis concepts; it copies no third-party Skill and depends on no external runtime.

For every statutory, policy, standard, accessibility, records, privacy, equality, finance, or service-rule input, record the issuing authority, document title/identifier, jurisdiction, version or effective date, retrieved/verified date, exact official URL or supplied location, applicability question, and named authorized interpretation owner. A source without a verifiable operative version remains `current-source check required`. Never substitute model memory, a secondary summary, or this Skill for a current official source.

Keep official text, authorized legal/policy interpretation, operational hypothesis, recommendation, and public commitment separate. Only a named jurisdiction-appropriate policy or legal owner may interpret law or policy; only the accountable public authority may decide eligibility, entitlement, policy, budget, record changes, or public commitments. Accessibility/equality, privacy/data-protection, records, finance, service-design, operational, communications, and accountable-officer reviews remain explicit human gates.

Recheck source currency at the evidence cutoff and before any operational or public use. Record superseded, conflicting, unavailable, or inaccessible sources as gaps; never silently choose between them.
