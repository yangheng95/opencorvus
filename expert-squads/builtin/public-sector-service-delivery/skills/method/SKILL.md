# Public Sector Service Delivery Method

## Establish the service and authority boundary

1. Record the service name and version, responsible authority, jurisdiction, resident populations, channels, lifecycle stages, observation period, evidence cutoff, data classification, excluded actions, and named decision owner in [the boundary brief](assets/service-boundary-and-authority-brief.md).
2. Register every supplied statutory or policy input with issuer, version/effective date, retrieved date, exact location, and authorized interpretation owner. Treat it as an input, not permission to interpret law. Mark unverified current rules `current-source check required`.
3. Assign stable evidence IDs. Distinguish direct resident evidence, administrative data, staff interpretation, supplied policy interpretation, calculation, design hypothesis, conflict, and unknown.

## Analyze resident journeys and channel equity

1. Map declared segments across discover, understand, request/apply, provide evidence, wait, decide, receive, challenge, and follow-up stages where applicable.
2. Record the resident goal, channel, handoff, evidence request, observed barrier, language/accessibility or assisted-digital need, failure demand, and outcome for each stage in [the journey register](assets/resident-journey-and-channel-equity-register.md).
3. State population, sample, collection method, period, denominator, and bias limits. Do not infer protected characteristics or needs from proxies. Suppress or escalate unsafe small cells.

## Reconcile service flow and performance

1. Version the process map and define demand, arrival, completion, withdrawal, rework, first-time completion, elapsed time, touch time, backlog, and cost before comparing values.
2. For one cohort and period, test `opening backlog + arrivals - completions - valid withdrawals = closing backlog`. Record the unexplained residual rather than forcing a balance.
3. Separate new demand from failure demand and total elapsed time from active handling. State units, population, period, denominator, source system, extraction date, and owner for every measure.
4. Trace stated outcomes to outputs, measures, baselines, targets supplied by the authority, access impacts, dependencies, and accountable owners in [the outcome register](assets/service-delivery-outcome-register.md).

## Evaluate options and delivery risk

1. Compare only declared options and criteria. Show resident, distributional, accessibility, privacy, records, workforce, cost, feasibility, supplier, technology, and delivery effects.
2. Keep observed fact, authorized policy interpretation, operational hypothesis, recommendation, and public commitment in separate fields.
3. Record each risk with declared scale, source, trigger, leading indicator, mitigation owner, contingency, residual uncertainty, review date, and decision gate. Preserve branch disagreement.

## Join and hand off

Require all three independent branches. Reject incompatible service versions, populations, or time bases. Produce all three assets with artifact version, source/version/date, applicability, evidence ID, responsible owner, uncertainty, reviewer, and approval status. Retain missing fields as visible gaps and list current-source checks and reversible next steps.

## Authority boundary

Never interpret law, determine eligibility or entitlement, make a public commitment, approve policy or budget, claim accessibility or regulatory compliance, identify a resident unnecessarily, or mutate public records. Stop on uncertain operative versions, incompatible cohorts, unsafe personal data, absent accessibility evidence for a material channel, or unresolved legal authority. Require authorized policy, legal, accessibility, equality, privacy, records, finance, service-design, operational, and accountable-service-owner review.

## Assets

- Start every review with [Service Boundary and Authority Brief](assets/service-boundary-and-authority-brief.md).
- Capture segment/stage evidence in [Resident Journey and Channel Equity Register](assets/resident-journey-and-channel-equity-register.md).
- Join measures, outcomes, options, risks, and decisions in [Public Service Delivery Outcome Register](assets/service-delivery-outcome-register.md).
