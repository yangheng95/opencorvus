# Public Sector Service Delivery

Resident needs, process and accessibility evidence, and policy-delivery risks joined into an accountable public-service outcome register.

Run the three evidence branches independently, then dispatch the join owner only after all branch reports exist. Every scheduler and worker uses `public-sector-service-delivery/shared/method`. The Skill ships a service boundary/authority brief, a resident-journey and channel-equity register, and a service-delivery outcome register; all three retain source version/date, applicability, accountable owner, and uncertainty.

Never interpret law, determine eligibility or entitlement, make a public commitment, approve policy or budget, claim accessibility or regulatory compliance, or mutate public records. Protect resident data and require authorized policy, legal, accessibility, privacy, finance, and service-owner review.
