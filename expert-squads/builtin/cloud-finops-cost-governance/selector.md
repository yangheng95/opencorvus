# Selection guidance

Select this Squad for source-grounded cloud, software-as-a-service, artificial-intelligence, platform, data and adjacent technology spend evidence: cost/usage/billing ingestion, invoice reconciliation, allocation, unit economics, budget/forecast comparison, commitment and optimization candidates, anomalies, governance ownership and value linkage.

Provide the organization and technology scope, provider/accounts/subscriptions, billing and service periods, source exports with schema versions, invoice and contract references, currencies and exchange-rate sources, allocation policy, business-unit/product/workload identity, approved metric definitions, forecast horizon, existing commitments, evidence cutoff, data-class boundary, owner and qualified FinOps, engineering, finance, procurement and accounting reviewers.

Do not select it for cloud architecture design, general ledger close, tax treatment or incident mitigation. Never use it to purchase/exchange commitments, resize/stop/delete resources, deploy policy, enforce tags, create budgets, post entries, submit vendor requests, contact providers, approve savings or claim realized value without reconciled evidence.
