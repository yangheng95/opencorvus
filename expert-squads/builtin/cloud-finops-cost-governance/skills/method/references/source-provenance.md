## Modification notice

Modified by the OpenCorvus project on 2026-08-11 for a package-local, read-only evidence method. The local `cloud-finops-cost-governance-method` is a derivative and remains under CC BY-SA 4.0. It was rewritten into a provider-neutral evidence workflow with stable identity, provenance, reconciliation, uncertainty, explicit parallel branches, a join record and qualified-human decision gates.

Retained concepts are visibility and data quality before optimization, normalized cost/usage evidence, allocation, cost-to-value linkage, unit economics, commitment evidence, anomaly evidence, progressive review, and iterative governance. These concepts were adapted into the local method rather than copied as vendor-specific playbooks.

Excluded content includes vendor price tables, dated products and mechanics, fixed maturity percentages, universal targets, commitment coverage thresholds, purchase or negotiation instructions, scripts, Model Context Protocol integrations, provider commands, automated optimization, self-executing policy, tax/accounting assertions, and claims that a single benchmark applies to every organization. No upstream reference or playbook file was copied.
