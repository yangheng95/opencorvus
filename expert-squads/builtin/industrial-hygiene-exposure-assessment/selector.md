# Selection guidance

Select this package when the requested result is a source-grounded workplace exposure-assessment evidence pack covering agents, tasks, shifts, exposure routes, similar exposure groups, current occupational exposure-limit sources, sampling and analytical QA, compatible time-weighted calculations, controls, respiratory-program evidence, uncertainty, and qualified review.

Do not select it for process-hazard analysis, clinical or toxicological diagnosis, laboratory accreditation, product nonconformance, environmental-public exposure, site entry, live measurements, instrument operation, worker assignment, PPE or respirator selection, emergency response, overexposure/compliance determination, regulatory submission, or employee-health communication. Use current site-, agent-, method-, jurisdiction-, and program-specific controlled sources; absence of evidence is an unknown, never a safe or compliant result.
