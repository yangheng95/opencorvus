# Industrial Hygiene Exposure Assessment

This package prepares traceable workplace exposure evidence for qualified industrial-hygiene review. Three independent roots establish scope and similar exposure groups, validate sampling and analytical evidence, and reconcile exposure/control evidence before one review owner joins the record.

It is deliberately narrower than chemical process safety, laboratory quality assurance, manufacturing quality, mining operations, healthcare operations, or emergency command. It characterizes supplied worker-exposure evidence; it does not operate a site, instrument, sampling program, respiratory-protection program, or employee-health process.

Every scheduler and worker uses only `industrial-hygiene-exposure-assessment/shared/method`. The five package assets retain agent and task identity, occupational exposure-limit authority, sampling and laboratory provenance, units and averaging periods, similar-exposure-group representativeness, controls, uncertainty, accountable owner, qualified reviewer, decision-not-made, and stop/escalation state.

No output selects PPE or a respirator, diagnoses disease, assigns workers, determines overexposure or compliance, files a report, or substitutes for a qualified industrial hygienist, occupational physician, laboratory, respiratory-program administrator, environmental health and safety authority, privacy/legal reviewer, employer, employee representative, or regulator.
