Use `industrial-hygiene-exposure-assessment/shared/method` only after all three independent branches have completed.

## Input contract

Require exact versions/hashes of the scope/SEG register, sampling/analytical ledger, OEL applicability register and control evidence map; common agent/task/SEG/sample/time/unit keys; evidence cutoff; source-authority versions; all contradictions, censored or invalid measurements, coverage gaps and unresolved applicability; accountable owner; qualified industrial-hygiene, laboratory, occupational-medicine, respiratory-program, EHS, privacy/legal and employment reviewers; and decisions excluded from the review.

## Domain method

Join by stable keys, never by similar labels. Reconcile scope and SEG representation against actual sample links; reconcile sample timing/units/method QA against every exposure calculation; reconcile OEL agent/form/fraction/route/averaging period; and reconcile control claims against versioned operating/verification evidence. Preserve competing OELs and contradictions. Classify each statement as observed fact, supplied criterion, calculation, assumption, uncertainty, unknown or proposed evidence-gathering step. Do not turn numerical comparison into compliance or health judgment.

## Evidence output

Complete `industrial-hygiene-exposure-assessment-qualified-review-pack.md`. Include branch artifact hashes, joined keys, scope and coverage, source table, calculation table with operands, contradiction and uncertainty register, control-evidence status, reviewer matrix, open evidence requests, decision-not-made fields and explicit stop/escalation. Every claim must point to a frozen asset row and current source locator/version/date.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop when branch keys or versions disagree, sample coverage cannot represent the SEG/task, method validity is unresolved, values/units/periods are non-comparable, current OEL authority is absent, personal/medical evidence exceeds authorization, active exposure or illness needs immediate human response, or the requested output implies safe work, compliance, PPE, medical, employment or reporting action. Do not average contradictions away.

## Authority and qualified review

Never sign an exposure assessment, approve a SEG or sampling strategy, select an OEL/PPE/respirator/control, diagnose or determine causation, restrict workers, declare overexposure/compliance, submit reports or contact affected people. The final artifact is evidence for qualified human review. Record the industrial hygienist and each other accountable professional decision owner; leave every approval and conclusion field unfilled.
