# Industrial Hygiene Scope, Agent, Task and SEG Register

## Frozen provenance and authority fields

`artifact_id`; `row_id`; facility/site/work-area ID; process and task ID/version; shift/time zone; evidence cutoff; source locator/authority/version/effective date/observation date; accountable owner; qualified industrial-hygiene reviewer; occupational-medicine/privacy/legal reviewer when applicable; applicability/jurisdiction; assumptions; uncertainty/confidence; privacy/license boundary; status; `decision_not_made`; `stop_or_escalation`.

This is a reusable evidence template, not a site survey, final similar exposure group (SEG), health assessment, compliance decision or worker assignment. Use pseudonymized worker/member keys and the minimum necessary personal data.

## Agent and task rows

For each row record agent/stressor ID and name, CAS or controlled identifier, mixture/component relationship, chemical/physical/biological class, form, particle-size fraction, source/release point, exposure routes, task step, routine/non-routine/maintenance/upset state, frequency, duration with unit, shift, environment, control configuration/version, potentially exposed population key, historical campaign links and direct evidence locator.

## Proposed SEG rows

Record SEG ID/name, included pseudonymized member/task keys, similarity dimensions (agent, task, process, frequency, duration, environment and controls), membership evidence, within-group differences, counterexamples, exclusions and missing representation. Job title, proximity or one measurement is not sufficient evidence. Keep proposed, reviewed and approved states separate; the agent must leave final membership and representativeness to the qualified industrial hygienist.

## Completeness and stop review

List unresolved agent identity/form/fraction, missing route or time-at-task, variant task/control versions, unauthorized health data, abnormal conditions, sampling gaps and contradictions. Stop/escalate for active apparent danger or illness, ambiguous worker identity, unjustified SEG grouping, missing current source authority, or any request to select workers, enter the site, conduct interviews, choose a sampling plan, diagnose, restrict work or determine compliance.

`decision_not_made` must state: no final SEG, exposure, health, PPE, work-status or compliance decision was made. The qualified reviewer signs only in the external authorized system; no signature or approval belongs in this asset.
