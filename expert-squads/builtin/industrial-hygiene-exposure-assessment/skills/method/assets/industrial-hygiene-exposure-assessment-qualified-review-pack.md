# Industrial Hygiene Exposure Assessment Qualified-review Pack

## Package identity and authority

Record `artifact_id`, package version/hash, evidence cutoff, facility/process/task scope, agent/SEG/sample keys, source locator/version/date inventory, owner, qualified industrial hygienist, laboratory reviewer, occupational physician, engineering and respiratory-program reviewers, EHS/privacy/legal/employment or employee-representative reviewers as applicable, jurisdiction, assumptions, uncertainty, status, `decision_not_made` and `stop_or_escalation`.

This pack is a join of frozen evidence. It is not a signed exposure assessment, employee-health conclusion, PPE selection, work restriction, compliance determination or report.

## Required branch closure

1. Scope/SEG register exact hash and open identity, route, time, variability or representation issues.
2. Sampling/analytical ledger exact hash and open custody, calibration, blank, method, detection-limit, censoring, unit or uncertainty issues.
3. OEL applicability register exact hash and open agent/form/fraction, jurisdiction, effective-version or averaging-period issues.
4. Exposure/control map exact hash and open coverage, formula, conversion, control-state or respiratory-program issues.

## Joined evidence tables

List each material claim with source asset/row, classification (fact, observation, supplied criterion, calculation, assumption, uncertainty or unknown), exact operands/units/period, applicability and reviewer. Add a contradiction table where worker/SEG/task/sample/time/unit/source/control versions disagree. Do not choose among conflicting records or average them away.

## Qualified-review and stop matrix

For each unresolved item record professional decision needed, source needed, accountable owner, qualified reviewer and due/status without setting a deadline. Stop/escalate for active danger/illness, unauthorized personal or medical information, unrepresentative sampling, unresolved QA, stale/inapplicable OEL, mixed units/periods, or any request to operate, direct work, prescribe PPE, diagnose, determine compliance or communicate externally.

`decision_not_made` must explicitly enumerate: no final SEG, sample validity, exposure, health causation, overexposure, control effectiveness, PPE/respirator, work status, compliance or reporting decision was made. Human approvals remain in the authorized system and are never synthesized here.
