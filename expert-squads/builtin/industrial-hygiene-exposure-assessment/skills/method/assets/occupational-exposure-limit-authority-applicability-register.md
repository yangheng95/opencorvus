# Occupational Exposure Limit Authority and Applicability Register

## Canonical evidence fields

`artifact_id`; `row_id`; agent ID/name/CAS; form/fraction; exposure route and skin notation; OEL record ID; issuer and source locator; source authority, title, version, effective/retrieval dates; jurisdiction and workplace applicability; limit type (regulatory PEL, recommended limit, internal limit, band or other); value; unit; averaging period/basis; ceiling/excursion notation; mixture or extended-shift rule locator; owner; qualified reviewer; assumptions; uncertainty; status; `decision_not_made`; `stop_or_escalation`.

## Applicability trace

For each candidate limit, trace the measured agent/form/fraction/route to the source definition. Record whether the measurement unit and represented duration match the OEL basis and whether a conversion is required. Preserve conversion operands and source. Keep multiple authoritative limits side by side; do not select the legally controlling or most protective limit. Mark historical, superseded, draft, internal and external limits distinctly.

If the agent lacks an authoritative OEL, record `no_current_OEL_confirmed` with the exact search/retrieval evidence. A current NIOSH occupational exposure-banding process may be referenced only when an authorized qualified professional selected it. Do not invent a band, toxicological endpoint, protective range or control category.

## Review questions

Document gaps in identity, form/fraction, jurisdiction, effective period, shift basis, additive/mixture treatment, skin route, measurement method and local implementing procedure. Record whether the source is current as of the evidence cutoff and who must confirm applicability.

Stop/escalate for stale or inaccessible sources, mixed agents/forms/units/periods, missing implementation authority, an active exposure concern, or any request to decide overexposure, safety or compliance. `decision_not_made` must explicitly state that no OEL selection, legal interpretation, exposure conclusion, control decision or employee communication was made.
