# Exposure, Control and Respiratory-protection Evidence Map

## Provenance and decision boundary

Each row includes `artifact_id`, `row_id`, agent/task/SEG/configuration IDs, source locator/authority/version/effective and observation dates, evidence cutoff, value/unit/denominator, owner, qualified reviewer, applicability/jurisdiction, assumptions, uncertainty/confidence, privacy/license boundary, status, `decision_not_made` and `stop_or_escalation`.

This map does not prescribe controls, PPE, respirators, assigned protection factors, cartridges or change schedules. It records supplied evidence for the qualified industrial hygienist, engineer and respiratory-protection-program administrator.

## Exposure calculation rows

Record input sample row IDs, compatible agent/form/fraction/route, concentration/value and unit, represented interval, uncovered time, authorized averaging rule and source. Show `sum(C_i × t_i) / represented duration` or another explicitly controlled formula with operands and conversions. For an exposure ratio, record the exact applicable OEL row and `compatible measured value / OEL`. Keep raw, corrected, reported and calculated values separate. Record censoring treatment, measurement and model uncertainty, representation limitations and non-comparability.

## Control evidence rows

Record control ID/type (elimination/substitution, engineering, work-practice, administrative or respiratory), design/reference, exact agent/task/SEG/configuration, installed version/date, operating evidence, maintenance evidence, verification method/result/date, effectiveness evidence and open deficiencies. Installed, enabled, maintained, verified and effective are separate states.

For respiratory evidence record only current program locator/version, hazard-evaluation link, equipment model and approval-list locator, fit-test/medical-evaluation/training/maintenance evidence as authorized pseudonymized statuses, and responsible program administrator. Never expose medical details or infer suitability.

Stop/escalate for incompatible units/periods, missing current OEL or formula authority, unsupported mixture treatment, active apparent danger, unverified control configuration, absent program authority, unauthorized personal/medical evidence, or a request for live work/PPE/control action. `decision_not_made` must say that no safety, overexposure, compliance, engineering, PPE, respirator, worker or medical decision was made.
