# Primary-source boundary

This clean-room method identifies evidence categories from official landing pages and requires the actual owner-controlled standards, procedures, studies and settings for work. It does not reproduce protected standard clauses, tables, equations, thresholds, settings guidance or examples.

- NERC Protection and Control standards family: https://www.nerc.com/standards/reliability-standards/prc
- NERC complete Reliability Standards catalog: https://www.nerc.com/standards/reliability-standards
- U.S. Federal Energy Regulatory Commission electric reliability: https://www.ferc.gov/electric-reliability
- IEC 60255-1 official publication page: https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/59762
- IEEE C37.113 official publication page: https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/C37.113/10433/
- Project-controlled sources: applicable regulator/ISO/RTO/utility procedures, approved network models and studies, settings governance, event/misoperation process, outage definitions and licensed IEEE/IEC editions.

Record jurisdiction, functional entity, source title, edition/version, effective date, locator, access date, applicability and access/license restriction. Never assume NERC, FERC, IEC, IEEE, ISO/RTO or utility definitions apply outside their declared scope.
