# Power Grid Protection Reliability Assurance

This package prepares source-bound electric power-system protection and reliability evidence. Four independent roots cover topology and protection configuration; fault studies and setting coordination; disturbance and misoperation evidence; and outage/reliability definitions. One explicit review owner joins every branch while preserving competing study cases, active-versus-approved settings, time-quality limits, and regulatory jurisdiction.

It does not access relays, Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA), or Energy Management Systems (EMS); change settings or logic; approve protection, switching, clearance, trip, reclose, or energization; declare a misoperation or root cause; publish reliability results; or replace protection engineers, operators, asset owners, cybersecurity teams, reliability coordinators, or regulators.
