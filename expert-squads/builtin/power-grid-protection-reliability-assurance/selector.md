# Selection guidance

Select for a named power-system protection and reliability evidence review involving topology/model baselines, protection zones, relays, current or voltage transformers, breakers, trip logic, teleprotection, fault studies, setting coordination, oscillography, misoperations, outages, or reliability metrics. Do not select for energy-market planning, battery product safety, live dispatch, switching, relay access, setting changes, trip/reclose/energization, cybersecurity response, or compliance certification.
