# Primary method sources

Sources are locators for current operator or professional review. They are not copied templates and do not create jurisdiction-neutral legal, tax, accounting, security, or business rules.

- US Small Business Administration, Manage Your Business: <https://www.sba.gov/counseling/manage-your-business/>. Supports keeping finance, tax/compliance, marketing/sales, cybersecurity, emergency, and lifecycle responsibilities visible as distinct operating concerns.
- US Small Business Administration, Manage Your Finances: <https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business/manage-your-finances>. Supports source-bound income, expense, asset, liability, equity, and cash-flow evidence without turning the Agent into an accountant.
- US Internal Revenue Service, Recordkeeping: <https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/recordkeeping>. Supports transaction-support evidence and operator-controlled record policy; it does not justify universal retention periods.
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 Small Business resources: <https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber/nist-cybersecurity-framework-0>. Supports explicit governance, identity, protection, detection, response, and recovery evidence without copying controls or claiming conformance.
- JSON Schema Draft 2020-12 Core: <https://json-schema.org/draft/2020-12/json-schema-core>. Defines the profile instance contract.
- IANA Time Zone Database: <https://www.iana.org/time-zones>. Time-zone identifiers and tzdb version are explicit because civil-time rules change.
- ISO 4217 official page: <https://www.iso.org/standard/64758.html>. Currency metadata is explicit; the standard’s protected content is not copied.
- OWASP Secrets Management Cheat Sheet: <https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Secrets_Management_Cheat_Sheet.html>. Supports reference-only secret handling, scope, ownership, audit, and rotation boundaries.
- OpenTelemetry logs and signals concepts: <https://opentelemetry.io/docs/concepts/signals/logs/> and <https://opentelemetry.io/docs/concepts/signals/>. Supports stable event/correlation identity across logs, metrics, traces, and business events without binding the profile to one provider.

For each review, record source version or retrieval date, applicability, owner, uncertainty, and the qualified professional responsible for current interpretation.
