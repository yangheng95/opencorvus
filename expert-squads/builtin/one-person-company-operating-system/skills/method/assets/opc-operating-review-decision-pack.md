# OPC operating review decision pack

Artifact version: `2026.08.11.1`. This is the only joined company-level output. It cannot be completed until the four root artifacts use the same company/profile/schema identity, compatible evidence cutoff, time/currency basis, stable reconciliation keys, and explicit authority boundary.

## Review identity and closure

Record package version/digest; `artifact_id`; company/profile/schema IDs, versions, and digest; branch artifact versions/hashes; evidence cutoff; IANA time zone/tzdb version; functional currency and conversion sources; jurisdictions; complete source/version/date inventory; owner approval identity; qualified reviewers; self-review disclosure; applicability; assumptions; uncertainty; privacy/license boundary; status; decisions excluded; `decision_not_made`; `outcome_unknown`; and stop/escalation.

## Joined operating scorecard

For each material item join offer/customer/channel/pipeline evidence to commercial events, cash/obligation evidence, delivery/customer/capacity evidence, and workflow/approval/observability evidence. Show metric definition and formula version, numerator, denominator, unit, window, cutoff, source, owner, uncertainty, contradiction, and missing key. Do not add incompatible values or convert currency without source/time evidence.

## Decision queues

The owner queue records stable decision ID, objective, evidence and counterevidence, business impact as supplied, capacity requirement, cash effect as supplied, dependency, reversible next step, approval class, owner, due window, stop condition, and expected reconciliation evidence. A separate qualified-review queue records legal, tax, accounting, finance, privacy, security, employment, insurance, regulated, or technical questions with source and named professional owner.

The next-cycle evidence plan records source gaps, retrieval owner, expected date, verification rule, and branch affected. Preserve unresolved contradiction and external `outcome_unknown` states; never hide them behind a green status.

`decision_not_made` enumerates that no public claim, price, discount, contract, payment, refund, procurement, filing, accounting/tax/legal treatment, customer commitment, publication, deployment, data export/deletion, permission/secret, bulk contact, risk acceptance, or professional conclusion was made. The pack is evidence for the owner and qualified reviewers, not execution authority.
