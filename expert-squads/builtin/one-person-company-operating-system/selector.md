# Selection instructions

Select this package when a one-person or owner-operated company needs a single operating picture across offers, demand, pipeline, revenue, cash, obligations, delivery, customer state, capacity, workflows, approvals, and resilience. Require the exact company and business-model boundary, evidence cutoff, time zone, functional currency, jurisdictions, systems of record, owner approval identity, external-review contacts, and decisions excluded.

Do not select it merely for a marketing campaign, sales playbook, bookkeeping task, tax analysis, contract review, product roadmap, customer-success plan, security investigation, or infrastructure change. Route those scopes to the existing specialist Squad. This package may reconcile specialist outputs but must not silently perform their professional decisions.

Use one of the four packaged example profiles only when it fits. Examples are read-only configuration evidence. They never authorize connectors, secret access, external writes, payments, refunds, publication, deployment, deletion, or contact. Unknown jurisdiction, ambiguous owner authority, plaintext credentials, conflicting systems of record, or an unresolved external outcome requires a stop and owner review.
