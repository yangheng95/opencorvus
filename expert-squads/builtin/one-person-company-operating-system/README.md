# One Person Company Operating System

This package gives an owner-operated company one evidence-bound operating review across offer and demand, revenue and obligations, delivery and customer capacity, and automation governance. It is a control and reconciliation surface, not a collection of fictional executives and not a substitute for specialist marketing, sales, finance, tax, legal, product, customer-success, security, or engineering work.

Every scheduler and worker uses only `one-person-company-operating-system/shared/method`. Four zero-dependency analysts run concurrently and one explicit owner joins their exact outputs. Five core assets preserve company identity, source/version/date, measures and units, owners, approvals, uncertainty, applicability, stop conditions, decisions not made, and outcomes that require reconciliation.

## Commercial profiles

The Skill owns one Draft 2020-12 schema and four versioned profiles under `skills/method/examples/`:

- `micro-saas.json` for subscriptions, entitlements, product telemetry, support, deployments, and recurring revenue evidence.
- `consulting.json` for opportunities, signed scope, capacity, deliverables, invoices, payments, and client handoffs.
- `creator-media.json` for content rights, editorial work, audience evidence, sponsors, affiliates, publication, and revenue.
- `digital-product-commerce.json` for catalog, orders, payments, refunds, entitlement or fulfillment, inventory, returns, and support.

Select a profile only when its business-model boundary matches the operator’s evidence. Otherwise create a custom profile using the same schema and leave unknown fields explicit. A profile is configuration evidence, not authorization to connect to or write any system.

## Commercial quick starts

- “Use the micro-SaaS profile to reconcile the last 30 days of subscription, entitlement, support, availability, and cash evidence; identify the three owner decisions that matter next week.”
- “Use the consulting profile to reconcile signed scope, booked capacity, deliverable acceptance, invoice aging, and client commitments without sending or changing anything.”
- “Use the creator/media profile to join the publication calendar, rights coverage, sponsor obligations, audience response, and attributed revenue; hold every public claim for approval.”
- “Use the digital-product/commerce profile to reconcile order, payment, fulfillment, refund, inventory, and support records; mark ambiguous external outcomes before any retry.”

## Authority

The package never sends, publishes, pays, refunds, signs, files, deploys, deletes, changes permissions, or contacts external parties. Same-person preparation and review is recorded as `self_review`, never independent review. Legal, tax, accounting, financing, insurance, privacy, employment, regulated, and production decisions remain with the owner and named qualified professionals.
