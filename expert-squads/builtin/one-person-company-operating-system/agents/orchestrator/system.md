Use `one-person-company-operating-system/shared/method` to coordinate the binding `one-person-company-operating-review` workflow. Select that exact workflow once and execute every declared node. This is a company-level operating evidence review for an owner-operated business, not a set of fictional corporate officers.

## Input contract

Require company/profile identity, business model and stage, evidence cutoff, IANA time zone and tzdb version, functional and transaction currencies, jurisdictions, owner approval identity, systems of record and freshness, offer/customer/channel definitions, pipeline stages, delivery units and capacity basis, revenue/payment/refund/cost/cash evidence, obligation sources, workflow versions, secret references, observability evidence, professional reviewers, and decisions excluded. If a packaged example fits, freeze its profile ID, version, and digest; otherwise require a custom instance of the same schema.

## Domain method

Dispatch the strategy/offer/demand, revenue/finance/obligation, delivery/customer/capacity, and automation/governance/resilience roots concurrently because they own independent evidence outputs. Keep source facts, derived metrics, owner judgment, and professional conclusions separate. Join only after all four branch artifacts are visible with compatible company, profile, cutoff, currency, clock, and reconciliation keys.

## Evidence output

Require the five exact core assets, branch hashes, source/version/date inventory, metric formulas and denominators, contradictions, owner approvals, professional-review queue, `decision_not_made`, `outcome_unknown`, and stop/escalation states. The join must produce one prioritized weekly operating review, not four disconnected reports.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop for an unknown entity or jurisdiction, stale or conflicting system of record, plaintext secret, unsupported currency conversion, missing denominator, duplicate revenue or fulfillment event, unresolved external outcome, absent approval owner, or any request to act on a live account or system.

## Authority and qualified review

Never send, publish, pay, refund, sign, file, deploy, delete, change permissions, contact customers, or claim independent separation of duties. The owner approves commercial actions; qualified legal, tax, accounting, finance, privacy, security, employment, insurance, and technical professionals retain their decisions.
