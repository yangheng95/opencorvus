Use `one-person-company-operating-system/shared/method` for the strategy, offer, customer, channel, demand, and experiment branch only.

## Input contract

Require company and profile ID/version, business model and stage, evidence cutoff, offer IDs and versions, target customer or audience definitions, channel IDs, price and promise versions, funnel-stage definitions, source systems and freshness, demand observations, experiment records, budgets as supplied, owner, qualified marketing/sales/product reviewers, applicable jurisdictions, and decisions excluded. Treat customer statements, analytics, transactions, and operator beliefs as different evidence classes.

## Domain method

Freeze offer, customer, channel, and time boundaries before comparison. Reconcile funnel numerators with explicit denominators and stage-entry rules. For each demand claim, distinguish observed behavior, stated intent, inferred explanation, and unknown. Map each experiment to a hypothesis, exact intervention, population, exposure, measure, guardrail, source cutoff, result, contradiction, and opportunity cost. Compare offers and channels only on compatible cohorts, windows, currencies, and attribution rules; never import an industry benchmark as a target.

## Evidence output

Complete `opc-offer-channel-demand-experiment-portfolio.md`. Record stable row IDs, source/version/date, offer/customer/channel/version, metric formula/numerator/denominator/unit, evidence strength, counterevidence, attribution limits, cost/capacity dependence, owner, reviewer, applicability, uncertainty, status, approval reference, decision not made, and stop/escalation. Return exact keys for the join.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop for undefined stage denominators, mixed offer versions, missing attribution window, unverifiable public claim, non-comparable cohorts, hidden channel cost, consent/privacy ambiguity, or a request to publish, change price, launch a campaign, purchase media, promise growth, or contact a lead.

## Authority and qualified review

Never guarantee demand, conversion, retention, revenue, or product-market fit. Never publish, change catalog or price, spend, contact, scrape restricted data, or make legal/privacy claims. Require the owner and appropriate marketing, sales, product, finance, legal, privacy, and brand/rights reviewers.
