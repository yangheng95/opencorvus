Use `one-person-company-operating-system/shared/method` only after all four independent operating branches complete.

## Input contract

Require exact versions and hashes of the company baseline, offer/demand portfolio, revenue/cash/obligation ledger, and delivery/customer/automation register; one company/profile ID and schema version; compatible evidence cutoffs, clocks, currencies, jurisdictions, event IDs, customer/order/project/subscription keys, metric definitions, source inventory, contradictions, owner approvals, professional reviewers, and decisions excluded. Reject a synthetic role chart that disguises one owner as independent approvers.

## Domain method

Reconcile cross-branch identities before prioritization: offer to customer/channel/pipeline; commercial event to invoice/payment/refund/cash; promise to delivery/capacity/support; and any proposed action to workflow, approval, external-effect, observability, and recovery evidence. Separate fact, calculation, inference, owner choice, and qualified professional decision. Rank only work items with explicit benefit/risk evidence, capacity cost, cash effect as supplied, dependency, reversible next step, owner, approval, and stop condition. Preserve disagreements rather than averaging them away.

## Evidence output

Complete `opc-operating-review-decision-pack.md`. Include branch hashes; source/version/date register; company/profile/config digest; joined operating scorecard; offer-to-cash-to-delivery reconciliation; capacity and obligation conflicts; workflow and external-effect risks; prioritized owner decision queue; qualified external review queue; next-cycle evidence plan; assumptions and uncertainty; `self_review`; `decision_not_made`; `outcome_unknown`; and stop/escalation. Every material statement cites an exact asset row.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop for cross-branch identity drift, incompatible cutoffs/currencies/units, missing source, duplicate or unmatched event, professional question presented as operational fact, plaintext secret, absent owner approval, unresolved external outcome, overcommitted capacity, or a requested live commercial/financial/customer/technical action.

## Authority and qualified review

Never send, publish, pay, refund, sign, file, deploy, delete, change access, contact a party, or claim legal, tax, accounting, financial, privacy, security, employment, insurance, regulatory, or independent-review authority. The result is a decision pack for the owner and named professionals, not an executed operating decision.
