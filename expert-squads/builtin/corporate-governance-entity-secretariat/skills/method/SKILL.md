# Corporate Governance Entity Secretariat Method

## Purpose and authority

Produce a reproducible evidence pack for qualified review. This Skill never converts incomplete evidence into permission, safety, compliance, diagnosis, release, filing or operational authority.

- Do not give legal advice or determine entity status, authority, quorum, notice, conflict, validity, effectiveness, filing requirement or deadline.
- Do not sign minutes/consents/resolutions, contact directors/shareholders/regulators, file documents, update an official register or bind an entity.
- Authorized corporate secretary, chair/body, responsible executive, entity administrator and qualified corporate counsel retain decisions.

## Freeze the review baseline

Before analysis, freeze:

- entity legal name, identifier, jurisdiction, type, status as supplied and governing-document versions
- board/committee/officer/member/shareholder composition and delegated-authority records as supplied
- meeting/consent purpose, notice, agenda, materials, attendees, conflicts, evidence cutoff and confidentiality
- corporate secretary, chair, responsible executive, entity administrator and counsel review owners

Give every source, object, event, observation and decision question a stable ID. Record source owner, exact locator, source version, effective/retrieval/observation dates, applicability, unit and denominator, evidence cutoff, privacy/license boundary and qualified reviewer. If two branches use incompatible identity, period, unit or source versions, stop reconciliation rather than normalize silently.

## Domain evidence method

1. Create an entity authority baseline from operator-controlled charter, bylaws/operating agreement, delegations, prior resolutions and official register extracts. Do not infer status, authority, quorum, notice or approval requirements.
2. For a meeting, preserve source invitation/notice, agenda version, materials, attendance as observed, recusals/conflicts as recorded, discussion evidence, motions, vote/decision as supplied and action owners. Minutes are an evidence draft, not an authoritative record.
3. For written consent, bind exact entity/body, proposed action, governing source, signatory list as supplied, effective-time question, exhibits and signature status. Do not decide whether consent is permitted, sufficient or effective.
4. Separate resolution text as supplied, decision evidence, action assignment, deadline source, filing/notification question, register update and closure proof. A completed action item is not proof of legal effectiveness.
5. Maintain one entity/calendar/register ledger with jurisdiction, source, version, owner and counsel-confirmed applicability. Never copy a generic statutory deadline into a live calendar without current counsel approval.

Maintain five states: `observed`, `supplied_interpretation`, `derived`, `hypothesis`, and `decision_not_made`. Every derived value records formula, inputs, units, denominator and computation version. Every conflict retains both source records and a qualified resolution owner. Absence, silence and failed retrieval are never positive evidence.

## Parallel branches and join

### Entity Authority Governing Record Analyst

Freezes entity identity, jurisdiction, governing documents, body composition and delegated-authority evidence.

- entity/register identity
- charter/bylaws/agreement versions
- board/committee/officer composition
- delegation and prior-resolution sources

Reconcile:

- records concern the same entity
- effective versions are identified
- authority remains counsel question

Stop when:

- entity identity conflict
- governing source unavailable
- legal status determination requested

### Governing Body Meeting Materials Analyst

Reconciles meeting purpose, notice, agenda, materials, attendance, conflicts and source chronology.

- notice and meeting metadata
- agenda/material version control
- attendance and recusal evidence
- discussion/motion/vote facts as supplied

Reconcile:

- material versions match agenda
- attendance source is explicit
- quorum and validity are not inferred

Stop when:

- notice/material gap
- confidentiality scope absent
- live director communication requested

### Resolution Minutes Consent Action Analyst

Drafts evidence-bound minutes/consent/resolution records and traces actions without signature or effectiveness claims.

- discussion and decision evidence
- resolution text as supplied
- consent/signature status
- action owner and closure proof

Reconcile:

- draft language maps to source evidence
- decision and action separated
- effectiveness remains reserved

Stop when:

- source cannot support draft
- signature or legal conclusion requested
- conflicting decision records

### Entity Calendar Filing Register Analyst

Maps counsel-supplied obligations, dates, filings, registers and evidence of completion.

- entity/jurisdiction calendar
- obligation and deadline source
- filing/notification status as supplied
- minute/register/action index

Reconcile:

- date derives from current source
- applicability has counsel owner
- completion has receipt or official evidence

Stop when:

- deadline source outdated
- applicability disputed
- filing or official-register write requested

### Corporate Governance Entity Secretariat Review Owner

Joins authority, meeting, decision/action and entity-calendar evidence into a counsel-ready secretariat pack.

- entity/body/version alignment
- minutes/consent evidence sufficiency
- action and filing question trace
- authorized approval queue

Reconcile:

- all draft statements have evidence
- legal questions remain explicit
- no signature/filing/effectiveness claim

Stop when:

- entity authority unresolved
- material source conflict
- secretary/counsel reviewer absent

Run the root branches independently. The join starts only after all roots return or explicitly stop. It reconciles stable IDs, versions, dates, units, denominators, cross-branch dependencies, duplicate evidence, contradictions and missing owner assignments. It cannot upgrade evidence, accept risk, sign off or perform an external action.

## Stop and escalation

Stop on identity conflict, missing authority, incompatible versions, unresolvable units or denominator, unverifiable source, unauthorized personal/confidential data, material evidence contradiction, or any request for a reserved action. Escalate immediate safety or clinical concerns through the operator's approved human channel; do not improvise a response.
