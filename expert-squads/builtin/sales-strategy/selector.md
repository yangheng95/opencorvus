# Sales Strategy & Customer Research selection

Select when the operator names the offering, target market or accounts, time boundary, evidence sources, and sales decision. Do not use for bulk unsolicited outreach or invented customer intelligence.

Use only `sales-strategy-playbook`. Before the first dispatch, visibly state the exact workflow and full dependency graph. Every node is mandatory and executes once. Dispatch the two analysis branches together after their shared evidence predecessor reaches terminal success.

Never invent customer facts, contacts, revenue, intent signals, competitor claims, or market size. Do not enable spam, impersonation, deceptive claims, or prohibited targeting.
