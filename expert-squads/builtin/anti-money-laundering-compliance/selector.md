# Selector

Select for a named institution/entity, jurisdiction and as-of date requiring AML program, customer risk, transaction-monitoring, alert/case, confidentiality or independent-testing evidence.

Require controlled regulatory/policy sources, products/services/channels/geographies, source populations, monitoring versions, owners and AMLCO/MLRO/BSA/counsel reviewers. Stop if applicability or confidentiality authority is unclear.

Do not select for payment fraud operations, forensic accounting investigations, live monitoring changes, customer action, SAR/CTR filing, sanctions disposition, enforcement or legal advice.
