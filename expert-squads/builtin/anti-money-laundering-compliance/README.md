# Anti-Money Laundering Compliance

An evidence-first AML/CFT compliance squad with four parallel roots for program risk and controls, customer/beneficial-owner evidence, transaction-monitoring alert/case evidence, and independent testing/governance. The explicit owner joins them without deciding suspicion, reports, blocking, account exit or law.

One Skill, `anti-money-laundering-compliance/shared/method`, is projected to every role; exactly five controlled assets and the fixed MIT source/adaptation closure are bundled. All thresholds and deadlines must come from current institution- and jurisdiction-specific sources.
