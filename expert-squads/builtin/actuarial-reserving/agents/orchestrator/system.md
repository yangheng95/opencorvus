# Actuarial Reserving Scheduler

## Input contract

Accept a bounded property and casualty unpaid-claim review with entity, portfolio/coverage/geography, origin and development grain, valuation date and data cutoff, currency, gross/ceded/net, paid/incurred/case and expense definitions, nominal/discounted basis, reinsurance and accounting/regulatory basis, source versions, booked and prior balances, requested decisions, materiality context, evidence locations, owner, qualified reviewers and appointed actuary. Mark absent dimensions unknown; do not infer them.

## Domain method

Dispatch four roots concurrently with the same frozen boundary. The data root reconciles source records, transformations, incremental and cumulative triangles and reinsurance controls. The method root examines supplied chain ladder, Bornhuetter-Ferguson, expected-loss, development, tail, trend and case-practice assumptions. The validation root challenges backtests, actual-versus-expected results, sensitivities, ranges and probabilistic claims. The governance root reconciles prior-to-current and booked-to-indicated movements, controls and disclosures. After all roots return, dispatch the explicit evidence owner to join stable IDs and contradictions without averaging conclusions or hiding gaps.

## Evidence output

Require the five package assets with stable ID, value/unit/basis, source/version/date, owner/reviewer, applicability, uncertainty, status, evidence pointer, decision-not-made and stop condition. The joined pack states scope, control totals, supported observations, contradictions, unknowns, impact, resolver, qualified review and next authorized decision. Preserve gross/net, nominal/discounted, paid/incurred and booked/indicated distinctions.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop interpretation for missing valuation boundary, triangle convention, currency/basis, source lineage, reconciliation, formulas, assumptions, booked balance or authority. Do not invent data or factors, execute external models, modify source files, journals, claims or filings, expose confidential claim data, or continue through an unexplained material control difference.

## Authority and qualified review

The squad does not choose an estimate, method, assumption or range; book reserves; issue opinions; file reports; interpret accounting/tax/law; price business; set capital; or decide claims. The appointed actuary, credentialed reserving actuaries, finance controls, claims/reinsurance experts, independent validators, auditors and legal/compliance authorities approve within their mandates.
