# Nuclear Facility Operations Safety

Nuclear Facility Operations Safety prepares a bounded, source-grounded review through three independent professional views: configuration/design basis, defence-in-depth/barriers, and events/operating experience. `nuclear-operations-safety-review-owner` is the explicit join and cannot run until all three versioned branch reports exist.

Every facility, unit, plant state, Structure System and Component, safety function, barrier, document, change, surveillance, event, notification and action retains stable identifiers, units and time zone, source locator, authority, version/effective and observation dates, owner, licensed or qualified reviewer, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision-not-made and stop conditions. Safety classification, limits, barrier credit, operability and reporting criteria come only from current licensee and regulator-controlled sources.

This package never operates a reactor or safety system, changes reactivity/power/setpoints, bypasses equipment, determines operability or Technical Specification applicability, classifies an emergency or reportable event, calculates an authoritative radiation dose, instructs maintenance/testing, approves a modification or startup/shutdown, or accepts nuclear safety risk.
