Use `nuclear-facility-operations-safety/shared/method` only after all three independent nuclear branch reports exist.

## Input contract

Require exact artifact paths, versions and hashes for the configuration/design-basis register, plant-state/surveillance ledger, defence-in-depth map and event/operating-experience log; common facility/unit/plant-state/SSC/function/barrier/event/time keys; license and controlled-source versions/effective dates; evidence cutoff/time zone; accountable licensee owner; licensed/qualified reviewers; security/safeguards boundary; unresolved branch unknowns; and excluded operating, operability, reporting, emergency and licensing decisions. Reject partial or incompatible branches.

## Domain method

Confirm roots remained independent and source-addressable. Reconcile only stable IDs and compatible plant-state/time/source versions. Create explicit contradictions where design requirements, physical configuration, documents, barrier/status evidence and event records disagree. Use only licensee-supplied safety classification and prioritization; never invent risk, probability or barrier credit. Link every bounded evidence action to source need, affected function/SSC, existing control, dependency, owner, qualified reviewer, review date, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision authority and stop condition.

## Evidence output

Complete `nuclear-facility-operations-safety-review-pack.md` with branch versions/hashes, joined findings, contradictions, configuration gaps, challenged functions/barriers, event/action links, operating-experience applicability, bounded evidence-review actions, owners, licensed/qualified reviewers, uncertainty, status, decision-not-made, stop/escalation and separate human-decision fields.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop when any branch is absent, facility/unit/plant-state/SSC identity conflicts, current authority or success criterion is missing, active abnormal/emergency state appears, protected security/safeguards data would be exposed, or a conclusion would imply operability, Technical Specification action, reportability, dose, configuration acceptance, restart, compliance or risk acceptance.

## Authority and qualified review

Never operate, direct control, change reactivity/power/setpoints, bypass equipment, determine operability/Limiting Condition for Operation, classify or report an event, direct emergency/work/test response, approve modification/startup/shutdown, close actions or accept risk. Route decisions to licensed operator/shift manager, configuration/system/design engineering, work control, radiation protection, quality/event investigation, independent safety review, emergency director, licensee management and regulator.
