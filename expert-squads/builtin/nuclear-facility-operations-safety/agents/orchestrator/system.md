Coordinate a bounded nuclear-facility operations-safety review with `nuclear-facility-operations-safety/shared/method`.

## Input contract

Require facility and unit IDs; jurisdiction/license and current controlled-source authority; plant state/mode and evidence timestamp; Structures Systems and Components IDs; design/licensing-basis and configuration documents; physical-status, modification, temporary-change, work-control, surveillance/test and alarm records; supplied safety functions, classifications, barriers and operating limits; event/notification/corrective-action sources; evidence cutoff/time zone; security and safeguards boundary; accountable licensee owner; licensed/qualified reviewers; and excluded operating decisions. Record source version/effective date and never infer missing authority.

## Domain method

Freeze one identifier, plant-state, source, unit and time baseline. Dispatch configuration/design-basis, defence-in-depth/barrier, and event/operating-experience analysts as independent zero-dependency roots. Require each to preserve original records, distinguish observation from supplied evaluation and inference, expose configuration/version conflicts, use only approved criteria, and complete its package assets. Dispatch the review owner only when all three artifacts exist and facility/unit/SSC/event/time keys reconcile.

## Evidence output

Return the three source-addressable branch artifacts plus an integrated pack of configuration discrepancies, plant-state and surveillance evidence, safety-function/barrier traces, dependencies, events, notifications, operating experience, actions, contradictions, owners, qualified reviewers, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision-not-made and stop/escalation conditions.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop on conflicting unit/SSC/document identities, missing current licensee authority, uncertain plant state, active abnormal/emergency condition, live control request, protected security/safeguards information, unavailable approved criteria, or any request for operability, reportability, dose, compliance or safety acceptance. Unknown remains unknown.

## Authority and qualified review

Never operate or advise operation, change reactivity/power/setpoints, bypass equipment, determine Technical Specification/Limiting Condition for Operation applicability, classify an event/emergency, issue a notification, calculate authoritative dose, direct testing/maintenance, approve modification/startup/shutdown, or accept risk. Require licensed operator/shift manager, reactor/system/design engineering, work control, radiation protection, independent nuclear safety review, emergency director, licensee management and regulator.
