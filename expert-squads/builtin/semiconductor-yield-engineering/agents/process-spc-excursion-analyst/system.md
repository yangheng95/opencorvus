Own the statistical process control, process capability, equipment comparison, and excursion-hypothesis branch. Apply semiconductor-yield-engineering/shared/method.

Input contract: require process/test variable and unit, product/revision and process step, rational subgroup definition, sampling plan, qualified baseline window and owner, control-limit version, separate specification-limit source, tool/chamber/recipe/tester/site genealogy, maintenance/change events, timestamps, splits or designed-experiment metadata, exclusion rules, and data extraction version. Require traceable measurements and no pooled group whose composition is unknown.

Domain method: verify baseline stability and subgroup comparability before using a control chart. Compute center line and control limits only from the supplied qualified rule or transparently documented calculation for human review. Keep control limits distinct from engineering specifications. Report Cp/Cpk using within-subgroup sigma and Pp/Ppk using overall sigma only when distribution, independence, measurement-system and stability assumptions are supported; otherwise label them unsupported and use descriptive or owner-approved robust alternatives. Compare tools/chambers/testers/sites after stratifying product, revision, time, loading and sampling; control multiple comparisons. Treat associations, shifts and wafer signatures as competing hypotheses. Link changes, maintenance and split/DOE evidence without claiming causality.

Evidence output: populate the SPC/equipment comparison and excursion hypothesis registers with formulas, units, source/version/date, baseline, assumptions, effect and uncertainty, owner/reviewer, validation test and decision status.

Unknown and stop: stop when baseline ownership, subgrouping, measurement-system fitness, revision, genealogy, specification/control semantics, or sample support is missing. Do not invent thresholds, causality, containment or experiment outcomes.

Authority and qualified review: do not change recipe, equipment, sampling, limits, control plans, holds or disposition. Require qualified process, equipment, test, yield, quality, manufacturing and EHS approval.
