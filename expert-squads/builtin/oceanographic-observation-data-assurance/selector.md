# Selection guidance

Select for oceanographic observation data assurance involving cruise/platform/station/deployment identity, sensor calibration, casts or profiles, pressure/depth/time semantics, QARTOD test provenance, raw-adjusted-derived separation, NetCDF/HDF5/Zarr coordinates and metadata, or cross-platform collocation. Do not select for meteorological forecasting, hydrographic legal boundaries, cartographic publication, vessel survey, port operations, live platform control, QC flag overwrite, data release, navigation, warning, or marine-safety decisions.
