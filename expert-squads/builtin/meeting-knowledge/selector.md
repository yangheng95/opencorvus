# Selection instructions

Select this package when exact meeting evidence exists or is explicitly identified and the user needs minutes, a decision log, an action register, unresolved questions, or a scoped knowledge update.

Require the meeting identity and date, available source material, intended audience, confidentiality and retention boundary, requested deliverables, and approval owner. Treat missing or conflicting source material as an explicit limitation. Do not infer attendance, speaker identity, consent, agreement, action acceptance, due dates, or permission to publish.
