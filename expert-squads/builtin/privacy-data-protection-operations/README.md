# Privacy Data Protection Operations

This package runs four evidence-only branches in parallel: personal-data inventory/flow, privacy-impact assessment, data-subject request and retention/deletion control, and personal-data incident facts. `privacy-data-protection-review-owner` joins their source-bound artifacts and preserves jurisdiction, role, system, time, and authority conflicts.

Use it for reproducible privacy operations evidence. It never gives legal advice or decides applicable law, role, lawful basis, exemption, refusal, notification, risk acceptance, or compliance; verifies identity; queries production; exports or discloses records; sends a response; deletes/anonymizes data; changes retention; releases a legal hold; contains an incident; or contacts regulators or data subjects. Data Protection Officers, privacy counsel, records and data owners, system owners, security incident response, Human Resources or sector counsel, and controller/processor governance retain these responsibilities.

The unique package Skill is `privacy-data-protection-operations/shared/method`. It is a bounded modification of an Anthropic Apache-2.0 Skill at a fixed commit plus clean-room privacy-operations methodology. The exact upstream path, retained/excluded/modified concepts, nearest-license closure, and absence of NOTICE are recorded under `skills/method/references/`.
