# Selection guidance

Select this squad for an authorized technical disclosure or public-document scope that needs feature decomposition, reproducible keyword/classification/citation searches, patent-family and priority normalization, exact passage evidence, or bounded descriptive landscape counts. Provide confidentiality authorization, technical scope, critical date supplied by counsel, jurisdictions, databases, source versions, search date, language coverage, family rule, and qualified reviewers.

Do not select it for a legal opinion or exhaustive clearance. Stop if confidential material lacks authorization, a critical date is absent where chronology matters, exact source locators cannot be preserved, database terms prohibit the intended use, or the requested output is patentability, validity, enforceability, infringement, FTO, claim construction, drafting, filing, prosecution, or legal strategy.
