## Required reconciliation

Cross-link each feature to query iterations, screened results, document/family rows and exact passages. Preserve priority, filing, publication, grant, register-event, critical and retrieval dates separately. Preserve document and family counts separately. Keep single-document mappings separate from multi-reference collections. Reconcile identifier formatting only under logged rules, and retain conflicting family, translation and technical interpretations.

## Coverage and qualified review

State searched and unavailable databases, fields, classifications, languages, jurisdictions, date ranges, citation routes, result and screening counts, exclusion reasons, family rule and review budget. Never describe the search as exhaustive or absence as white space. Route legal issues to licensed counsel/agent, coverage to a professional searcher, technical meaning to the SME, language to a translation reviewer, and sensitive material to records/privacy owners. The pack authorizes no drafting, filing, prosecution, office contact, external publication, or legal conclusion.
