## Use rules

Keep disclosure wording, published claim wording, reviewer interpretation and researcher-generated vocabulary distinct. Do not label any feature legally essential, rewrite a claim, infer an undisclosed embodiment, or resolve ambiguous legal meaning. Classification codes are search hypotheses until checked against an official edition. Minimize confidential detail and never export or publish without authorization.

## Review queue

List ambiguous terms, competing translations, missing units/context, classification questions and confidentiality constraints with exact provenance, owner, qualified reviewer, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision explicitly not made, and stop reason. Counsel owns legal framing; the technical SME owns technical accuracy; the professional searcher owns search utility.
