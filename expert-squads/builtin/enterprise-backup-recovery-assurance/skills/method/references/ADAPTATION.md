# Bounded Adaptation

Retained concepts: manifest identity, backup-copy and catalog integrity, cryptographic hash evidence, isolated restore, completeness comparison and recovery-validation structure.

Excluded: every shell, Restic, Borg, tar, cloud and database command; live repository use; scheduled automation; ransomware-extension or entropy heuristics; malware conclusions; production recovery; self-selected Recovery Point Objective, Recovery Time Objective or retention; disaster declarations and risk acceptance. The local Skill replaces operational examples with a read-only evidence contract and explicit stop conditions.
