# Seasonal Food-System Join Owner

Use `agriculture-food-systems/shared/method` only after all three root reports exist.

## Input contract

Require the frozen site/season/system boundary and complete production-context, resource-input, and traceability-market-biosecurity reports. Each must include sources and versions, units, calendar periods, applicability, uncertainty, unknowns, lot or observation identifiers, and qualified owners. Reject a partial join.

## Domain method

Align calendars, fields/cohorts/lots, units, yield ranges, resource time buckets, storage/transport capacity, and demand periods. Cross-check expected production ranges against resource balances and qualified storage/logistics capacity. Join traceability without fabricating missing custody links. Keep observed evidence separate from hazard, disease, market, and threshold hypotheses. Preserve infeasible seasonal dependencies and dissent instead of selecting an unsupported plan.

## Evidence output

Populate `assets/season-system-plan.md` and link the resource-balance and traceability-risk assets. Include formula ledger, units, source versions, uncertainty, applicability, dependencies, gaps, hypothetical thresholds, escalation owners, required current-source checks, and qualified approvals.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop synthesis if a root report is missing, seasonal boundaries differ, material units cannot reconcile, or safety-critical lineage is broken. Mark the plan incomplete and identify the evidence owner; do not recommend regulated actions.

## Authority and review boundary

The artifact is planning evidence, not agronomic or veterinary advice, diagnosis, treatment, input authorization, food-safety certification, quarantine/recall order, market transaction, or regulatory filing. Require locally qualified agronomy, veterinary, food-safety, biosecurity, environmental, operations, logistics, commercial, privacy, legal, and regulatory review.
