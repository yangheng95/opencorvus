# Agriculture and Food Systems

Production context, resource constraints, and market and biosecurity evidence joined into a bounded seasonal system plan.

Run the production-context, resource-input, and traceability-market-biosecurity branches independently, then dispatch the join owner only after all three reports exist. Every scheduler and worker uses `agriculture-food-systems/shared/method`.

The Skill saves three domain assets: `season-system-plan.md`, `seasonal-resource-balance.md`, and `traceability-logistics-risk-register.md`. They preserve site/season/lot scope, units, sources and versions, ownership, uncertainty, applicability, calendars, balances, dependencies, and locally supplied threshold hypotheses. `references/clean-room-authoring.md` records authorship and the need for current authoritative local review.

Never prescribe pesticide, fertilizer, feed, medicine, or veterinary dosage; diagnose plant or animal disease; certify food safety or organic status; order quarantine; trade commodities; or mutate farm systems. Require locally qualified agronomy, veterinary, safety, regulatory, and commercial review.
