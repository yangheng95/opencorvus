# Procurement & Vendor Decision

Procurement & Vendor Decision prepares an evidence-backed vendor decision pack without purchasing, approving, signing, or representing that due diligence is complete beyond the available evidence.

The binding `vendor-decision-pack` workflow starts three independent specialist reviews from the same operator scope: commercial comparison, vendor diligence, and approval-evidence governance. `procurement-decision-integrator` is the explicit join node and may run only after all three specialist Artifacts reach terminal success.

Every score preserves its criterion, source, observation date, uncertainty, and weighting rationale. Vendor claims remain claims until independently supported. Missing security, financial, legal, beneficial-ownership, sanctions, reference, or conflict-of-interest evidence is visible in the final pack rather than replaced with an assumption.

The package prepares a recommendation and approval evidence. It never places an order, accepts terms, authorizes spend, or substitutes for the operator's procurement, security, finance, legal, or executive approval authority.
