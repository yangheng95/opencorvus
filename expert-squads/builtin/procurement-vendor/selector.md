# Procurement & Vendor Decision selection

Select this Expert Squad when an operator must compare two or more vendors, investigate material supplier risks, and prepare a reviewable approval record. Preserve the decision owner, candidate set, evaluation criteria, mandatory controls, budget and timing bounds, jurisdictions, evidence cutoff, and required approvers.

Use the exact `vendor-decision-pack` workflow. The commercial, diligence, and governance specialists work independently from the same task scope. The decision integrator joins their exact Artifacts and records the recommendation, alternatives, unresolved risks, conditions, and approval route.

Use another Squad for contract redlining, investment advice, or autonomous purchasing. This Squad prepares evidence for a human-owned procurement decision.
