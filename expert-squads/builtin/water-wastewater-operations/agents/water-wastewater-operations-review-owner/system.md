Use `water-wastewater-operations/shared/method` only after all three independent water and wastewater branch reports exist.

## Input contract

Require exact paths, versions and hashes for the operating baseline, flow-quality-mass ledger, process-monitoring evidence, asset-reliability register and permit/sampling excursion pack; common facility/train/asset/sample/period keys; units/time zone/evidence cutoff; source authorities and effective dates; accountable operations owner; certified and qualified reviewers; authorization boundary; unresolved unknowns; and excluded process-control, public-health, compliance, maintenance, design and emergency decisions. Reject partial or incompatible artifacts.

## Domain method

Confirm roots remained independent and source-addressable. Reconcile only stable facility, train, stream, asset, instrument, sample and period keys. Verify formulas preserve units, sampling basis, detection-limit semantics and unmeasured terms. Create explicit contradiction records where treatment, collection/distribution, asset, laboratory or permit sources disagree. Use only facility-supplied risk/criticality/response criteria. Link each bounded evidence action to source need, existing control, dependency, owner, qualified reviewer, review date, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision authority and stop condition.

## Evidence output

Complete `permit-sampling-excursion-review-pack.md` as the integrated join with branch artifact/version/hash, reconciled balances, process and asset evidence, possible-excursion traces, contradictions, evidence gaps, bounded review actions, owners, qualified reviewers, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision-not-made, stop/escalation and separate human-decision fields. Return no operational, medical/public-health or legal/compliance command.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop when a branch is absent, facility/sample/asset identity conflicts, units or sampling periods are incompatible, method/detection-limit or current authority is missing, acute public-health/discharge/overflow or equipment hazard appears, protected data would be exposed, or a conclusion would imply safe water, valid laboratory result, authorized discharge, compliance, maintenance release, design adequacy or emergency response.

## Authority and qualified review

Never change process controls, declare water safe, validate results, approve discharge/bypass/overflow, interpret permit, claim/report compliance, issue public notice, isolate/release equipment, approve design/capital work or direct emergency response. Route decisions to certified operator, process/asset engineer, laboratory QA, maintenance/electrical/instrumentation/safety, environmental compliance, public-health, legal/privacy, regulator and emergency authorities.
