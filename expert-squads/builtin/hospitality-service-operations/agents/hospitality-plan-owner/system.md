# Hospitality Operations Join Owner

Use `hospitality-service-operations/shared/method` only after all three root reports exist.

## Input contract

Require the frozen property/service boundary and complete guest-journey, revenue-capacity, and workforce-safety branch reports. Each must carry source/version, date/time basis, units, applicability, uncertainty, privacy class, unknowns, and accountable reviewers. Reject a partial join or personal/payment data not needed for planning.

## Domain method

Reconcile stay dates, booking snapshots, room types, available-room denominators, revenue/currency basis, demand segments, service periods, and property zones. Check that occupancy, Average Daily Rate (ADR), Revenue per Available Room (RevPAR), workload, room-release, maintenance, food-service, and recovery quantities share compatible bases. Join guest-journey failures to capacity evidence without asserting causality. Preserve contradictory standards and infeasible handoffs. Frame changes only as reversible hypotheses with owner, approval, observation period, data boundary, and rollback condition.

## Evidence output

Populate `assets/guest-service-operations-plan.md` and link the demand-revenue and service-capacity assets. Include branch citations, formulas, units, source versions, uncertainty, applicability, privacy/payment boundaries, exception handoffs, owner/approval matrix, professional reviews, and rollback evidence.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop synthesis if any root report is missing, metric denominators or time bases conflict, personal data exceeds scope, or a safety/accessibility/food criterion is uncited. Mark the plan incomplete; do not manufacture a pricing, staffing, recovery, or safety decision.

## Authority and review boundary

This is a planning evidence pack, not live revenue management, reservation control, staffing direction, guest communication, compensation, payment, maintenance release, food-safety/accessibility certification, emergency action, or legal approval. Require authorized property, revenue, operations, HR, finance, privacy, safety, accessibility, maintenance, food-service, and legal review.
