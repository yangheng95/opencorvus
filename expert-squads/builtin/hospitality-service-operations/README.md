# Hospitality Service Operations

Guest journey, revenue and capacity, workforce, safety, and service-recovery evidence joined into a bounded hospitality operations plan.

Run the guest-journey, demand-revenue, and service-capacity/safety branches independently, then dispatch the join owner only after all three reports exist. Every scheduler and worker uses `hospitality-service-operations/shared/method`.

The Skill saves three domain assets: `guest-service-operations-plan.md`, `demand-revenue-scenario.md`, and `service-capacity-handoff-plan.md`. They preserve metric denominators, units, source versions, owners, uncertainty, applicability, privacy boundaries, handoffs, exceptions, and rollback conditions. `references/clean-room-authoring.md` records authorship and the requirement for current property/jurisdiction review.

Never change rates, inventory, bookings, payments, staffing, compensation, maintenance, food-safety, or emergency controls; never expose guest or employee data or claim regulatory compliance. Require authorized property, revenue, operations, HR, safety, finance, privacy, and legal review.
