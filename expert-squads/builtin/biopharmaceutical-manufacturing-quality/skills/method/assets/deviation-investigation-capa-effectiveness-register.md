# Deviation, Investigation, CAPA and Effectiveness Register

Use one `DIC-###` record for a deviation/OOS/OOT/nonconformance trigger, investigation fact/hypothesis/test, impact/extent status, CAPA action or effectiveness observation. This asset does not determine cause, invalidate results, close records, disposition product or claim compliance.

Canonical fields: `record_id`, `object_ids`, `value_unit`, `source_location`, `source_authority`, `source_version`, `effective_date`, `observation_date`, `owner`, `qualified_reviewer`, `applicability`, `uncertainty`, `status`, `decision_not_made`, `stop_or_escalation`.

## Required evidence

- Site/product/process/batch/lot/material/equipment and deviation/OOS/OOT/CAPA IDs.
- Trigger/problem statement tied to objective evidence and approved specification/procedure/method source/version.
- Immutable chronology with fact, contemporaneous assessment, hypothesis, test, counterevidence and approved conclusion kept distinct.
- Containment/status action only as already authorized, with owner/time/source; no instruction.
- Investigation plan/team authority and selected cause-analysis method; no auto-routing or fixed severity/recurrence threshold.
- Impact/extent-of-condition status as supplied, affected genealogy and unresolved alternatives.
- CAPA action, targeted cause/control, owner, due date, change/training/validation dependencies and implementation evidence.
- Predeclared effectiveness measure, denominator/window/criterion from approved source, observation/result/unit and uncertainty.
- Source/version/effective/observation dates, owner, Quality Unit/qualified reviewer, applicability and status.
- Decision not made: no cause/impact, lab invalidation, closure, disposition/release, recall/reporting or compliance decision.
- Stop/escalation: identity/spec conflict, unresolved lab phase, altered evidence, active product/patient/sterility concern or missing authority.

## DIC-001 baseline

- Trigger/object IDs and chronology: unknown
- Specification/procedure/method: unknown
- Hypotheses/tests/counterevidence: unknown
- Impact/extent status: unknown
- CAPA/action/implementation/effectiveness: unknown
- Owner/qualified reviewer: unassigned
- Applicability/uncertainty/status: unknown / draft
- Decision not made: no deviation, OOS/OOT, cause, CAPA, batch, recall or GMP decision
- Stop/escalation: Quality Unit and qualified technical authorities must establish current evidence

Action completion is not effectiveness or closure. Preserve unresolved causes and avoid ending with unsupported “human error.”
