Use `biopharmaceutical-manufacturing-quality/shared/method` only after all three independent biopharma branch reports exist.

## Input contract

Require exact paths, versions and hashes for the batch/genealogy ledger, deviation/CAPA register, validation/control-strategy matrix and continued-verification review; common site/product/process/batch/lot/material/equipment/deviation/CAPA/validation keys; master/executed/specification/method/system versions and dates; evidence cutoff/time zone; data/privacy boundary; accountable Manufacturing/Quality owner; Quality Unit/Qualified Person and qualified reviewers; unresolved unknowns; and excluded record/change/closure/disposition/release/submission decisions. Reject incomplete or incompatible branches.

## Domain method

Confirm roots remained independent and source-addressable. Reconcile only stable IDs and compatible product/process/record/method versions. Create contradictions for batch entries, genealogy, investigation, CAPA, laboratory, validation or trend evidence that disagree. Use only approved specifications, CQA/CPP/control strategy, sampling/statistical and Quality Unit criteria; never invent threshold or decision. Link bounded evidence actions to source need, existing control, deviation/change, dependency, owner, qualified reviewer, review date, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision authority and stop condition.

## Evidence output

Complete `biopharma-manufacturing-quality-review-pack.md` with branch versions/hashes, joined findings, genealogy gaps, deviations/investigations/CAPA/effectiveness, validation/CPV traces, contradictions, bounded review actions, owners, qualified reviewers, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision-not-made, stop/escalation and separate human-decision fields.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop when a branch is absent, IDs/record/process versions conflict, audit trail/current approved specification is missing, laboratory status is unresolved, active contamination/sterility/product-quality concern appears, or conclusion would imply data-integrity breach, root cause/impact, deviation/CAPA closure, validation success, batch/equipment/area disposition/release, reportability, submission or GMP compliance.

## Authority and qualified review

Never alter records/parameters, validate results, determine cause/impact/disposition, close deviation/OOS/OOT/CAPA, approve validation/change, release equipment/area/batch, claim product quality/sterility/GMP, submit to authority or advise patients. Route decisions to Manufacturing, Quality Unit/Qualified Person, process/validation, laboratory QA, microbiology/contamination control, engineering/maintenance, data integrity, regulatory/legal and site leadership.
