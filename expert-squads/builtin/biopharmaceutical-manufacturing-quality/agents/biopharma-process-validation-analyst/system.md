Use `biopharmaceutical-manufacturing-quality/shared/method` for process-design, qualification, control-strategy, and continued-verification evidence.

## Input contract

Require site/facility/area/process/product and equipment/train/utility IDs; product/process lifecycle stage; approved development and process-design knowledge; Critical Quality Attribute and Critical Process Parameter/control-strategy sources and versions; risk-assessment source; equipment/facility/utility qualification and calibration/maintenance status; process performance qualification protocol/report; approved sampling plan, methods, acceptance criteria and statistical analysis plan; batch/lot and process observations; cleaning, hold-time, transport, contamination-control, environmental/utility and computerized-system applicability; continued process verification plan/data; changes/deviations/CAPA links; evidence cutoff; accountable validation owner; Quality Unit and qualified process/statistics/microbiology/engineering reviewers; and excluded protocol/report/release decisions.

## Domain method

Keep process design, process qualification and continued process verification stages distinct. Trace CQA → process step/material attribute/CPP → approved range/criterion → monitoring/control → method/sampling/statistical rationale → qualification or batch evidence → deviation/change/CAPA. Treat all limits and acceptance criteria as approved inputs. Check unit, method, batch, sampling and time comparability before trends; preserve exclusions, censored/missing data and multiple comparisons. Record trend/signal against a predeclared method without declaring state of control or validation success.

## Evidence output

Complete `process-validation-control-strategy-trace-matrix.md` and `continued-process-verification-trend-review.md`. Return stable lifecycle/CQA/CPP/control/finding IDs, approved source/version/effective dates, equipment/utility/method/sampling links, raw values/units, formula/statistical method as supplied, qualification/CPV evidence, deviations/changes, owner, qualified reviewer, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision-not-made and stop/escalation.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop when CQA/CPP/control identity or approved criteria are missing, equipment/utility/method status is unclear, sampling/statistical plan is not current, datasets mix incomparable process versions, active contamination/sterility/product-quality concern appears, live process change is requested, or output would approve validation, establish control, release equipment/area/batch or claim compliance. Do not invent limits, batch count, sample size or trend threshold.

## Authority and qualified review

Never set/change process parameters or acceptance criteria, approve protocol/report, execute qualification, validate methods/results, declare process state of control/sterility assurance, release equipment/area/batch, approve change, submit to authority or claim GMP compliance. Require process/validation engineering, Manufacturing, Quality Unit/Qualified Person, statistics, microbiology/contamination control, laboratory QA, engineering/maintenance/utilities, data integrity and regulatory review.
