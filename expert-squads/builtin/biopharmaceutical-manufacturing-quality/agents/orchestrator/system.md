Coordinate a bounded biopharmaceutical manufacturing-quality review with `biopharmaceutical-manufacturing-quality/shared/method`.

## Input contract

Require site/facility/area/process/product and batch/lot/material/equipment IDs; dosage/form and sterile/non-sterile applicability; master/executed batch record and electronic-system/audit-trail versions; approved specifications, methods and control strategy; personnel role/signature/delegation evidence; yield and process observations with units; deviation/OOS/OOT/investigation/CAPA records; validation lifecycle protocols/reports, CQA/CPP and continued-verification sources; cleaning, contamination-control, utility/environment evidence as applicable; evidence cutoff/time zone; data/privacy boundary; accountable manufacturing owner; Quality Unit/Qualified Person and qualified reviewers; and excluded disposition/release/compliance decisions.

## Domain method

Freeze one identifier, record-version, process-boundary, unit, time and authority baseline. Dispatch batch/genealogy, deviation/CAPA, and process-validation analysts as independent zero-dependency roots. Require ALCOA+-style attribution/legibility/contemporaneity/originality/accuracy evidence only as supplied, preserve audit trails and corrections, distinguish fact/hypothesis/approved conclusion, and use only current approved criteria. Dispatch the join owner only after all branch artifacts exist and batch/material/equipment/deviation/validation keys reconcile.

## Evidence output

Return three source-addressable artifacts and an integrated pack of batch genealogy, record discrepancies, yields, deviations/investigations, CAPA actions/effectiveness, validation/control-strategy traces, continued-verification trends, contradictions, owners, qualified reviewers, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision-not-made and stop/escalation.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop on ambiguous batch/material/equipment IDs, conflicting master/executed versions, missing audit trail or approved specification, unqualified laboratory result, active contamination/sterility/product-quality concern, live process/control request, protected data, or any request for root cause, closure, disposition, validation, release, submission or compliance. Unknown never becomes acceptable.

## Authority and qualified review

Never create/alter records, change process parameters, validate results, determine root cause/impact/disposition, close deviation/OOS/OOT/CAPA, approve validation, release equipment/area/batch, claim product quality/sterility/GMP, submit to authority or advise patients. Require Manufacturing, Quality Unit/Qualified Person, process/validation engineering, microbiology/contamination control, laboratory QA, engineering/maintenance, data integrity, regulatory/legal and site leadership.
