Use `biopharmaceutical-manufacturing-quality/shared/method` for the deviation, OOS/OOT, investigation, impact, CAPA, and effectiveness branch.

## Input contract

Require site/product/process/batch/lot/material/equipment IDs; deviation, nonconformance, OOS/OOT, complaint/recall or audit trigger IDs as applicable; event chronology and source records; approved specification/method/procedure versions; containment/status actions already authorized; affected batches/materials/processes/systems; investigation plan/team/delegation; hypotheses, tests, interviews and objective evidence; laboratory investigation status supplied by laboratory authority; impact/extent-of-condition records; CAPA action/owner/due date/change-control/training/validation links; effectiveness protocol/measure/window/evidence; evidence cutoff; accountable Quality Unit owner; qualified process/lab/engineering/medical/regulatory reviewers; and excluded root-cause/closure/disposition decisions.

## Domain method

Define the problem from objective evidence and build an immutable chronology. Separate observed fact, supplied preliminary assessment, hypothesis, test, counterevidence and approved conclusion. Use any root-cause tool only when the authorized investigation plan selects it; never use an automatic decision tree or fixed recurrence/severity threshold. Preserve multiple contributing causes and unresolved alternatives. Trace action → targeted cause/control → implementation/change/training/validation evidence → predeclared effectiveness measure/window → observed result/uncertainty. Do not close a CAPA because an action completed.

## Evidence output

Complete `deviation-investigation-capa-effectiveness-register.md`. Return stable deviation/CAPA/finding IDs, affected genealogy, chronology, specification/procedure sources and versions, containment as supplied, hypotheses/tests/counterevidence, extent/impact status as supplied, action/owner/dates, effectiveness protocol/results, qualified reviewer, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision-not-made and stop/escalation.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop when trigger/batch/specification identity conflicts, OOS/OOT laboratory phase is unresolved, evidence is altered or unavailable, investigation authority is missing, patient/product/sterility/contamination risk requires authorized escalation, planned effectiveness measure/window is absent, or output would determine cause, impact, disposition, reportability, closure, recall or compliance. Do not blame personnel or replace evidence with “human error.”

## Authority and qualified review

Never determine final root cause/impact, invalidate a laboratory result, approve containment, create/change/close deviation/OOS/OOT/CAPA, approve change/training/validation, disposition/release product, decide recall/reporting, submit to authority or claim GMP compliance. Require Quality Unit/Qualified Person, Manufacturing, process/validation engineering, laboratory QA, microbiology/medical safety as applicable, regulatory/legal and site leadership.
