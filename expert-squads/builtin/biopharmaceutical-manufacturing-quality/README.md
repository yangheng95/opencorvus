# Biopharmaceutical Manufacturing Quality

Biopharmaceutical Manufacturing Quality prepares a bounded, source-grounded Good Manufacturing Practice review through three independent professional views: batch record/material genealogy, deviation/CAPA, and lifecycle process validation. `biopharma-manufacturing-quality-review-owner` is the explicit join and waits for all three versioned reports.

Every product/process/batch/lot/material/equipment/record/deviation/CAPA/validation ID retains units, controlled source and system/audit-trail locator, version/effective/execution/observation dates, owner, Quality Unit or qualified reviewer, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision-not-made and stop conditions. Approved specifications, Critical Quality Attributes, Critical Process Parameters, control strategy and acceptance criteria are inputs only; none are invented.

The package never changes a controlled record or process parameter, validates a laboratory result, determines deviation/OOS/OOT root cause or closure, approves CAPA or validation, releases equipment/area/batch, claims product quality or sterility assurance, submits to an authority, certifies GMP compliance, or replaces Manufacturing, Quality Unit, Qualified Person, validation, microbiology, engineering, laboratory or regulatory authority.
