# Tax Compliance method

Fix legal entities, jurisdictions, reporting framework, periods, transactions, currencies, taxes, materiality, data cutoff, and requested decisions before research. Separate source records, accounting treatment, book-to-tax differences, tax positions, calculations, assumptions, estimates, and unknowns.

Prefer official tax legislation, finance-ministry material, tax-authority notices, accounting standards, filing instructions, and other authoritative records. Every rate, threshold, deadline, treatment, and conclusion records jurisdiction, effective period, as-of date, official Uniform Resource Locator (URL), and limitation. Never hard-code or invent a rate, deadline, account balance, invoice, or filing status.

Accounting analysis covers recognition, measurement, presentation, journal-entry logic, reconciliations, invoice and document chains, and controls. Tax analysis covers registration, taxable events, tax base, rates, indirect tax, corporate income tax, withholding, deductions, credits, filing, payment, and evidence retention only where applicable. Calculations state inputs, units, currency, formula, rounding, and source.

The compliance plan prioritizes risk, remediation, filing calendar, evidence retention, owners, due dates, and residual exposure. The finished work is compliance research and planning, not a substitute for review by qualified accounting and tax professionals.
