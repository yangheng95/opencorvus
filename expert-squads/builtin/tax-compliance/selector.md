# Tax Compliance selection

Select `tax-compliance-assessment` only for a bounded entity- and period-specific accounting and tax compliance matter with identifiable jurisdictions, reporting framework, transactions, records, and explicit data gaps. Preserve all supplied currencies, units, periods, source restrictions, and decision deadlines.

Describe only the current useful responsibilities and actual dependencies. Reference nodes are optional; accounting controls and tax obligations are an example of independent parallel work. Do not select this Squad for generic tax education without an entity-specific compliance plan or when the entity, jurisdiction, or period is absent and cannot be recorded as an explicit unknown.

The final delivery must retain the qualified-professional disclaimer and distinguish supplied fact, accounting treatment, tax treatment, calculation, assumption, estimate, unknown, remediation judgment, and residual exposure.
