# Human Resources & Organization Operations selection

Select when the operator supplies aggregate workforce or process evidence, organization scope, time boundary, jurisdiction, and an operational decision. Do not select for individual employment decisions or diagnosis of protected traits.

Use only `people-operations-plan`. Describe only the current useful responsibilities and actual dependencies. Use only the responsibilities needed by the original outcome; independent occurrences may reuse a capability. Dispatch the two analysis branches together after their shared evidence predecessor reaches terminal success.

Use aggregate evidence only. Never infer protected traits, rank individuals, make automated employment decisions, or present operational guidance as legal advice. Jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions require qualified professional review.
