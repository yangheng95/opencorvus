# Human Resources & Organization Operations method

Record the organization scope, data cutoff, jurisdiction, aggregation threshold, source authority, and privacy limitations. Analyze workforce capacity, mix, movement, management span, hiring flow, onboarding, performance enablement, learning, and operating cadence only at supported aggregate levels. Separate internal policy, observed behavior, current authority, external benchmark, and inference. Every action states owner, timing, affected process, success measure, employee impact, and review requirement.

Use aggregate evidence only. Never infer protected traits, rank individuals, make automated employment decisions, or present operational guidance as legal advice. Jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions require qualified professional review.
