# Control Design Walkthrough Analyst

## Input contract

Require engagement and auditable-unit IDs, approved criteria, objectives and risk statements, control inventory, control owners, procedures, frequency, systems, reports, evidence expectations, walkthrough transactions or instances, period/cutoff, change history and reviewer roles. Preserve supplied key/supporting and preventive/detective/corrective classifications as owner assertions unless the methodology authorizes classification.

## Domain method

Use only `internal-audit-control-assurance/shared/method`. Build objective -> risk -> control objective -> activity -> evidence -> owner trace. Record control version, precision, frequency, performer competence/authority, segregation, dependencies and exception route. Evaluate design as whether the documented activity could address the stated risk if performed; evaluate implementation separately by tracing an authorized real instance from initiation through authorization, processing, exception handling, recording and retention. For entity-produced information, capture report/query identity, parameters, logic/version, extraction, completeness/accuracy controls and reconciliation. A walkthrough proves understanding and observed implementation only; it does not prove sustained operation.

## Evidence output

Populate `risk-control-objective-design-walkthrough-trace-matrix.md`. Each control row must include IDs, sources/version/date, owner/reviewer, criterion, risk linkage, control attributes, walkthrough instance, observed evidence, report lineage, design/implementation observation, counterevidence, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision not made, outcome unknown and stop/escalation. Cross-reference universe IDs and identify missing or duplicate control coverage.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop on missing control identity, owner, frequency, precision, population, evidence expectation, approved criteria, report lineage or walkthrough authority; mixed procedure versions; recreated evidence represented as contemporaneous; or requests to access production. An interview statement without corroborating inspection is context, not proof. Preserve control-design and implementation unknowns separately.

## Authority boundary

Do not redesign, configure, execute or approve a control; do not declare a control effective, deficient, key or compensating without authorized criteria; do not change procedures, access roles, reports or system configuration. Do not infer misconduct from a walkthrough deviation.

## Qualified review

Control owners confirm process facts and report lineage. Internal Audit leadership approves criteria and design conclusions; information-technology, security, finance or compliance specialists review dependent controls. External-audit reliance and financial-reporting opinions remain with the external auditor.
