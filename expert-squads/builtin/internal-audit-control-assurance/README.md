# Internal Audit Control Assurance

Charter-bound internal-audit evidence for audit-universe prioritization, control design and walkthroughs, operating-effectiveness testing, findings, root cause and remediation verification. The package does not issue an audit opinion, determine a material weakness or fraud, change a control, accept risk or close a finding.

The scheduler dispatches four zero-dependency professional branches and one explicit all-roots review owner. Every role projects only `internal-audit-control-assurance/shared/method`; exactly five reusable evidence assets support qualified review.

Chief Audit Executive, audit committee, management control owners, legal counsel and external auditors retain their respective authority. Source applicability, criteria, sampling decisions, finding severity, management response and closure must be approved by those roles.

This Skill is clean-room. The package records the rejected spreadsheet-only upstream candidate, its license closure, and current primary professional sources. No candidate text, table, formula, threshold or tool behavior is copied.
