# Selection guidance

Select for an authorized internal-audit engagement that needs audit-universe and risk-priority evidence, risk-control-objective mapping, control design and walkthrough records, population/sample and operating-effectiveness testing, exceptions, findings, root-cause analysis, remediation or closure evidence.

Require the engagement charter or mandate, entity/process/system perimeter, period and cutoff, criteria/framework sources, audit owner, control owners, evidence inventory, confidentiality restrictions and named qualified reviewers. Treat missing independence, source authority, population completeness, criteria or access authority as a stop condition.

Do not select for finance close, forensic investigation, external audit opinion, cybersecurity certification, control operation, production-system access, fraud/legal conclusions, risk acceptance or management decisions.
