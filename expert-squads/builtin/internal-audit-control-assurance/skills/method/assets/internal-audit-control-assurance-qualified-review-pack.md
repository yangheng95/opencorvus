# Internal Audit Control Assurance Qualified Review Pack

This pack reconciles the authorized audit universe with control design, walkthrough, operating effectiveness, finding, root-cause, and remediation evidence.

## Reusable evidence contract markers

- artifact_id: stable artifact or row identity
- source_id_locator: exact authoritative source locator
- source_version_date: immutable source version and applicable date
- qualified_reviewer: named discipline reviewer with decision authority
- units_and_denominator: value, unit, currency, population and denominator or not-applicable rationale
- assumptions_uncertainty: authorized assumptions, unknowns, confidence and reason
- decision_not_made: explicit professional decisions this artifact does not make
- outcome_unknown: unresolved outcome stated without inference
- stop_escalation: exact hold point, reason, escalation owner and required review

## Cross-branch reconciliation

Reconcile auditable-unit totals, included/excluded scope, objectives and risks, control IDs and versions, walkthrough coverage, population totals, selected samples, results, exception-to-finding links, remediation versions and closure evidence. Record each mismatch with source locators, owners, competing explanations, impact on coverage and next evidence. Do not average incompatible periods or select a favorable record.

## Decisions intentionally reserved

List audit-plan changes, scope-limit acceptance, sampling/extrapolation, finding language and severity, fraud/legal referral, risk acceptance, management response, external-audit reliance, final communication and closure. For each, name the authorized human role, evidence needed and due-date source. Package completion is not assurance.

## Reviewer sign-off record

Record reviewer name/role, authority source, review date, exact artifact versions reviewed, decision, conditions, rejected conclusions and follow-up. Preserve prior versions and disagreements. Until signed by the appropriate role, retain `status: review required` and `outcome_unknown: true`.
