# Primary method sources

- Institute of Internal Auditors, 2024 Global Internal Audit Standards, effective for quality assessments beginning 2025-01-09: https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/2024-standards/global-internal-audit-standards/ . Apply only to internal-audit professional practice and verify the current edition and local adoption at the engagement cutoff.
- Public Company Accounting Oversight Board AS 2201, An Audit of Internal Control Over Financial Reporting That Is Integrated with An Audit of Financial Statements: https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201 . Use only when its issuer, engagement and financial-reporting scope apply; it does not convert internal-audit work into an external audit opinion.
- United States Government Accountability Office, Standards for Internal Control in the Federal Government, 2025 Revision, GAO-25-107721: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-25-107721 . Use for applicable United States federal-government control contexts or as an explicitly adopted reference, not as universal law.

Source text, thresholds and definitions are not copied into this Skill. The engagement owner must supply the currently applicable criteria, approved methodology, sampling basis, terminology and professional reviewers. Conflicts between sources remain explicit.
