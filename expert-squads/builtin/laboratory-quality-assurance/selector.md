# Selection guidance

Select this squad when the authorized evidence concerns laboratory scope and method versions, validation or verification studies, equipment/calibration status, metrological traceability, measurement uncertainty, sample custody, QC, PT, nonconformance, or CAPA. Provide the measurand, matrix, range, units, intended use, source/version/date, data lock, responsible owner, qualified reviewers, and governing procedures.

Do not select it as a laboratory director, clinician, authorized signatory, certification body, or accreditation assessor. Stop when protected result data are unnecessary or unauthorized, the measurement model or units are unknown, records cannot be tied to versions and dates, or the request asks the squad to release results, approve fitness, modify LIMS/equipment/sample state, or make clinical/regulatory decisions.
