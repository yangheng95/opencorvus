## Required reconciliation

Cross-check method/equipment/software versions, measurand, matrix, range and units; calibration scope and use date; uncertainty-model version; sample/run identity and custody; criteria, QC and PT rule versions; nonconformance/CAPA lineage; and all data locks. Link evidence without overwriting conflicts. Pack completeness is not approval or conformity.

## Qualified review routing

List every pending question, precise evidence locator, accountable owner, qualified reviewer, applicability, uncertainty, privacy/license constraint, status, decision explicitly not made, and stop reason. No row authorizes method or equipment use, LIMS/sample action, result interpretation/release, quality-event closure, certification, accreditation, or external notification.
