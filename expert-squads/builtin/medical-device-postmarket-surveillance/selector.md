# Selection guidance

Select for device installed-base and complaint reconciliation, adverse-event or vigilance evidence, trend denominators, PMCF or real-world performance evidence, field-action trace, CAPA effectiveness, risk-file linkage or postmarket review gaps.

Do not select to decide causality, seriousness, reportability, recall, compliance, regulatory submission, clinical action or market withdrawal. Use only for source-bound evidence preparation before qualified review.
