# Medical Device Postmarket Surveillance

Complaint, installed-base, device-event, vigilance, trend, Post-Market Clinical Follow-up (PMCF), Real-World Evidence (RWE), field-action, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) and risk-file evidence without reportability, recall or compliance authority.

The scheduler dispatches four independent professional branches, then one explicit all-roots join preserves conflicts and produces a qualified-review pack. Every scheduler and worker projects only `medical-device-postmarket-surveillance/shared/method`.

- Do not close or recode a complaint, decide causality/seriousness/reportability, diagnose or treat, contact patients or regulators, submit reports, initiate a recall or field action, or approve CAPA/risk acceptance.
- Do not hard-code reporting clocks, trend thresholds or jurisdictional conclusions; bind every requirement to the operator-supplied current source.
- Complaint/vigilance specialists, medical safety, quality/CAPA, risk, regulatory, privacy/legal and manufacturer authorities retain decisions.

The method is a bounded MIT adaptation with clean-room evidence governance. Exact provenance, license and exclusions are saved under `skills/method/references/`; the five assets are evidence structures, not completed decisions.
