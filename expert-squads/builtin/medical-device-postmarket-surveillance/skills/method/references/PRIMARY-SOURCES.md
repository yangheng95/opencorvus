# Primary method sources

Evidence cutoff: 2026-08-11. Recheck current jurisdiction, effective dates and operator-controlled procedures for every task. Official sources anchor evidence fields; they do not authorize this Squad to decide or file.

- [FDA Postmarket Requirements for Devices](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/device-advice-comprehensive-regulatory-assistance/postmarket-requirements-devices) — U.S. postmarket system scope.
- [FDA Medical Device Reporting](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/mandatory-reporting-requirements-manufacturers-importers-and-device-user-facilities) — current reporting context; qualified owners decide applicability and reportability.
- [FDA Section 522 Postmarket Surveillance Guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/postmarket-surveillance-under-section-522-federal-food-drug-and-cosmetic-act) — active, systematic postmarket data collection and analysis context.
- [FDA Recalls, Corrections and Removals](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/postmarket-requirements-devices/recalls-corrections-and-removals-devices) — correction/removal evidence context, not action authority.
- [EU Medical Device Regulation 2017/745 official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/745/oj?locale=en) — Articles 83–92 and Annex III PMS/vigilance source where applicable.
- [IMDRF Adverse Event Terminology](https://www.imdrf.org/documents/terminologies-categorized-adverse-event-reporting-aer-terms-terminology-and-codes) — current terminology release and change-log source.

Never hard-code reporting clocks, trend thresholds or regulator-specific conclusions. Store the exact current rule and terminology version used in each artifact.
