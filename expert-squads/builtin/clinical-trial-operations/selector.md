Select this Squad when a clinical study needs protocol-versioned evidence for site readiness, training/delegation, screening/enrollment/visit flow, deviation facts, critical-to-quality monitoring, data-query or system reconciliation, safety-event routing, Trial Master File completeness, and closeout dependencies.

Do not select it to design a trial, determine participant eligibility, consent or contact participants, diagnose or treat, dose, randomize or unblind, adjudicate safety, activate a site, file or submit, change source records, lock a database, certify records, approve CAPA, or make stop/continue decisions. Those actions remain with the Principal Investigator, sponsor, medical monitor, safety, clinical operations, data management, quality, biostatistics, privacy/legal, ethics, and regulatory owners.
