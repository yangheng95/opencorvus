Use `clinical-trial-operations/shared/method` to prepare the safety-routing, reconciliation, Trial Master File, retention, and closeout branch.

Input contract: require study/protocol/amendment/site/jurisdiction, approved safety-management and escalation plan/version, de-identified event IDs, source system exports and timestamps/time zones, role/delegation map, approved reporting workflow and deadlines as supplied, safety/clinical database reconciliation rules, TMF reference model or sponsor index/version, expected artifacts, document metadata/signature/version status, repository audit-trail references, closeout plan, investigational-product/specimen/vendor/financial and data dependencies, retention basis, evidence cutoff, owners, and qualified reviewers.

Domain method: verify only capture, acknowledgement, routing, follow-up, reconciliation, and evidence timing against the supplied approved workflow. Keep seriousness, causality, expectedness, relatedness, reportability, and medical assessment as externally supplied decisions. Reconcile safety and clinical systems by de-identified event/subject IDs and versions. For TMF, distinguish expected, present, legible, current, signed/approved as applicable, superseded, misfiled, duplicate, restricted, and unresolved; link each artifact to process, country/site, milestone, owner, audit trail and retention requirement. Closeout status is a dependency map, not certification.

Evidence output: populate the safety routing/reconciliation log and TMF/closeout pack with event/document IDs, protocol/site/jurisdiction, workflow or index source/version/date, timestamp/time zone and duration unit, evidence status, reconciliation result, applicability, uncertainty, privacy class, audit trail, owner, qualified reviewer, escalation, retention/handoff dependency, and pending decision.

Unknown and stop: stop on suspected immediate participant risk, missing safety owner, conflicting event IDs, absent authorized workflow, unauthorized health data, broken audit trail, unclear TMF index/version, or a request to adjudicate/report, modify systems, file documents, certify completeness, destroy/retain records, or close a site/study.

Authority and qualified review: do not determine seriousness/causality/expectedness/reportability, contact a participant or authority, submit a report, certify TMF, approve retention/destruction, close a site, or decide stop/continue. Require Principal Investigator, sponsor medical monitor/safety, clinical operations, data management, Quality Assurance, TMF/records, privacy/legal, regulatory/ethics, pharmacy/laboratory/vendor, and accountable closeout owner.
