Use `clinical-trial-operations/shared/method` to prepare the critical-to-quality, data-quality, query, reconciliation, and risk-based monitoring branch.

Input contract: require study/protocol/amendment/site and jurisdiction scope, approved critical-to-quality factors, risk and monitoring plan/version, data-management plan, edit-check/query rules, de-identified system exports and audit-trail references, source/eCRF/laboratory/vendor/safety/IRT and other reconciliation definitions, expected data and milestone dates, monitoring reports and follow-up evidence, units/time zone, evidence cutoff, data classification, owners, and qualified reviewers. Do not request unrestricted source records.

Domain method: trace each critical-to-quality factor to failure mode, participant protection or result-reliability impact, detection source, monitoring method/frequency supplied by the plan, signal, threshold only if current and approved, issue, mitigation owner, and review status. Quantify missingness and query aging with explicit denominators, timestamps and units. Reconcile system pairs by stable event/subject/specimen IDs and version; distinguish mismatch, pending update, duplicate, late data, transformation, and unresolved audit-trail question. Monitoring prioritization remains a documented hypothesis for sponsor approval.

Evidence output: populate the risk-based monitoring/data-quality plan with factor/risk/signal/query/reconciliation IDs, protocol/site/system scope, metric numerator/denominator/unit/window, source/version/date, monitoring evidence, finding and counterevidence, applicability, uncertainty, audit trail, owner, qualified reviewer, escalation, action hypothesis, and decision status.

Unknown and stop: stop on unauthorized participant data, missing audit trail, ambiguous system version, incomparable timestamps or units, unapproved threshold, unresolved immediate participant-protection concern, or any request to edit source/eCRF/vendor data, close a query without evidence, change a monitoring plan, or certify compliance.

Authority and qualified review: do not perform source-data verification claims, issue/close queries in live systems, approve monitoring changes, accept risk, close CAPA, lock a database, or claim data integrity. Require sponsor oversight, monitor, data management, biostatistics, medical/safety, Quality Assurance, privacy/security, vendor/system owner, and Principal Investigator/site review as applicable.
