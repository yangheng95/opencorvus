Use `clinical-trial-operations/shared/method` to prepare the screening, enrollment, visit, discontinuation, and deviation-fact branch.

Input contract: require study/protocol/amendment/site IDs and effective periods, authorized de-identified participant/subject IDs, screening/enrollment/randomization/completion/withdrawal definitions, approved eligibility and visit-schedule rule locations, visit-window units/time zone, screening/consent/enrollment/visit/disposition exports with system versions and dates, deviation process and categories supplied by the sponsor, evidence cutoff, privacy classification, owner, escalation route, and qualified reviewers. Do not inspect direct identifiers or decide eligibility.

Domain method: reconcile each subject's operational events only to the protocol/amendment effective for that site and date. Keep screened, consented, enrolled, randomized, treated, completed, discontinued, withdrawn, and lost-to-follow-up counts distinct under supplied definitions. Calculate rates with explicit numerator, denominator, window and missingness. Determine visit status only from the supplied protocol window and exact event timestamp. Record a potential deviation as facts, rule location, impact question, evidence, owner, and pending classification; preserve amendment crossover and system corrections.

Evidence output: populate the enrollment/visit/deviation ledger with stable event IDs, de-identified subject/site, protocol/amendment, event timestamp/time zone, status, numerator/denominator and unit, rule source/version/date, deviation fact and evidence, applicability, uncertainty, privacy class, audit-trail reference, owner, qualified reviewer, escalation, and decision status.

Unknown and stop: stop on missing consent/data authority, direct identifiers, conflicting subject/site IDs, uncertain protocol effective date, ambiguous visit window, unreliable export, suspected immediate safety issue, or any request to determine eligibility, obtain consent, contact a participant, randomize/unblind, waive/classify a deviation, or change a record.

Authority and qualified review: do not make participant, medical, protocol, or regulatory decisions. Require Principal Investigator/site staff, sponsor clinical operations, medical monitor where needed, data management, biostatistics for denominators, monitor, privacy, Quality Assurance, and authorized deviation owner.
