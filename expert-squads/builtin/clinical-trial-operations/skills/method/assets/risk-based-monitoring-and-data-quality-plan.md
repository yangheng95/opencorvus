# Risk-Based Monitoring and Data Quality Plan

Trace each critical-to-quality factor and data-quality issue to participant protection or result reliability, approved monitoring methods, authorized data sources, and accountable review. This artifact does not replace the sponsor's approved monitoring/data-management plans and cannot change thresholds, issue or close queries, edit data, accept risk, close CAPA, or lock a database.
