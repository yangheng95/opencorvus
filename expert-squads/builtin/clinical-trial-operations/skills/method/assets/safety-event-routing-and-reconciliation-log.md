# Safety Event Routing and Reconciliation Log

Check capture, acknowledgement, routing, follow-up, timing, and system reconciliation only against the supplied approved safety workflow. Seriousness, causality, expectedness, relatedness, reportability, medical assessment, deadline applicability, and regulatory action remain externally supplied qualified decisions.

- Log ID / version / status:

- De-identification, privacy, and access classification:
- Responsible safety owner / medical monitor / Principal Investigator / clinical-operations / data-management / privacy / regulatory reviewers:
- Applicable event domain, excluded actions, unresolved system differences, and overall uncertainty:

## Immediate stop record

If evidence suggests immediate participant risk or the authorized safety owner/escalation route is absent, stop analysis and notify the named human owner through the approved internal route. Do not contact participants or authorities, determine reporting, submit a report, change a system, or provide medical instructions.
