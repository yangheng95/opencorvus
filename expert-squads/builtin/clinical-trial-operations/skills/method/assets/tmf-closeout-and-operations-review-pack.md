# TMF Closeout and Operations Review Pack

Compare the approved Trial Master File (TMF) index and closeout plan with traceable evidence. Presence is not currency, approval, applicability, certification, retention authorization, site closeout, database lock, or study closure. Preserve superseded records, audit trail, restrictions, and unresolved contradictions.

- Pack ID / version / status:

- TMF reference model or sponsor index/version/effective date:
- Approved closeout, retention, data-management, investigational-product, specimen, vendor and financial plan versions:
- Repository/system/export versions, audit-trail scope, evidence cutoff/time zone:
- Count, duration, storage, or retention units:
- Privacy, access, legal-hold and restricted-document classification:
- Responsible TMF/records owner / clinical operations / data management / Quality Assurance / regulatory/ethics / privacy/legal / site reviewers:
- Applicable process/milestone, exclusions, known conflicts, and overall uncertainty:
