# Clinical Trial Operations

Clinical Trial Operations prepares a bounded, protocol-versioned operations review through four independent professional views: startup/site readiness, enrollment/conduct, data quality/monitoring, and safety/Trial Master File/closeout. `clinical-trial-operations-review-owner` is the explicit join node and waits for all four branches.

Every row retains study, protocol/amendment, site, jurisdiction, system-of-record, role/delegation, source/version/date, time zone and unit, responsible owner, qualified reviewer, applicability, uncertainty, audit-trail status, and decision state. Participant-flow denominators, visit-window facts, critical-to-quality risks, data queries, safety routing, and essential-document status remain traceable to approved inputs and current authority.

The package never determines eligibility, obtains consent, treats or doses, randomizes or unblinds, contacts participants, adjudicates safety reporting, activates a site, submits to an authority or ethics body, changes source data, locks a database, certifies a Trial Master File, approves Corrective and Preventive Action, or decides whether a study stops or continues.
