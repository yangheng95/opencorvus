# Adaptation and official sources

Local modifications require exact jurisdiction/date/source versions, goods configuration, candidate-versus-final labels, BOM/process provenance, valuation basis, potential-match uncertainty, broker-entry reconciliation, contradictions and human authority stops.

- WCO Harmonized System: https://www.wcoomd.org/en/topics/nomenclature/overview/what-is-the-harmonized-system.aspx
- WTO Customs Valuation: https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/cusval_e/cusval_e.htm
- WTO Rules of Origin: https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/roi_e/roi_e.htm
- US CBP rulings: https://www.cbp.gov/trade/rulings
- EU Common Customs Tariff: https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/customs/common-customs-tariff-cct_en

No protected WCO text or upstream numeric value is copied. Current official and operator-controlled sources govern.
