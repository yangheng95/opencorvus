# Origin, valuation, preference and duty evidence ledger

## Control contract

- Template ID: `CTC-OV-TEMPLATE-001`
- Source/version/date/cutoff: exact tariff, nomenclature, origin rule, valuation source, list, ruling, document, broker or entry version and effective date.
- Quantity/unit/value basis: goods quantity/unit, price/value/currency/exchange basis and rates only if supplied by authority.
- Owner/reviewer: transaction/data owner, IOR/EOR, licensed broker, trade specialist and counsel.
- Applicability: goods/part/configuration, transaction/shipment/entry, parties, origin/destination, jurisdiction and date.
- Assumptions/uncertainty: product facts, supplier evidence, candidate code, origin, valuation, match and document limits.
- Privacy/license: party and protected tariff/ruling access.
- Status/decision not made: no classification, origin, value, duty, preference, sanctions/licence, filing or hold/release decision.
- Stop: missing goods, source, jurisdiction/date, role, document, authority or material reconciliation.

## Completion and review

Use separate rows for distinct goods/configurations, transactions, parties, documents, candidate classifications, origin rules, valuation inputs, screening matches, licences and entry fields. Reconcile descriptions, quantities, units, values, currency, origin and code claims across commercial, transport, broker and declaration records.

Preserve considered/rejected candidates and conflicting sources. A table does not assign classification, origin, value or legal status. The owner attests only provenance and completeness; IOR/EOR, broker, specialists, counsel and authorities decide and file. Never contact an external party or modify a production record.
