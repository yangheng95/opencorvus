# Selector

Select for a bounded cross-border transaction requiring goods/party/document, classification-candidate, origin, valuation, preference, restricted-party, licence, broker or customs-entry evidence.

Require exact goods/configuration, parties/roles, jurisdictions/dates, tariff/rule/list versions, documents, owner and broker/trade/counsel reviewers. Stop if those are absent.

Do not select for logistics dispatch, port operations, legal advice, final classification/origin/value/duty/licence, filing, hold/release or authority contact.
