# Selection guidance

Select for FAFSA/ISIR data provenance, verification documents, academic/program/calendar inputs, cost of attendance, packaging, award, disbursement, reconciliation, satisfactory academic progress, return/withdrawal or overaward evidence. Do not select to determine eligibility, change records, originate/disburse/return funds, contact students or make compliance conclusions.

Use for controlled financial-aid administration evidence prepared for authorized institutional review.
