Never calculate or normalize S/E/C or ASIL. Preserve the organization's exact classifications, rationales and scope. Similar names are not proof that a goal applies to a new variant. This matrix surfaces trace gaps only; the functional-safety manager and independent assessor, supported by system, human-factors, test, cyber and regulatory experts, make professional classifications and approve goals.
