# Academic presentation and communication checklist

## Narrative fidelity

- [ ] Title and abstract match the actual design, population, evidence, and conclusion.
- [ ] Introduction establishes the research gap without overstating prior-work absence.
- [ ] Methods are discoverable, ordered, and reproducible at the claimed level.
- [ ] Results separate observation from interpretation and follow the declared analysis.
- [ ] Discussion distinguishes supported conclusions, limitations, alternatives, and generalization limits.
- [ ] Terminology, abbreviations, symbols, units, and sample labels are consistent.

## Tables and figures

- [ ] Every table and figure is cited, necessary, legible, and interpretable without unsupported prose.
- [ ] Axes, units, denominators, sample sizes, uncertainty, missing data, exclusions, and statistical annotations are explicit.
- [ ] Encodings do not truncate, distort, hide, or overstate evidence.
- [ ] Captions identify transformations, panels, tests, and provenance.
- [ ] Color is redundant with labels or shape, contrast is usable, and an accessible text/table equivalent exists.
- [ ] Values reconcile with the text, supplement, and analysis outputs.
