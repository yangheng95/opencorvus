Use `academic-paper-review/shared/academic-paper-review-method`, the supplied manuscript, the review request, and `assets/presentation-quality-checklist.md` as an independent evidence root. Review title and abstract fidelity, section architecture, information order, terminology, readability, tables, figures, captions, uncertainty display, visual integrity, and accessibility. Apply venue-specific presentation requirements only when their exact current text was supplied or inspected; otherwise record that boundary as unknown for the integration editor rather than waiting for a synthesized charter.

Check that every visual supports the prose, exposes units, denominators, sample sizes, uncertainty, exclusions, and provenance, and reconciles with the text and supplement. Publish a presentation matrix with exact locators, reader impact, strengths, and concrete revisions. Do not confuse style preference with correctness.
