Use `materials-failure-analysis/shared/method` only after custody/history, characterization and mechanics branches complete.

## Input contract

Require exact versions/hashes of the failed-component custody/history register, fracture-surface map, material/property ledger and load/environment/hypothesis matrix; common component/evidence/face/specimen/location/orientation/time/unit/source keys; preservation and legal constraints; evidence cutoff; all contradictions, alterations, measurement/model limitations and competing hypotheses; owner; evidence custodian; qualified materials/metallurgy/fractography/mechanical/fracture/corrosion/NDE/lab/application/legal reviewers; and excluded decisions.

## Domain method

Join by exact evidence and spatial/orientation keys. Reconcile as-received condition and alterations against every image/test; reconcile material/process/specification and specimen representativeness; reconcile fracture observations with load/environment/mechanics predictions. Preserve each hypothesis, support, counterevidence and untested discriminator. Label source fact, observation, derived calculation, interpretation, hypothesis, assumption, uncertainty and unknown. Never collapse correlation or morphology into root cause.

## Evidence output

Complete `materials-failure-analysis-qualified-review-pack.md`. Include branch hashes, evidence/custody lineage, component/specimen map, service chronology, method/source table, observation and calculation tables, contradiction/uncertainty register, competing-hypothesis matrix, open evidence requests, reviewer/authority matrix, decision-not-made and stop/escalation. Every statement cites a frozen asset row.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop for identity/custody/orientation gaps, undocumented alteration, nonrepresentative specimen, missing calibration/raw data, material/process mismatch, incompatible geometry/load/unit/model, active hazard/legal hold, unresolved competing evidence, or any request to handle/test evidence, declare cause/defect/liability, perform fitness, set repair/inspection, dispose, recall or return to service.

## Authority and qualified review

Never sign a failure analysis, choose a definitive mechanism/root cause, attribute fault, certify material or calculations, determine admissibility, fitness, rework/recall/scrap or release. The final pack is bounded evidence. Leave all cause, liability, disposition, safety and return-to-service fields to named qualified humans in authorized systems.
