# Materials Failure Analysis Qualified-review Pack

## Package identity and authority

Record `artifact_id`, package version/hash, incident/component/evidence scope, cutoff, preservation/legal-hold boundary, source locator/version/date inventory, owner, evidence custodian, qualified materials/metallurgy/fractography, mechanical/fracture/fatigue, corrosion, NDE, laboratory, design/application, safety and legal/regulatory reviewers, applicability, assumptions, uncertainty, status, `decision_not_made` and `stop_or_escalation`.

This is a join of frozen evidence, not a signed root-cause report, expert opinion, fitness assessment, disposition or release.

## Required branch closure

1. Component/history/custody register exact hash, as-received condition, spatial identity, custody/alteration and chronology gaps.
2. Fracture-surface map exact hash, raw-image provenance, location/orientation/scale, observations, artifacts and competing interpretations.
3. Material/property ledger exact hash, specimen representativeness, methods/instruments/calibration, values/units and uncertainty.
4. Load/environment/hypothesis matrix exact hash, source loads/geometry/environment, model validity, calculations, support/counterevidence and alternatives.

## Joined evidence and hypotheses

List each statement with source asset/row and classification as source fact, observation, calculation, interpretation, hypothesis, assumption, uncertainty or unknown. Create contradiction tables for identity, custody, alteration, material/process, specimen, orientation, images/tests, load/environment and model. For every hypothesis show converging and conflicting evidence from all three roots; do not force closure.

## Qualified-review and stop matrix

Record evidence/source needed, authorization required, owner and qualified reviewer. Stop/escalate for active hazard/legal hold, custody/orientation/alteration gap, missing raw data/calibration, nonrepresentative specimen, invalid model, unresolved competing evidence or a request to handle/test, attribute cause/fault, certify, perform fitness, repair, recall, scrap or release.

`decision_not_made` enumerates: no evidence admissibility, definitive origin/mechanism/root cause, material defect/conformance, liability/culpability, allowable/remaining life, fitness-for-service, repair/rework/recall/scrap or return-to-service decision was made. Human decisions stay in authorized systems.
