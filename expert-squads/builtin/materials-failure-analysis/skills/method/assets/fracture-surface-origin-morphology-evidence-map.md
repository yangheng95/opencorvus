# Fracture-surface Origin and Morphology Evidence Map

## Canonical fields

Each row records `artifact_id`, `row_id`, component/evidence/face/feature/specimen IDs, location/coordinate/orientation, source locator/version/date, file hash, imaging method/instrument/mode, calibration, magnification/scale and unit, preparation/alteration history, owner, qualified fractography/material reviewer, applicability, assumptions, uncertainty, custody/license boundary, status, `decision_not_made` and `stop_or_escalation`.

## Observation map

For every image region, record raw observation without causal language; possible origin/direction only with location, scale and basis; morphology terminology source; contamination, corrosion, rubbing, recovery or preparation artifact risk; and image/data quality. Link mating-face or serial-section evidence when available. Keep observation, interpretation and hypothesis separate.

## Competing interpretations

Create rows for overload, fatigue/progressive crack growth, creep, stress-corrosion cracking, hydrogen effects, corrosion, wear/fretting, processing/joining or another supplied mechanism. For each record expected features, observed support, counterevidence, alternative explanations and evidence still needed. Do not infer root cause from beach marks, dimples, cleavage, intergranular appearance, discoloration or any single feature.

## Representativeness and stop

Record whether the face was as-received or altered, whether the image covers the candidate origin and whether the specimen represents the failed region. Stop/escalate for lost orientation, undocumented cleaning/coating/sectioning, missing raw file/scale/calibration, ambiguous origin, imaging artifact, active hazardous evidence, or request to prepare/image/test/declare mechanism. `decision_not_made` states no definitive origin, mechanism, defect, root cause, liability, fitness or disposition decision was made.
