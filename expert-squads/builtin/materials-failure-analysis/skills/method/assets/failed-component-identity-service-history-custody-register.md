# Failed-component Identity, Service History and Custody Register

## Canonical provenance and authority

Record `artifact_id`, `row_id`, incident/scope ID, source locator/authority/version/effective and observation dates, evidence cutoff, quantity/unit/denominator where a value is recorded, owner, evidence custodian, qualified materials/application/legal reviewers, applicability/jurisdiction, assumptions, uncertainty/confidence, privacy/proprietary/license and legal-hold boundary, status, `decision_not_made` and `stop_or_escalation`.

This register is an evidence index. It does not authorize collection, cleaning, disassembly, cutting, testing, admissibility, root-cause, liability, fitness, disposition or return to service.

## Component and evidence identity

Record system/assembly/component/mating-part IDs, manufacturer/model/serial, drawing/design revision, material grade, heat/lot, supplier, manufacturing/process/heat-treatment/joining/coating/surface-finish references, installation location, coordinate convention, feature/fracture-face/fragment/specimen IDs and exact spatial/orientation relationships. Link photographs and files by hash, scale and timestamp.

## As-found/as-received and custody

Describe packaging, seal, labels, condition, contaminants/deposits/corrosion products, fracture faces, loose fragments and residual hazards using observations only. Record each custody transfer with from/to, date/time zone, purpose, seal/condition and authority. Record every documented recovery, cleaning, disassembly, sectioning, coating, etching or test alteration as a separate event; unknown alteration remains unknown.

## Design, manufacture and service chronology

Trace design/specification, material/procurement, manufacture/inspection, installation, service loads/environments, maintenance/repair/change, anomaly/event, recovery and examination. Preserve conflicting serial/lot/revision/time sources and missing records.

Stop/escalate for ambiguous identity, custody gap, lost orientation/mating relation, undocumented alteration, active hazardous residue/energy, proprietary/personal evidence outside authority, legal hold or any physical-action request. `decision_not_made` states no authenticity/admissibility, defect, misuse, cause, liability, fitness, repair, recall, scrap or release decision was made.
