# Product Video Production

This package turns verified product facts into a production-ready video brief, timed narrative, storyboard, shot list, asset ledger, accessibility plan, and acceptance checklist.

Narrative and visual planning share one frozen brief and run in parallel. The delivery reviewer joins both branches, checks every claim and timing decision, resolves contradictions, and publishes the canonical handoff.

The package does not claim that a video was rendered. Its authored Skill requires the team to name the exact granted media-generation or editing capability before attempting media production. Without that capability, the correct terminal deliverable is the reviewed handoff for a human or separately equipped production Task.
