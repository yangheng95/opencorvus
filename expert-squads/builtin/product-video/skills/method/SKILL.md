# Product Video Production Method

## Provenance

This is a clean-room OpenCorvus Skill authored for this package on 2026-08-10. No third-party Skill bytes are copied. It exists because the reviewed candidate sources did not establish a compatible, redistributable media-production Skill with the exact runtime capabilities required by this package.

## Required inputs

Freeze the product or feature, audience, target channel and aspect ratio, target duration, call to action, approved claims with sources, mandatory brand or legal constraints, available assets, deadline, and the exact media-generation or editing tools granted to the active Task. Record unknowns instead of inventing them.

## Workflow

1. The brief strategist publishes one bounded brief and numbered claim ledger. Every factual statement in later copy must reference that ledger or be labeled creative language.
2. After the brief is frozen, narrative and visual planning run independently in parallel.
3. The narrative branch produces a time-coded script, on-screen text, spoken text, alternate opening, call to action, and claim references. It checks spoken-word timing against the requested duration.
4. The visual branch produces a scene-by-scene storyboard, shot list, asset source and rights ledger, motion or transition notes, captions, contrast and audio-description needs, and production dependencies.
5. The delivery reviewer joins both predecessors, aligns every scene and line, resolves timing and claim conflicts, records remaining unknowns, and publishes one canonical production handoff plus acceptance checklist.

## Capability boundary

Before generating, editing, or rendering media, name the exact granted tool and verify that it supports the requested operation and output format. If no compatible tool is granted, stop at the production handoff. Never describe a script, storyboard, placeholder, or plan as a rendered video.

## Acceptance contract

The terminal handoff includes the frozen brief, claim ledger, time-coded script, storyboard, shot and asset ledger, rights and accessibility notes, production dependencies, validation checklist, and an explicit status of either `production-ready-handoff` or `rendered-and-verified`. The latter is valid only with inspectable generated media and tool evidence.
