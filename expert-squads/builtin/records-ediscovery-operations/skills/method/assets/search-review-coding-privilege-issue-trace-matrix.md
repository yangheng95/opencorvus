# Search, review coding, privilege flag, and issue trace matrix

## Reusable evidence contract

- artifact_id: required stable artifact or row identity
- source_id_locator: exact authoritative source, document, system export, manifest, cell, page or event locator
- source_version_date: immutable source version plus issuance, extraction, collection and effective dates as applicable
- qualified_reviewer: named discipline owner with decision authority
- units_and_denominator: value, unit, currency, population and denominator, or explicit not-applicable rationale
- assumptions_uncertainty: authorized assumptions, unknowns, confidence and reason
- decision_not_made: no legal, collection, preservation, privilege, production, disposition or compliance decision
- outcome_unknown: state unresolved completeness, authenticity, relevance, legal effect and operational outcome
- stop_escalation: exact hold point, reason, escalation owner and required reviewer

Also record owner, entity/jurisdiction/applicability, cutoff/effective date, privacy/privilege/confidentiality/security/license state, evidence status and post-cutoff changes.

- artifact_id: REDO-REVIEW-001
- owner: search and review evidence owner
- qualified_reviewer: discovery counsel, search/TAR specialist, review lead and privacy counsel
- decision_not_made: no search sufficiency, responsiveness, privilege, waiver, relevance, admissibility or legal conclusion
- outcome_unknown: recall, precision, responsive population, privilege status and issue consequence remain unknown

For search evidence record query or model ID/version, syntax, fields, stemming/fuzzy settings, date/time behavior, population snapshot, validation/control set, sampling design, hit count, unique-family count, excluded/exception count, reviewer protocol and authorized decision rules. Never invent a term, threshold, recall/precision claim or population.

For review evidence record batch, reviewer role, coding protocol/version, document/family ID, timestamp/time zone, responsiveness and issue flags as supplied, privilege basis flag/source, author/sender/recipient/date metadata, confidentiality indicator, description restrictions, redaction/withholding annotation source, conflicts, overturns, quality-control sample and reviewer disposition. A coding or privilege flag is an allegation/input, not a legal decision.

Map each issue and request to exact source text locator, candidate documents, supporting and counterevidence, missing evidence, assumptions, uncertainty and counsel question. Stop on changing indexes, absent population denominator, ambiguous query semantics, family breaks, unclear privilege/privacy or live-platform change requests.
