# Production, retention, disposition, and qualified review pack

## Reusable evidence contract

- artifact_id: required stable artifact or row identity
- source_id_locator: exact authoritative source, document, system export, manifest, cell, page or event locator
- source_version_date: immutable source version plus issuance, extraction, collection and effective dates as applicable
- qualified_reviewer: named discipline owner with decision authority
- units_and_denominator: value, unit, currency, population and denominator, or explicit not-applicable rationale
- assumptions_uncertainty: authorized assumptions, unknowns, confidence and reason
- decision_not_made: no legal, collection, preservation, privilege, production, disposition or compliance decision
- outcome_unknown: state unresolved completeness, authenticity, relevance, legal effect and operational outcome
- stop_escalation: exact hold point, reason, escalation owner and required reviewer

Also record owner, entity/jurisdiction/applicability, cutoff/effective date, privacy/privilege/confidentiality/security/license state, evidence status and post-cutoff changes.

- artifact_id: REDO-REVIEW-PACK-001
- owner: records and e-discovery review owner
- qualified_reviewer: records owner, information governance, discovery counsel, privacy/security, collection/processing and production specialists
- decision_not_made: no hold release, deletion, disposition, privilege/responsiveness/admissibility/spoliation ruling, production, transfer or legal advice
- outcome_unknown: completeness, legal sufficiency, production scope and disposition eligibility remain unknown until reviewers decide

Confirm all four roots used the same authority baseline, matter/cutoff/time-zone scope, source digests, schedules/holds, collection and processing manifests, review protocol and request/production specification. Reconcile custodian/system/document/family/collection/request/production IDs; input/output/exception counts; units/denominators; hashes; chain events; Bates ranges; formats; redaction/withholding sources; load-file relationships; encryption and transfer-manifest evidence.

The pack includes the authority baseline; custodian/source/collection ledger; processing/family exception register; search/review/privilege/request matrix; production and disposition mapping; evidence index; missing sources; post-cutoff changes; privacy/security restrictions; contradictions; reviewer questions and disposition slots. Conflicting hashes, dates, coding, privilege flags, scope and authority sources remain separate.

A disposition records reviewer identity/discipline, date, source version, accepted/rejected/returned state, rationale locator and follow-up owner. Stop if any root is absent, manifests or families do not reconcile, authority/privilege is disputed, or live action/legal judgment is required. Completion means ready for review, never authorization to collect, produce, delete or release.
