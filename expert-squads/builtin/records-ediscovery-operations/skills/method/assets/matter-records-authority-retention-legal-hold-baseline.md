# Matter, records authority, retention, and legal-hold baseline

## Reusable evidence contract

- artifact_id: required stable artifact or row identity
- source_id_locator: exact authoritative source, document, system export, manifest, cell, page or event locator
- source_version_date: immutable source version plus issuance, extraction, collection and effective dates as applicable
- qualified_reviewer: named discipline owner with decision authority
- units_and_denominator: value, unit, currency, population and denominator, or explicit not-applicable rationale
- assumptions_uncertainty: authorized assumptions, unknowns, confidence and reason
- decision_not_made: no legal, collection, preservation, privilege, production, disposition or compliance decision
- outcome_unknown: state unresolved completeness, authenticity, relevance, legal effect and operational outcome
- stop_escalation: exact hold point, reason, escalation owner and required reviewer

Also record owner, entity/jurisdiction/applicability, cutoff/effective date, privacy/privilege/confidentiality/security/license state, evidence status and post-cutoff changes.

- artifact_id: REDO-BASE-001
- owner: records and information-governance coordinator
- qualified_reviewer: records owner and litigation/discovery counsel
- decision_not_made: no preservation-duty, hold-scope, release, retention, disposition, spoliation or legal-sufficiency decision
- outcome_unknown: operative duties and disputed scope remain unknown until authorized interpretation

Freeze matter/program ID, written authority, entities, jurisdictions, custodians, departments, systems, data types, relevant periods, cutoff and time zone, permitted sources, records schedules, disposition authorizations, preservation instructions, legal hold/release sources, confidentiality/privilege/privacy/security handling and reviewers. Record issuer, exact locator, version, effective date and supersession for each source.

Create atomic scope assertions linking source to entity, custodian, system, data category and period with proposed, included, cited-excluded, disputed, unavailable or reviewer-resolved state. Retain conflicts among schedules, holds and instructions. Notices and acknowledgements are supplied evidence snapshots only, not proof of delivery or compliance. All four roots use the same digest.

Stop on missing authority, ambiguous jurisdiction/entity, conflicting/superseded sources, unclear privilege or requested live action. Do not infer a duty, deadline, schedule, release or disposition authorization.
