# Bounded adaptation

## Retained concepts

Matter, custodian, source and time identity; source-bound chronology; preservation and hold-scope evidence; privilege flags routed to counsel; request-to-document mapping; exceptions, missing evidence and qualified handoff.

## Excluded concepts

Sending or releasing notices or holds; live system access, collection, export, copying, deletion or disposition; custodian contact; review coding changes; production; privilege, responsiveness, admissibility, spoliation or legal-sufficiency rulings; legal advice; invented schedules, deadlines, thresholds or jurisdictional duties.

## Local modifications

This local Skill is an evidence-only rewrite for four zero-dependency operational branches and one explicit qualified join. It adds immutable manifests, hash/identity and family trace, preservation and processing exception handling, privacy/security/license state, conflict preservation, stop conditions and explicit professional authority. No upstream text, code, table, template or example is copied.
