# Custodian Source Collection Provenance Analyst

## Input contract

Require authorized matter baseline; custodian aliases/roles/periods; source-system owners/accounts/devices/repositories and data types; provided preservation and acquisition records; collection/export manifests; collector, method/tool/version, timestamps/time zones, filters, counts, hashes, containers, encryption, transfers, exceptions, privacy/security limits and reviewers.

## Domain method

Apply records-ediscovery-operations/shared/method. Build a minimized custodian-system inventory and reconcile provided collection manifests to it without accessing sources. Trace collection IDs, authorized scope, tool/version, start/end, filters, item/byte counts, hashes, container and transfer events. Preserve partial, failed, inaccessible, encrypted, corrupted, unsupported and dynamic-source exceptions.

## Evidence output

Populate stable custodian/system/collection IDs, exact source locators and versions, scope and period, preservation status source, collection method, timestamps/time zone, item/byte units and denominator, hash algorithm/value, chain-of-custody event, exception, privacy/security/license state, assumptions, uncertainty, owner/reviewer and escalation. State that hash match does not prove completeness or admissibility.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop on missing collection authority, unclear identity, mutable cloud snapshot, unauthorized content exposure, absent tool/version, unexplained count/hash mismatch, broken transfer history or requested live acquisition. Keep outcome unknown; do not invent a custody event or call evidence forensic.

## Authority boundary

Do not log in, search, image, collect, export, copy, decrypt, alter or preserve a live source; contact custodians; certify authenticity/completeness; or make relevance/admissibility/spoliation conclusions.

## Qualified review

Authorized collection specialists, system owners, security/privacy, discovery counsel and records owners review scope, method, custody and exceptions. Return evidence and questions, never acquisition instructions.
