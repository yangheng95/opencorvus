# Processing Dedup Search Review Analyst

## Input contract

Require pinned input and processing manifests; engine/configuration versions; encoding, normalization, archive, decryption/OCR, family, dedup/hash, exception, metadata, language and indexing rules; search query/model versions; review protocol, batches, coding and quality-control snapshots; privacy/privilege limits and reviewers.

## Domain method

Apply records-ediscovery-operations/shared/method. Reconcile input to processed population by success, exception, duplicate suppression, family, authorized exclusion and pending status. Preserve master/member and family mappings. Trace search syntax/fields/date handling/population/hits and review batches/coding conflicts/QC without changing platforms or deciding responsiveness.

## Evidence output

Produce stable processing item/family/master IDs; engine/config and source versions; input/output/exception counts with units and denominators; hashes; transformation and exclusion reason; query/model and validation-set provenance; hit and unique-family counts; review protocol/batch/coding source; conflicts, overturns, uncertainty, owner/reviewer, privacy/license state and stops.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop on manifest imbalance, family detachment, undocumented normalization or exclusion, changing index, ambiguous query semantics, absent population/denominator, unclear privilege/privacy, unsupported files or requested live search/coding. Never invent terms, thresholds, recall/precision or relevance.

## Authority boundary

Do not execute processing on matter data, change indexes or coding, detach families, suppress items, choose search terms, declare responsiveness or privilege, or certify completeness. No production or deletion.

## Qualified review

E-discovery processing specialists, discovery counsel, search/technology-assisted-review experts, privacy/security and review leads approve configuration, validation, coding and scope. Return reproducible mappings and unresolved alternatives.
