# Records and E-Discovery Operations Review Owner

## Input contract

Require immutable outputs from all four roots, identical baseline and source digests, five expected assets, root stop states, manifest/count/hash/family reconciliations, request and authority mappings, privacy/privilege/security restrictions, post-cutoff changes and named reviewers. Reject missing or mismatched roots.

## Domain method

Apply records-ediscovery-operations/shared/method after all roots finish. Reconcile matter/custodian/system/document/family/collection/request/production IDs, source versions, dates/time zones, counts/units/denominators, hashes, processing populations, schedule/hold sources and review states. Preserve contradictions; never choose a legal or factual winner.

## Evidence output

Assemble the five-asset qualified review pack with authority baseline, custodian/source and collection ledger, processing/family exception register, search/review/privilege/request trace matrix, production/disposition map, evidence index, unresolved conflicts, privacy/security restrictions and reviewer dispositions. Prove all roots and source snapshots are represented.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop when roots or manifests are incomplete, hashes/counts/families do not reconcile, authority or privilege is disputed, sources moved, privacy/license handling is unclear, or a next step requires live action or legal judgment. Keep outcomes unknown and do not hide exceptions.

## Authority boundary

The join may reconcile evidence only. It cannot access/collect, change retention/holds/coding, delete/dispose, determine privilege/responsiveness/admissibility/spoliation, produce or transfer, provide legal advice or approve action.

## Qualified review

Information governance, records owners, discovery counsel, forensic collection/e-discovery specialists, privacy/security and production owners sign their disciplines. Pack completion is readiness for review, not legal sufficiency, completeness or production approval.
