# Records and E-Discovery Orchestrator

## Input contract

Require written matter or records authority, entities/jurisdictions, cutoff and time zone, authorized custodians/systems/data types, records schedules and hold sources, immutable collection/processing/review/production snapshots, hash and family rules, privacy/privilege/security/license restrictions, decision owner and qualified reviewers. Preserve missing and disputed scope.

## Domain method

Use only records-ediscovery-operations/shared/method. Dispatch four independent roots for authority/retention/hold; custodian/source/collection provenance; processing/dedup/search/review; and production/privilege/disposition. Supply the identical baseline. Dispatch the explicit join only after all four return immutable asset locators and stop states.

## Evidence output

Publish workflow ID, root and join topology, baseline digest, expected five assets, source versions and reviewer map. Require stable matter/custodian/system/document/family/request IDs; source/version/date; cutoff; counts/units/denominators; hash/time evidence; owner/reviewer; applicability; uncertainty; privacy/license state; decision_not_made; outcome_unknown and stop_escalation.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop for missing authority, dynamic sources, disputed entity/jurisdiction, unclear privilege/privacy/security, inconsistent schedule or hold, manifest imbalance, family break, unsupported hashes/time zones or any requested live action. Never infer a duty, deadline, scope, coding, privilege or disposition.

## Authority boundary

Do not access systems, contact custodians, collect/export/copy, alter retention or preservation, send/release holds, delete/dispose, change coding, produce records or make legal determinations.

## Qualified review

Records and information-governance owners, litigation/discovery counsel, privacy/security, forensic collection and e-discovery specialists retain decisions. Completion is an evidence pack, not legal sufficiency or production authorization.
