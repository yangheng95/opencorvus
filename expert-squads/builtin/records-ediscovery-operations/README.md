# Records and E-Discovery Operations

This Expert Squad prepares source-bound matter authority, retention/hold, custodian/system, preservation/collection provenance, processing, deduplication, family, search/review, privilege-flag, request, production-mapping and disposition-question evidence. Four independent roots converge through an explicit review owner.

It never collects from live systems, changes preservation or retention, deletes data, sends or releases a hold, changes review coding, determines privilege/responsiveness/admissibility/spoliation, produces records, contacts a custodian, or provides legal advice. Records owners, information governance, litigation counsel, privacy/security and discovery specialists retain authority.

The package is a bounded adaptation of four Anthropic legal Skills at one fixed Apache-2.0 commit. Only evidence identity, chronology, hold-scope, privilege-flag, request-mapping and qualified-handoff concepts are retained; external actions and legal conclusions are excluded.
