# Selection guidance

Select this Squad for an authorized matter or records program needing a reproducible evidence pack across authority and retention/hold sources; custodian and source-system inventory; preservation and collection provenance; processing, normalization, deduplication and family relationships; search and review evidence; privilege flags, request mapping, production mapping and disposition questions.

Provide matter authority, entities and jurisdictions, effective cutoff, records schedules, hold instructions, custodian/system sources, collection and processing manifests, hash and time-zone rules, review protocol, request and production specifications, privacy/privilege restrictions, decision owner and qualified reviewers.

Do not select it to access or collect live data, alter holds or retention, delete or dispose, send notices, release holds, code documents, rule on privilege/responsiveness/admissibility/spoliation, produce material or offer legal advice.
