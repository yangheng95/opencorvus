# Selection guidance

Select this squad for an authorized, date-locked evidence set involving adverse-event case versions and duplicates, aggregate counts or signal review, RSI version trace, or signal action governance. Provide the product/event scope, source inventory, versions, data lock, jurisdiction or program applicability, owners, and required reviewers.

Do not select it to replace clinical care, a safety physician, Qualified Person Responsible for Pharmacovigilance (QPPV), Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities (MedDRA) coder, formal reporting or submission workflow, label governance, privacy review, legal advice, or regulator interaction. Stop if patient-level identifiers are unnecessary or unauthorized, denominators and provenance cannot be separated, or the requested decision exceeds evidence-preparation authority.
