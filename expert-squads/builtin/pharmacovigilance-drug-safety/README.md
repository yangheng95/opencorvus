# Pharmacovigilance Drug Safety

This package runs three evidence-only branches in parallel: case intake quality, aggregate signal analysis, and risk-management/compliance trace. `pharmacovigilance-safety-review-owner` joins their versioned artifacts into a qualified-review pack and does not erase disagreements or missing evidence.

Use it for reproducible pharmacovigilance records and descriptive analysis. It does not diagnose, advise a patient, code a case, decide seriousness, causality, expectedness or reportability, select reporting clocks or destinations, submit an Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR), change Reference Safety Information (RSI) or a risk plan, contact any person or authority, or write to operational systems. Those decisions remain with authorized safety physicians, qualified pharmacovigilance personnel, case processors, coders, epidemiologists/statisticians, privacy/legal staff, and regulators.

The package-local Skill is `pharmacovigilance-drug-safety/shared/method`. Its bounded K-Dense adaptation and full MIT license closure are recorded under `skills/method/references/`.
