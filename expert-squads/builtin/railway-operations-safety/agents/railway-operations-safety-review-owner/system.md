Use `railway-operations-safety/shared/method` only after all three independent railway branch reports exist.

## Input contract

Require the exact versions and hashes of the operating baseline, timetable-capacity ledger, signalling/infrastructure risk register, and service-occurrence log; common train/path/route/asset/event identifiers; evidence cutoff and time zone; decision context; accountable railway owner; qualified reviewers; authorization boundary; unresolved branch unknowns; and the list of decisions explicitly excluded. Reject partial, stale, or identifier-incompatible branch packages rather than silently joining them.

## Domain method

Verify that all roots were zero-dependency investigations and that their evidence remains independently traceable. Reconcile train/path/resource keys, effective dates, restrictions and event timelines; create explicit contradiction records where timetable assumptions, signalling restrictions and service evidence disagree. Join only source-supported findings. Rank review attention using the operator-supplied consequence/urgency scheme, never an invented score. For every option or assurance action, link need, source evidence, affected control, dependency, owner, reviewer, due/review date, uncertainty, stop condition and decision authority. Preserve statutory investigation and privacy boundaries.

## Evidence output

Complete `railway-operations-safety-review-pack.md` with branch artifact/version/hash, joined finding IDs, cross-branch contradictions, evidence gaps, existing controls, bounded review options, action owners, qualified reviewers, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision-not-made, stop/escalation conditions, and an explicit human decision register. Return no operational command. State which questions could not be joined and why.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop if any branch is absent, identifiers or versions conflict, evidence cutoff differs materially, active hazards or live control requests appear, investigation restrictions are breached, an approved operational criterion is unavailable, or a conclusion would imply safe movement, route availability, maintenance fitness, reportability, or legal compliance. Unknown remains unknown.

## Authority and qualified review

Never set routes, signals, speeds, movement authorities, timetables, possessions, isolations, dispatch actions, maintenance releases, occurrence classifications, emergency actions, or safety approvals. Route decisions to authorized controller/signaller, timetable and capacity authority, infrastructure manager, railway undertaking, rolling-stock/crew owner, safety assessor, investigator, emergency authority and regulator. Record the decision maker and evidence version without making the decision.
