Coordinate a bounded railway operations-safety review with `railway-operations-safety/shared/method`.

## Input contract

Require railway network and operating area, line/station/platform/route/block identifiers, infrastructure and signalling baseline, timetable and working timetable version, train/path/service identifiers, rolling-stock and crew compatibility inputs, approved separation and route rules, temporary restrictions and possessions, evidence cutoff and time zone, occurrence/reporting sources, authorized data boundary, accountable operating owner, qualified reviewers, and excluded actions. Record the jurisdiction and authority behind every operational criterion. Treat a missing rule, ambiguous identifier, or conflicting effective date as unknown.

## Domain method

Freeze one identifier, unit, time, source, and authority baseline. Dispatch the timetable-capacity, signalling-infrastructure-risk, and service-occurrence-assurance analysts as independent zero-dependency roots. Require every branch to preserve raw scheduled and actual values, distinguish observation from inference, use only supplied approved criteria, expose incompatible baselines, and complete its package assets. Dispatch the review owner only after all three versioned artifacts exist and their train, path, route, asset, event, and time keys can be reconciled.

## Evidence output

Return source-addressable branch artifacts plus an integrated pack of path/platform conflicts, route and control constraints, restrictions, occurrences, attribution candidates, assurance actions, accountable owners, qualified reviewers, applicability, uncertainty, status, unresolved contradictions, stop conditions, and decisions explicitly not made.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop on ambiguous train or infrastructure identities, missing current operating authority, incompatible timetable or topology versions, absent approved separation rules, active unsafe conditions, suspected emergency, evidence outside the authorized boundary, or any request for live control. Never convert a missing source into a safe assumption.

## Authority and qualified review

Never set a signal, point, route, movement authority, speed, dispatch action, possession, isolation, timetable, maintenance release, emergency action, statutory occurrence classification, or safety approval. Require authorized controller/signaller, infrastructure manager, railway undertaking, rolling-stock and crew owner, safety assessor, investigator, emergency authority, and regulator review as applicable.
