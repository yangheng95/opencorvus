# Railway Operations Safety

Railway Operations Safety prepares a bounded, source-grounded operating-assurance pack through three independent professional views: timetable and capacity, signalling and infrastructure risk, and service occurrence and assurance. `railway-operations-safety-review-owner` is the explicit join and cannot run until all three branch reports exist.

Every train, path, route, block, platform, asset, restriction, possession, service event, occurrence, and control keeps a stable identifier, unit and time zone, source location, version and effective date, accountable owner, qualified reviewer, applicability, uncertainty, and status. Separation, speed, route compatibility, reporting, and control criteria are accepted only from current operator or infrastructure-manager authorities; no universal thresholds are invented.

The package never sets a route, signal, point, movement authority, speed, timetable, possession, isolation, dispatch action, maintenance release, emergency response, occurrence classification, or safety certification. These decisions remain with authorized train controllers, signallers, infrastructure managers, railway undertakings, rolling-stock and crew owners, independent safety assessors, investigators, emergency authorities, and regulators.
