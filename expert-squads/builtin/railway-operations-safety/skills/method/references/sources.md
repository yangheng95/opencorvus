# Sources and clean-room boundary

## Authorship and scope

This package method and its templates were authored clean-room for evidence reconciliation in railway operations. They do not reproduce text, tables, thresholds, rules, signal principles, reporting tests, or procedures from an external Agent Skill. They provide a provenance structure and calculation discipline; the operator must supply current rules for the named network, jurisdiction, equipment and effective date.

## Primary method authorities to refresh

- European Union Agency for Railways, Common Safety Method for Risk Evaluation and Assessment: `https://www.era.europa.eu/domains/common-safety-methods/risk-evaluation-assessment-csm_en`
- Electronic Code of Federal Regulations, 49 CFR Part 225, Railroad Accidents/Incidents: Reports Classification, and Investigations: `https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-II/part-225`
- Electronic Code of Federal Regulations, 49 CFR Part 236, Rules, Standards, and Instructions Governing the Installation, Inspection, Maintenance, and Repair of Signal and Train Control Systems: `https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-II/part-236`

These sources establish why risk, signalling and occurrence evidence needs authoritative review; they do not create globally applicable operating rules. Before use, record retrieval date, jurisdiction, current version/effective date and the local approved documents that govern the actual railway. If current authority cannot be confirmed, stop.
