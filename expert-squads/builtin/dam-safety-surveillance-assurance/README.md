# Dam Safety Surveillance Assurance

Dam facility configuration, inspection, instrumentation, performance, potential-failure-mode and control evidence assurance without operational, engineering-release or emergency authority.

Use for source-bound dam inspection and surveillance evidence prepared for qualified dam-safety review.

The scheduler dispatches 4 independent professional evidence branches, then one explicit join preserves conflicts and produces a qualified-review pack. Every scheduler and worker projects only `dam-safety-surveillance-assurance/shared/method`.

- Do not operate gates, spillways, outlets, reservoir level or instrumentation; do not change monitoring frequency or thresholds.
- Do not declare a dam safe/unsafe, assign hazard class, accept risk, order repair, activate an emergency action plan, issue a warning or direct evacuation.
- Dam owner, qualified dam-safety/geotechnical/structural/hydraulic engineers, regulator, operations and emergency authorities retain decisions.

Source, license, adaptation and clean-room boundaries are saved under `skills/method/references/`. The five reusable domain assets under `skills/method/assets/` are evidence structures, not completed decisions.
