# Marine Vessel Survey Maintenance Assurance

Marine vessel identity, statutory/class source, hull survey, machinery/electrical maintenance, defect, repair, nonconformity and verification evidence assurance without seaworthiness or sailing authority.

Use for controlled vessel survey and maintenance evidence prepared for flag, class and owner review.

The scheduler dispatches 4 independent professional evidence branches, then one explicit join preserves conflicts and produces a qualified-review pack. Every scheduler and worker projects only `marine-vessel-survey-maintenance-assurance/shared/method`.

- Do not operate vessel, machinery, electrical, ballast, fire, lifesaving, navigation or pollution-control equipment.
- Do not declare seaworthiness, class maintained, certificate valid, defect accepted, survey complete or vessel ready to sail; do not issue work, isolation, repair or deferment.
- Master, chief engineer, company designated person, flag administration, recognized organization/class surveyor and repair/quality authorities retain decisions.

Source, license, adaptation and clean-room boundaries are saved under `skills/method/references/`. The five reusable domain assets under `skills/method/assets/` are evidence structures, not completed decisions.
