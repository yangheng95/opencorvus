Own process handoffs, recorded fraud indicators, reserve and payment evidence, privacy, segregation, and approval-control analysis. Apply `insurance-claims-operations/shared/method`.

Input contract: require the claim ID, process stage/version, source-system extracts and timestamps, reserve-definition and transaction-code documentation supplied by the operator, currency, approval matrix, privacy classification, and accountable claims, special-investigation, finance, and privacy reviewers.

Method and checks: map first notice, assignment, evidence requests, review, proposed settlement, approval, payment, closure, and reopening handoffs with owner, received/complete times, queue time, and exceptions. Record fraud indicators only as source-attributed observations against an operator-supplied indicator definition; never label a person or infer fraud. Reconcile reserve and payment records only with the supplied roll-forward definition: list opening balance, increases, decreases, payments or recoveries, closing balance, each transaction ID, and `variance = reported closing - computed closing`. If signs or transaction semantics are not supplied, do not compute. Check maker/reviewer/approver separation, duplicate payment references, authorization evidence, retention, access, privacy, and escalation status.

Evidence output: populate the financial and control register with amount, currency, valuation date, source/version, control owner, reviewer, uncertainty, exception status, and next authorized check. Return queue-time evidence, unresolved roll-forward variance, recorded indicator queue, privacy issues, and missing approvals.

Stop when transaction semantics, currency, period, source lineage, or approval authority is missing; when protected information exceeds the task scope; or when a live investigation, reserve change, settlement, or payment would be required. Do not set reserves, trigger investigations, authorize transactions, accuse a party, or certify control effectiveness. Route decisions to licensed adjusters, special-investigation professionals, finance/payment approvers, privacy owners, and counsel.
