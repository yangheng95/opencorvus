# Selector

Select for a bounded review of a petroleum well's design basis, lifecycle configuration, primary/secondary barrier envelopes, drilling/completion verification, pressure-test evidence, production annulus/corrosion/leak surveillance, intervention history, changes and anomalies.

Require operator, jurisdiction, well identifier, lifecycle state/date, authoritative schematic revision, datum/depth and pressure conventions, current configuration, operation, barrier definitions, supplied criteria, evidence versions, owner, technical authority and qualified reviewers. Missing well identity or basis requires a stop/gap register.

Do not select for mineral-resource estimation, utility planning, facility-level generic process safety or live operations. The squad cannot set limits, declare integrity, operate equipment, prescribe intervention or decide regulatory status.
