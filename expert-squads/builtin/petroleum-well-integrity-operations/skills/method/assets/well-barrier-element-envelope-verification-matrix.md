# Well-barrier element and envelope verification matrix

## Control header

- Template ID: `PWI-BAR-TEMPLATE-001`
- Purpose: Trace credible flow paths to primary/secondary envelopes, elements and verification evidence.
- Quantity and unit: every value records unit, pressure basis, depth/datum reference, temperature condition, denominator and conversion source as applicable.
- Source/version/date: immutable locator, document/system version, effective/test timestamp and data cutoff.
- Owner: named operator evidence owner.
- Qualified reviewer: drilling/completion/production/well-integrity engineer and technical authority; independent examiner where required.
- Applicability: operator, jurisdiction, well/UWI, field/facility, lifecycle state, configuration/schematic and operation.
- Assumptions: only operator-authorized design or interpretation assumptions with source.
- Uncertainty: measurement, calibration, sampling, model, configuration and interpretation limits.
- Confidentiality/license: protect operational data and controlled standards.
- Status: draft, evidence-present, contradicted, unresolved, or qualified-review-complete.
- Decision not made: no barrier validity, integrity classification, MAASP/limit, operational, permit, waiver or compliance decision.
- Stop/escalation: ambiguity in well/configuration/basis, absent evidence, failed reconciliation or missing authority stops work.

## Completion procedure

Use a separate row for every well configuration, flow path, barrier element, test, time-series observation, anomaly or disposition item. Never merge sidetracks, completions, annuli, depth references, pressure bases or lifecycle states. Preserve chronological status and superseded configurations. Link summarized claims to controlled evidence rather than copying restricted data.

Cross-check the other four assets by stable identifiers. Identify flow paths without two supplied envelopes, elements without verification, tests from mismatched configurations, surveillance without calibrated instruments, anomalies without disposition and changes without before/after configuration. Record contradictions; do not resolve them through assumption.

## Required review questions

1. Are well identity, UWI, lifecycle state, schematic revision and effective date exact?
2. Are datum/depth, gauge/absolute pressure, temperature and units explicit?
3. Is each barrier statement attributed to an authorized source rather than asserted by the agent?
4. Does test evidence identify configuration, procedure, calibration, expected and observed response?
5. Are surveillance gaps and configuration changes visible in trends?
6. Does every unresolved issue name impact, owner, technical authority and stop condition?
7. Has the record avoided any live well-control or operational recommendation?

## Controlled closeout

The owner attests only to evidence provenance and completeness. Qualified engineers, technical authority, independent examiner and regulator record disposition in the authoritative system. Never expose credentials, alter well records, connect to control systems, send alarms or commands, prescribe field action or paste protected standard text. Retain corrections and superseded evidence under operator policy.
