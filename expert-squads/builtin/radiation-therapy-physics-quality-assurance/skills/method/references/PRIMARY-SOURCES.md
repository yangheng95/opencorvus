# Primary method sources

Evidence cutoff: 2026-08-11. Recheck current versions, local licence conditions and operator-controlled procedures for every task. These official sources anchor clean-room decomposition; no controlled text, table, formula or threshold is copied.

- [IAEA Radiotherapy Programme Implementation](https://www.iaea.org/resources/hhc/medical-physics/radiotherapy/programme-implementation) — equipment/software acceptance and commissioning, QA programme and independent audit context.
- [IAEA SSG-46 Radiation Protection and Safety in Medical Uses of Ionizing Radiation](https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/PUB1775_web.pdf) — current international safety-guide identity and qualified-role context.
- [IAEA TRS-398 Rev. 1](https://www.iaea.org/publications/15048/absorbed-dose-determination-in-external-beam-radiotherapy) — current absorbed-dose code-of-practice identity and calibration traceability context.
- [IAEA TECDOC-1583](https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/te_1583_web.pdf) — treatment-planning-system commissioning and typical-technique QA evidence context.
- [IAEA Accident Prevention through Treatment Planning](https://www.iaea.org/resources/rpop/health-professionals/radiotherapy/accident-prevention/errors-in-treatment-planning) — change validation, independent calculation and TPS evidence lessons.
- [eCFR 10 CFR 35.41](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/chapter-I/part-35/subpart-B/section-35.41) — one current U.S. written-procedure source where applicable; the operator must identify the actual jurisdiction and adopted requirements.

Use the actual facility licence, qualified medical-physics procedures, locally authorized tolerance/action sources, calibration certificates and regulator/accreditor requirements. Website summaries never replace those controlled sources.
