# Radiation Therapy Physics Quality Assurance

Source-bound radiotherapy equipment, dosimetry, treatment-planning-system, patient-specific quality, change, incident and independent-audit evidence without clinical or release authority.

The scheduler dispatches four independent professional branches, then one explicit all-roots join preserves conflicts and produces a qualified-review pack. Every scheduler and worker projects only `radiation-therapy-physics-quality-assurance/shared/method`.

- Do not create or approve treatment plans, prescribe dose or fractionation, operate equipment, authorize beam-on, adjust a treatment system, handle a source, or return equipment to service.
- Do not invent tolerance/action levels, classify a medical event, report externally or declare safety, compliance, calibration validity or clinical fitness.
- Qualified medical physicists, radiation oncologists, radiation therapists, radiation-safety/licence owners and authorized service or regulatory reviewers retain decisions.

The Skill is clean-room. Rejected-candidate evidence and primary-source method anchors are under `skills/method/references/`; the five assets are controlled evidence structures, not completed professional decisions.
