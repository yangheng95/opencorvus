# Customer Success Operations

Customer Success Operations converts exact customer evidence and lifecycle facts into a reviewable operating plan. It separates the customer evidence ledger from the lifecycle and handoff map, runs those analyses concurrently, joins both into one plan, and independently reviews the result.

The package does not update a customer relationship management system, change an account, promise retention, or invent customer data. Operators remain responsible for account decisions and external writes.
