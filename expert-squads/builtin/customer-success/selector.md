# Selection instructions

Select this package when the requested outcome is a customer-success operating decision grounded in supplied or discoverable customer, support, product-usage, commercial, or relationship evidence.

Require an explicit population or segment, time boundary, decision question, available evidence, and intended operator. Report missing evidence and unknowns instead of manufacturing health scores, churn reasons, customer quotes, service-level results, or renewal outcomes. External account, contract, pricing, entitlement, communication, and customer relationship management changes remain operator actions.
