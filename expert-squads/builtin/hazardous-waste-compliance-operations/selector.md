# Selection guidance

Select this squad when the requested output is an evidence-controlled register or review pack covering generation process and waste-stream identity, process knowledge and analytical evidence, generator quantities by physical site and calendar month, accumulation-unit records, or manifest/disposition reconciliation.

Do not select it to provide legal advice, declare a material hazardous or non-hazardous, assign codes or generator category, set a compliance deadline, sample or handle waste, specify packaging/labels, sign or submit records, arrange transport, choose treatment/disposal, or manage a spill or emergency. Those actions require the exact applicable federal/state/local authority and authorized qualified personnel.
