# Generator Category and Accumulation Unit Compliance Map

This map reconciles evidence for one physical site and calendar month. It does not declare generator category or compliance, establish a deadline, or authorize any container/tank/area action.

## Site-month quantity section

For every row record `artifact_id`, immutable `row_id`, legal entity, physical site and supplied IDs, jurisdiction, calendar month and time zone, stream and generation-event IDs, source-defined waste class, source quantity/value, unit, batch/time denominator, measurement or estimate method/version, conversion factor and unit, density/source if used, counted result, inclusion/exclusion rationale with exact authority locator/version/date, episodic or correction link, cutoff/effective date, source locator/version/date, owner, qualified reviewer, applicability, assumptions, uncertainty/confidence, privacy/license, status, `decision_not_made`, `outcome_unknown`, and stop/escalation. Keep sites, months, classes, and revisions separate; no threshold is embedded here.

## Accumulation unit section

Link each unit/container/tank/area ID to location, type, capacity and unit, contents/stream, start and marking dates as supplied, label/marking evidence, condition, compatibility/secondary-containment evidence, inspection record/version/date, training-role record, emergency/contingency-plan reference, corrective-action record, and effective interval. Report evidence presence and conflicts; do not infer a compliance state or instruct an operator.

## Controlled review template

`artifact_id=GCAU-MAP-001`; `row_id=GCAU-ROW-0001`; `site_month=site pending / YYYY-MM`; `stream_event=pending`; `value_unit_denominator=pending`; `conversion_source=pending`; `applicable_rule_version=pending`; `accumulation_unit=pending`; `source_locator/version/date=pending`; `cutoff_or_effective_date=YYYY-MM-DD`; `owner=facility waste coordinator`; `qualified_reviewer=environmental health and safety lead/qualified environmental professional`; `applicability_jurisdiction=one site/month/rule edition only`; `assumptions=none for missing quantities or controls`; `uncertainty_confidence=unreconciled`; `privacy_license=operator-controlled`; `status=stopped`; `decision_not_made=no category, compliance, deadline, label, movement, storage, inspection, correction, or emergency action`; `outcome_unknown=true`; `stop_escalation=obtain source quantities, exact rule, unit identity, and qualified review; invoke emergency plan for danger`.
