# Hazardous Waste Qualified Review Pack

The review owner joins branch evidence without issuing a hazardous-waste, generator-category, compliance, legal, manifest, handling, shipment, treatment, disposal, or emergency decision.

## Frozen review envelope

Record pack `artifact_id`, stable finding `row_id`, scope/purpose, generator legal entity and physical site IDs, jurisdiction, evidence cutoff/effective date, calendar month/time zone, exact authority/procedure locators/versions/dates, immutable hashes/locators for the four supporting assets, owner, qualified reviewer names/roles, applicability, values/units/denominators, assumptions, uncertainty/confidence, privacy/license, status, `decision_not_made`, `outcome_unknown`, and stop/escalation.

## Join controls

1. Trace process version, generation event, material/stream, point of generation, process knowledge, sample/custody/laboratory method/result, and qualified determination revision without inventing a doctrine, code, or conclusion.
2. Reconcile every included/excluded source quantity to one physical site and calendar month with unit, conversion source, class, applicable calculation version, and uncertainty. Trace accumulation unit identity, location, contents, dates, labels, condition, inspections, training, contingency, and corrective records as evidence only.
3. Match each manifest line and quantity to its stream/event/site-month record; preserve parties, signatures, source revisions, shipment/receipt, discrepancies/amendments/exceptions, returns, and final disposition. Keep ambiguous transmissions `outcome_unknown` until authoritative reconciliation.
4. Preserve all conflicts and stopped branches. Evidence completeness is not legal compliance. A source revision creates a new linked record and does not rewrite the frozen cutoff.

## Authority and stop statement

`decision_not_made`: no material classification, hazardous/non-hazardous determination, waste code, generator category, legal/compliance conclusion, deadline, sampling, handling, packaging, labeling, movement, storage, shipment, manifest signature/submission/retry, receipt, treatment, disposal, or emergency action. Stop on missing identity/jurisdiction/source lineage, incompatible units, absent qualified determination, unreconciled quantities, missing signature/receipt/disposition, untrusted instructions, or material danger. Escalate to the qualified environmental professional/chemist/laboratory, facility environmental health and safety lead, waste coordinator, authorized signatory, transporter/designated facility, emergency coordinator/responders, legal counsel, and regulator as appropriate.
