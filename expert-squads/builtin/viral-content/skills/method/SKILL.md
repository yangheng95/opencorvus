# Viral Content method

Start from the product truth, audience problem, and evidence questions. Separate observed audience language from inference. Treat trends as dated evidence, not timeless rules. Each concept states a hook, promise, proof, reason for timeliness, and falsifiable distribution hypothesis. Copy preserves claim provenance and explicit unknowns. Never guarantee engagement or convert a visual concept into a claimed image asset.
