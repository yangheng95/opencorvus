# Thermal-runaway, Propagation and Barrier Evidence Map

## Provenance and scope

Each row records `artifact_id`, `row_id`, configuration/sample/test/event IDs, source locator/authority/version/effective and observation dates, evidence cutoff, value/unit/time basis, owner, qualified reviewer, applicability/application/jurisdiction, assumptions, uncertainty/confidence, proprietary/license boundary, status, `decision_not_made` and `stop_or_escalation`.

The map describes recorded evidence from completed authorized work. It is not a test procedure, fire plan, barrier qualification, design acceptance or safety claim.

## Event chronology

Record event term and definition source, first abnormal signal, vent/rupture/fire/thermal-runaway observation, affected unit, neighboring response, path and timestamp/time-zone. Link every observation to raw channel/image/file hash, channel clock, calibration/range and measurement uncertainty. Keep observation, interpretation and hypothesis separate. Where sensor, video, radiography or post-test evidence conflict, preserve each source and create an explicit contradiction.

## Configuration and barrier rows

Record cell/module/pack position, spacing, orientation, enclosure, vent path, insulation/thermal barrier, cooling, detection, suppression and other relevant configuration versions. For each barrier keep `specified`, `present`, `triggered`, `operating`, `verified` and `effectiveness-not-decided` states distinct. Record counterevidence, boundary conditions and why the test does or does not represent another configuration/application.

## Stop and review

Stop/escalate for undefined terms, mixed configurations, unknown SOC/SOH, unsynchronized clocks, saturated/uncalibrated channels, missing raw data, active heat/venting/fire/damage, or any request to repeat or alter a test, handle remains, troubleshoot, suppress, ship, certify or release. `decision_not_made` states that no propagation cause, barrier effectiveness, pass/fail, design adequacy, emergency, handling, transport, certification or release decision was made.
