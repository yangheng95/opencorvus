# Selection guidance

Select this package for source-grounded battery cell/module/pack genealogy, operating-envelope, BMS/protection configuration, state-of-charge/state-of-health context, already-completed abuse-test conditions and instrumentation, thermal-runaway and propagation observations, barrier evidence, failure populations, censoring, reliability calculations and uncertainty.

Do not select it for live battery operation or testing, vehicle HARA/ASIL, grid dispatch, maintenance release, damaged-battery handling, firefighting, shipping, storage disposition, BMS/firmware or protection changes, certification, release or safety claims. Application-specific current standards and controlled procedures remain authoritative; results from one chemistry, format, configuration, state, initiation method or application must not be generalized without qualified review.
