# Valuation model checklist

## Comparable companies

- [ ] Peer selection, exclusions, business mix, geography, growth, margin, accounting, and capital structure are explained.
- [ ] Market values, enterprise values, financial periods, and selected metrics share explicit observation dates.
- [ ] Enterprise-to-equity bridges and metric definitions are reproducible.
- [ ] Outliers, negative denominators, non-comparable fiscal periods, and stale data are treated explicitly.
- [ ] Selected range is supported by the peer distribution rather than a single preferred point.

## Discounted cash flow

- [ ] Revenue, margin, tax, depreciation, capital expenditure, working capital, and free-cash-flow formulas have source or assumption lineage.
- [ ] Discount rate inputs, capital structure, risk-free rate, risk premium, beta or alternative method, and cost of debt are dated.
- [ ] Terminal growth or exit multiple is economically coherent with the forecast and peer evidence.
- [ ] Net debt, investments, minority interests, non-operating assets/liabilities, dilution, and currency bridge enterprise value to per-share equity value.
- [ ] Bull/base/bear cases and at least two-dimensional sensitivity recalculate from formulas.

## Output controls

- [ ] Observed facts, calculations, estimates, assumptions, and unavailable inputs are labeled.
- [ ] No fabricated current price, peer input, or precision is used to complete a table.
- [ ] Scenario assumptions reconcile with the fundamental analysis and thesis.
