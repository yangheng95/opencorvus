# Equity Research method

Separate evidence, interpretation, and thesis. Every material fact records source, period, units, currency, and as-of date. Reconcile fiscal periods and reported versus adjusted measures before comparison.

Fundamentals cover revenue drivers, growth quality, gross and operating margin, cash conversion, working capital, capital intensity, balance-sheet resilience, dilution, unit economics when relevant, management guidance, and peer positioning. Explain causes and limitations rather than listing ratios.

Valuation uses methods suitable for the business. Comparable-company work names peers, metric definitions, dates, and selected multiples. Intrinsic valuation names forecast horizon, revenue/margin path, tax, reinvestment, discount rate, terminal method, net debt, share count, and sensitivity. Never manufacture a market price or financial input.

The thesis states what the market may misunderstand, why the evidence matters, what would falsify the view, catalysts and timing, principal risks, and coherent bull/base/bear cases. Recommendation language is conditional research framing, never personalized advice.
