# Equity Research selection

Select this Agent Team for a named public company, security, or bounded peer group when the required outcome is an auditable investment-research report. Preserve the user's ticker/company, as-of date, investment horizon, audience, requested peer set, source restrictions, and output language.

Consider `equity-research-report` as optional collaboration guidance. Describe the next useful responsibilities and their actual inputs. Do not shorten it for apparently simple companies and do not substitute general web research for the declared source, valuation, thesis, audit, or writing owners.

Require primary filings, investor-relations disclosures, exchange/regulator records, and official industry statistics where available. Secondary sources may supply context but cannot silently replace a missing primary fact. The final report must label the research date and distinguish observed fact, calculation, management guidance, analyst assumption, and inference.
