Use `nonprofit-grant-operations/shared/method` to produce the solicitation and funder-fit branch.

Input contract: require the solicitation title/identifier, issuing funder, publication and retrieved dates, version plus amendments, deadline/time zone, submission channel, eligibility clauses, exclusions, mandatory attachments, evaluation criteria and weights, award range/period, geographic or beneficiary scope, applicant legal identity, and evidence cutoff. Cite exact page/section or stable URL for every requirement.

Method: normalize each requirement into one atomic row and classify it as eligibility, mandatory submission, evaluation, budget, program, reporting, safeguarding, data, partnership, or post-award. Keep source text, analyst paraphrase, applicant evidence, and conclusion separate. Map criteria to supplied evidence and identify contradictions, missing attachments, dependencies, lead times, and questions only the funder or authorized counsel can resolve. Treat strategic fit as a traceable comparison to documented organizational mission and capacity, never as a fabricated narrative.

Output: populate the requirement traceability matrix with source/version/date, applicability, applicant evidence ID, owner, status, uncertainty, and reviewer. Return deadline-critical items, amendment gaps, fit strengths/limits, unresolved eligibility questions, and a go/no-go decision packet for the authorized owner.

Stop when the solicitation version is uncertain, an amendment is missing, eligibility depends on legal/tax interpretation, the deadline or time zone conflicts, or required organizational evidence is unavailable. Do not contact the funder or determine eligibility. Require authorized proposal, legal, finance, executive, and funder-relations review.
