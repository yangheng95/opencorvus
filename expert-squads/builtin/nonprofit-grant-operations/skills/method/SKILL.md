# Nonprofit Grant Operations Method

## Freeze the opportunity

1. Register the solicitation identifier, issuer, version, all amendments, publication/retrieved dates, deadline/time zone, submission channel, proposed period, applicant entity, decision owner, data boundary, and excluded actions.
2. Atomize eligibility, mandatory, evaluation, budget, reporting, safeguarding, data, partnership, and post-award requirements in [the requirement matrix](assets/funder-requirement-traceability-matrix.md). Preserve exact page/section or stable URL.
3. Separate funder text, analyst paraphrase, approved organizational fact, draft proposal claim, projection, partner assertion, and unresolved question.

## Build program evidence and a testable logic model

1. Trace authorized need evidence to problem, inputs, activities, outputs, short/intermediate/long outcomes, assumptions, external factors, and risks in [the logic-model register](assets/logic-model-and-indicator-register.md).
2. Keep outputs (delivered products/services) distinct from outcomes (changes). Treat attribution as a claim requiring design evidence.
3. Define each indicator with numerator, denominator, unit, population, collection method, frequency, baseline, target-as-proposal, disaggregation/privacy limits, source, date, and owner.
4. Test capacity, partner status, beneficiary evidence, safeguarding, data collection burden, and causal links. Qualify or remove unsupported claims.

## Rebuild budget and post-award controls

1. Calculate each budget line from a declared basis such as quantity × rate × period. State currency, exchange-rate source/date, source/version, and uncertainty.
2. Reconcile direct + indirect + permitted contingency to request total. Reconcile cash and in-kind match separately; never assume uncommitted match or partner funding.
3. Map each cost to activity/output and exact rule. Label allowability, indirect-cost, tax, legal, and match treatment as review questions unless supported by authorized documentation.
4. Map cash timing, procurement, subaward, restricted-fund, timesheet, reporting, evidence retention, audit, safeguarding, and closeout controls in [the budget/control register](assets/budget-and-post-award-control-register.md).

## Join proposal and delivery readiness

Require all three independent branches and compatible solicitation versions, periods, currencies, and populations. Map every requirement to evidence, claim/attachment, owner, reviewer, and status. Map activities to budget, milestones, outputs, indicators, reports, and controls. Preserve disagreement and absent evidence in [the grant delivery plan](assets/grant-delivery-plan.md); never manufacture completeness.

## Authority and stop boundary

Never fabricate need, impact, outcomes, partners, beneficiary facts, consent, match, rates, or commitments; determine eligibility or legal/tax compliance; sign, certify, submit, contact a funder, accept an award, or approve spending. Stop on uncertain solicitation version, unresolved mandatory requirement, unsafe beneficiary data, missing safeguarding owner, unsupported partner/impact claim, irreconcilable budget, or unclear allowability/indirect-cost treatment. Require authorized program, monitoring/evaluation, grants, finance, legal/tax, safeguarding, privacy, procurement, partner, executive, and funder-facing review.
