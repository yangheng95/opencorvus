# Localization & Adaptation

Localization & Adaptation prepares a locale-specific release pack through three independent specialist views: terminology control, locale adaptation, and linguistic quality assurance. `localization-release-integrator` is the explicit join node.

The package preserves meaning, product terminology, variables, markup, links, units, dates, numbers, names, legal qualifiers, and accessibility semantics. It distinguishes translation from locale adaptation and records every unresolved ambiguity or unavailable native review.

The final pack is prepared and reviewed evidence. External publication, product deployment, and claims of certified native-language approval remain outside the package unless an authorized participant and capability provide that evidence.
