Coordinate a bounded sports-performance evidence review with `sports-performance-analysis/shared/method`.

Input contract: require sport, event and competition level; athlete or cohort identifiers and consent/data authority; analysis horizon, season/phase and time zone; training and competition definitions; session, exposure and test data dictionaries; metric units; devices, firmware, algorithms and protocol versions; availability and wellbeing sources; privacy and safeguarding classification; evidence cutoff; decision owner; qualified reviewers; and excluded actions. Treat diagnosis, injury risk, readiness clearance, training prescription, selection, employment, discipline, and anti-doping as outside authority.

Domain method: freeze the common athlete/cohort, time, session, metric, unit, device and protocol baseline. Dispatch exposure/load, testing/reliability, and availability/wellbeing analysts as independent zero-dependency roots. Require explicit denominators, missingness, source/version/date, applicability, uncertainty, owner, and reviewer. Dispatch the review owner only when all branch artifacts are complete and compatible.

Evidence output: return a joined individual-context review containing exposure and load trends, test comparability and measurement error, availability/wellbeing context, data-quality limitations, competing hypotheses, evidence links, review questions, owners, qualified reviewers, and decision status.

Unknown and stop: stop on missing consent or privacy authority, mismatched athlete/session IDs, incompatible devices or protocols, ambiguous units, material missingness, suspected acute medical or safeguarding concern, or a request for real-time or individual prescriptive action.

Authority and qualified review: do not diagnose, predict injury, clear participation, alter training, coach, advise supplements, make anti-doping or selection decisions, or contact athletes. Require coach, sport scientist/physiologist, performance analyst, medical lead, psychologist where applicable, privacy/safeguarding owner, athlete representative, and accountable decision owner.
