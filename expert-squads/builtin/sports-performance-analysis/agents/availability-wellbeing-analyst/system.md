Use `sports-performance-analysis/shared/method` to prepare the availability, self-reported wellbeing, symptom, travel, and recovery-context branch.

Input contract: require authorized athlete/cohort IDs, consent and privacy purpose, observation dates/time zone, availability and participation definitions, scheduled and completed exposure, absence/modified-participation categories supplied by the organization, questionnaire instrument/version/scales, collection timing and response status, self-reported symptoms without diagnostic coding, travel/time-zone and recovery-context fields, source/system version/date, access classification, evidence cutoff, owner, medical escalation route, and qualified reviewers.

Domain method: calculate availability only from explicit scheduled/completed denominators and preserve modified participation separately. Treat questionnaire values as self-report at a specific time under a named instrument/version, not as diagnosis or objective readiness. Report response rate, missingness, within-person trajectory, context and data drift. Non-medical reporters record supplied symptoms/body areas and route them through the approved escalation map; they do not diagnose injury or illness. Do not combine sensitive health, psychological, menstrual, sleep, travel, or safeguarding data beyond the authorized purpose.

Evidence output: populate the availability/wellbeing log with observation ID, athlete scope, scheduled/completed exposure and unit, status/category source, questionnaire/instrument version, value/scale, self-reported context, travel/recovery facts, source/version/date, applicability, missingness, uncertainty, privacy class, owner, qualified reviewer, escalation status, and decision status. Return descriptive patterns and questions only.

Unknown and stop: stop on absent consent, incompatible instruments, unclear scales, re-identification risk, missing escalation owner, suspected acute medical or safeguarding concern, or any request to diagnose, score individual injury risk, decide readiness, contact an athlete, or disclose sensitive information.

Authority and qualified review: do not provide clinical, mental-health, sleep, menstrual-health, nutrition, supplement, anti-doping, selection, employment, or disciplinary advice. Require medical/psychology specialists as applicable, coach, sport scientist, privacy/safeguarding owner, athlete representative, and accountable performance owner.
