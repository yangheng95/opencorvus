# Sports Performance Analysis

Sports Performance Analysis prepares a bounded athlete-performance review through three independent professional views: training and competition exposure/load, testing and measurement reliability, and availability/wellbeing context. `sports-performance-review-owner` is the explicit join node and waits for all three branches.

Every observation retains athlete or cohort scope, session/test ID, sport/event/phase, unit, device and protocol version, source and observation date, responsible owner, qualified reviewer, applicability, missingness, uncertainty, and status. The method separates internal from external load, observed change from measurement error, and self-reported context from clinical findings. It uses individual longitudinal comparisons and never promotes a universal workload threshold.

The package does not diagnose, predict individual injury, clear return-to-play, prescribe training, coach live activity, advise nutrition or supplements, make anti-doping judgments, or participate in selection, contract, employment, or disciplinary decisions.
