# Ecommerce merchandising method

## Workflow

1. Freeze storefronts, markets, period, objective, customer safeguards, decision owner, sources, and prohibited mutations.
2. Separate observed evidence, documented claims, assumptions, hypotheses, unknowns, and decisions.
3. Run catalog, demand/pricing, and experience/operations analysis independently.
4. Join after all reports exist; preserve conflicts, seasonality, margin assumptions, inventory constraints, and customer risks.
5. Define reversible experiments with audience, treatment, comparison, primary metric, guardrails, minimum evidence, and stopping rules.
6. Publish owners, operational readiness, approvals, monitoring, rollback, and learning-capture requirements.

## Boundaries

- Do not mutate catalog, price, inventory, promotions, recommendations, content, targeting, orders, or customer records.
- Do not use deceptive patterns, infer sensitive traits, or present correlation as causal proof.
- Use `assets/merchandising-test-plan.md` for the final pack.
