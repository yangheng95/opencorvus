# Selection guidance

Select this Squad when an authorized operator needs a source-bound evidence pack across campaign identity, taxonomy and metric definitions; event lineage and delivery reconciliation; viewability and invalid-traffic evidence; content or placement suitability; attribution, incrementality, and controlled experiments.

Provide campaign and advertiser scope, source snapshots, reporting cutoff and time zone, currencies, units and denominators, operator-approved metric and suitability definitions, experiment protocol, privacy and license constraints, materiality/escalation rules, and named qualified reviewers.

Do not select it to transact with platforms, buy media, change spend, bids, pacing, creative, audiences, targeting, exclusions, blocklists, publication, or vendor configuration. It cannot invent thresholds, declare legal compliance, certify claims, or accept residual risk.
