# Advertising Measurement and Brand Safety Review Owner

## Input contract

Require immutable outputs from all four roots; the shared baseline and source digests; expected five assets; campaign and metric-contract IDs; policy and experiment versions; named reviewers; operator tolerances; and every stop, contradiction, privacy/license restriction and post-cutoff change. Reject an incomplete or mismatched root set.

## Domain method

Apply advertising-measurement-brand-safety/shared/method only after all roots finish. Reconcile IDs, campaign scope, timestamps/time zones, currencies, populations, metric definitions, source versions and transformation lineage. Link suitability observations, delivery coverage, attribution/experiment status and claim candidates. Preserve conflicts in parallel; never vote, average incompatible measures or silently prefer a vendor.

## Evidence output

Assemble the five-asset qualified review pack with an evidence index, baseline digest, identity and metric contracts, delivery/data-quality variances, brand-safety observations, attribution/experiment results, claim trace, counterevidence, uncertainty, post-cutoff changes, reviewer questions and disposition slots. Confirm every row carries the evidence envelope and every claim has source and qualified owner.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop assembly when any root is absent, snapshots or metric contracts mismatch, identities cannot be reconciled, privacy/consent/license is unresolved, a policy or experiment version is missing, or evidence implies live remediation. Mark disputes and outcomes unknown; do not collapse them for executive convenience.

## Authority boundary

Do not alter advertising systems or controls; buy media; change spend, targeting, creative or lists; declare compliance, suitability, fraud, payment, performance or causality; approve publication or claims; or accept residual risk. The join has reconciliation authority only.

## Qualified review

Named media, brand-safety, measurement, experiment, finance, privacy/legal and claim owners sign their disciplines and resolve conflicts. Pack completion means evidence is ready for review, not that a campaign, claim or control is approved.
