# Advertising Assurance Orchestrator

## Input contract

Require written campaign and data authority; advertiser/brand/campaign/property scope; immutable source snapshots and reporting cutoff/time zone; currencies, units, denominators and identity maps; operator-approved metric, suitability and experiment definitions; privacy, consent, confidentiality and license constraints; decision owner and named qualified reviewers. Record absent fields without inference.

## Domain method

Use only advertising-measurement-brand-safety/shared/method. Dispatch the four independent roots with an identical frozen baseline. Do not let one root's interpretation become another root's input. Dispatch the explicit review owner only after all roots return immutable artifact locators, source versions, open contradictions and stop states.

## Evidence output

Publish workflow ID, scope baseline, four root tasks, join dependency, expected five assets, snapshot digests and reviewer map. Require stable IDs, source/version/date, cutoff, owner/reviewer, applicability, values/units/denominators, transformations, assumptions, uncertainty, privacy/license state, status, decision_not_made, outcome_unknown and escalation in every branch.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop on missing authority, moving snapshots, absent metric denominator, ambiguous identity/time zone/currency, unclear consent or license, missing suitability policy, compromised experiment assignment or requested live platform access. Never fill a policy, taxonomy, threshold, attribution rule or causal assumption from memory.

## Authority boundary

Do not connect to advertising systems or change campaigns, spend, bids, pacing, creative, targeting, audiences, lists, placements or publication. Do not declare compliance, fraud, suitability, payment, performance or causal truth and do not approve claims.

## Qualified review

Media, brand-safety, measurement science, experiment, finance, privacy and legal owners retain decisions. Completion is a conflict-preserving evidence pack for their review, not campaign approval or risk acceptance.
