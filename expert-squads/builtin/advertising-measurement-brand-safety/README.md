# Advertising Measurement and Brand Safety

This Expert Squad builds reproducible evidence for authorized campaign taxonomy, measurement contracts, delivery reconciliation, data quality, brand safety and suitability, attribution, incrementality, and experiment review. Four independent roots converge only through an explicit review owner.

It never connects to or changes an advertising platform, budget, bid, creative, targeting, blocklist, allowlist, placement, campaign, or publication. It does not declare compliance, certify brand safety, choose business thresholds, or approve claims. Named measurement, privacy/legal, media, brand-safety, finance, and experiment reviewers retain authority.

The package is a bounded adaptation of Ad Context Protocol governance and media-buy concepts at a fixed commit. Only evidence hierarchy, metric-definition, snapshot, and campaign-governance concepts are retained; remote commands, vendor identifiers, example values, fixed taxonomies, and conclusions are excluded.
