# Bounded adaptation

## Retained concepts

Property, collection, and content governance hierarchy; explicit metric definitions and measurement snapshots; campaign identity and source-bound governance evidence; separation of declared configuration from observed delivery.

## Excluded concepts

Every remote create, update, delete, activation, buying, planning, or reporting command; live network or platform access; budgets, bids, pacing, creative, targeting, audiences, blocklists, allowlists, or publication changes; vendor-specific identifiers; upstream example values; fixed taxonomy, threshold, or policy defaults; compliance, suitability, fraud, or claim conclusions.

## Local modifications

The local Skill is an evidence-only clean rewrite for four parallel assurance branches and a qualified join. It adds immutable source lineage, metric-denominator discipline, contradiction handling, privacy/license state, explicit unknowns, and human authority boundaries. No upstream text, code, table, schema, or example is copied.
