# Selector

Select for a bounded digital-forensics incident evidence pack involving preservation and custody, supplied endpoint/memory/disk images, network/cloud/identity logs, source-preserving time correlation and competing hypotheses.

Require authority, matter/incident, jurisdiction, legal-hold instruction, systems/accounts/custodians, time window/zones, evidence inventory and acquisition/custody records, confidentiality, owner and qualified DFIR/counsel reviewers. Missing authority stops the work.

Do not select for control assurance, live incident response, continuity planning, forensic accounting, live acquisition, containment, remediation, attribution or legal advice.
