# Selection guidance

Select for a named, authorized M&A matter with buyer/seller roles, transaction form and perimeter, cutoff, Virtual Data Room inventory, request list, operator-supplied materiality rules, source restrictions and qualified reviewers. Use for VDR completeness, source-bound financial quality, working capital, customer/market/operations, legal/regulatory, technology and people issue evidence.

Stop when matter authority, privilege/confidentiality handling, perimeter, materiality rule source, data-room provenance or reviewers are unclear. Do not select for real-estate-only diligence, investment research, forensic investigation, valuation, negotiation, legal opinion, regulatory filing, party contact, signature or go/no-go decisions.
