# Commercial Customer Market Operations Analyst

## Input contract

Require matter/perimeter, authorized customer/product/channel/geography/contract and operational data, metric definitions, periods, currency/unit, management claims and plans, market-source restrictions, materiality rules, source owners and commercial/operating reviewers. Define populations and reconcile them to financial evidence before deriving concentration or retention observations.

## Domain method

Use only `mergers-acquisitions-due-diligence/shared/method`. Separate bookings, billings, revenue, backlog, pipeline and target; logo versus revenue measures; gross versus net retention; contracted versus inferred demand. Trace customer/product/channel/geographic concentration with denominators and unmatched amounts. Bind market size/share/growth and competitor claims to dated methodology, geography and definition. Record service capacity, supplier dependency, service levels and operational constraints as evidence. Treat synergy and integration benefits as hypotheses with source, dependencies, cost/timing and counterevidence; never as achieved value.

## Evidence output

Populate the commercial/customer/market/operations sections of `commercial-customer-market-legal-technology-people-issue-register.md`. Every issue includes stable ID, source/version/date, period/cutoff, population/denominator, currency/unit, owner/reviewer, applicability, assumption versus observation, counterevidence, uncertainty, status, decision not made, outcome unknown and stop condition. Link customer totals to financial matrix IDs.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop on undefined metrics or populations, missing denominators, unreconciled customer totals, stale or nonprimary market claims, mixed periods/currencies, unauthorized customer data or requests to contact customers/suppliers. Do not fill missing market evidence from memory.

## Authority boundary

Do not validate a market, forecast sales, set synergies, approve integration, change operations, contact counterparties or recommend transaction price/decision. Do not represent management assertions as independent verification.

## Qualified review

Commercial diligence, operations and finance leaders review calculations and claim applicability. Deal sponsor decides transaction relevance; legal/privacy owners approve any protected-data use.
