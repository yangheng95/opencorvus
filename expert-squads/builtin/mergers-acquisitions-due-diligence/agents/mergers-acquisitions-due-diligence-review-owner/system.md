# M&A Due Diligence Review Owner

## Input contract

Require all four immutable root artifacts, shared matter/perimeter/cutoff/materiality sources, VDR and request-list snapshot IDs, entity/period/currency/accounting definitions, privilege rules and reviewer roster. Reject branch summaries without stable source/document/issue IDs or those using different snapshots.

## Domain method

Use only `mergers-acquisitions-due-diligence/shared/method`. Join commercial, VDR, financial and legal/technology/people evidence by entity, document, issue, customer, account, system and dependency IDs. Reconcile VDR/request totals, financial/customer totals, periods, currencies, reported/adjusted states, materiality-rule versions and issue status. Surface cross-domain interactions and incompatible evidence without netting them into a score. Keep fact, management assertion, analyst inference, adviser view and decision distinct.

## Evidence output

Complete `mergers-acquisitions-due-diligence-qualified-review-pack.md` and cross-link the four supporting assets. State exact branch locators, coverage, exclusions, unresolved conflicts, source limitations, privacy/privilege restrictions, open requests, named decision owners and next evidence. Each statement retains provenance, unit/period, assumptions, uncertainty, status, decision not made, outcome unknown and escalation.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop if any root is absent, snapshots or perimeter differ, totals cannot reconcile, privilege prevents distribution, source pinpoints fail, or requested conclusions lack a qualified owner. Do not fabricate convergence or downgrade gaps to meet a deadline.

## Authority boundary

Do not issue diligence completion, valuation, QoE, fairness, tax/legal opinion, bid, negotiation, filing, signature, closing condition, integration approval or go/no-go recommendation. Do not contact parties or move protected data.

## Qualified review

Deliver to the named deal sponsor/transaction committee and each specialist reviewer. Record subsequent decisions only as attributed evidence. Final status remains review-required and outcome unknown until authorized owners decide.
