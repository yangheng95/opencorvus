# Financial quality, working capital, and net debt trace matrix

## Reusable evidence contract markers

- artifact_id: stable artifact or row identity
- source_id_locator: exact authoritative source locator
- source_version_date: immutable source version and applicable date
- qualified_reviewer: named discipline reviewer with decision authority
- units_and_denominator: value, unit, currency, population and denominator or not-applicable rationale
- assumptions_uncertainty: authorized assumptions, unknowns, confidence and reason
- decision_not_made: explicit professional decisions this artifact does not make
- outcome_unknown: unresolved outcome stated without inference
- stop_escalation: exact hold point, reason, escalation owner and required review

## Evidence governance

Every record must carry a stable artifact or row ID; exact source locator, version, and date; the authorized cutoff or effective date; owner and named qualified reviewer; applicability, entity, jurisdiction, period, currency, unit, and denominator where relevant; operator-approved assumptions; uncertainty and confidence with reasons; privacy, privilege, confidentiality, and license state; current evidence status; decision_not_made; outcome_unknown; and explicit stop/escalation conditions. Missing fields remain visibly unknown and are never inferred. Evidence is restricted to the authorized transaction perimeter and immutable source snapshot.

- artifact_id: MADD-FIN-001
- owner: financial diligence workstream owner
- qualified_reviewer: transaction-services accountant, tax specialist, and deal sponsor
- decision_not_made: no valuation, purchase-price adjustment, tax opinion, forecast approval, or bid recommendation
- outcome_unknown: sustainable earnings, normalized working capital, net debt treatment, and transaction effect remain reviewer decisions

Create stable IDs for each reported amount, proposed normalization, working-capital classification, debt-like or cash-like item, and counterevidence item. Bind every amount to an exact trial-balance cell, management account, audited statement, bank schedule, debt schedule, contract, or workbook cell. Recompute arithmetic independently and reconcile management reporting to trial balance and audited statements while preserving eliminations, currency translation, policy changes, seasonality, acquisitions, and disposals.

For each adjustment record gross amount, sign, period, recurrence claim, supporting and contradicting evidence, and unresolved reviewer question. Never classify an item from its label alone. Working-capital evidence preserves account population, monthly history, seasonality, aged balances, cut-off issues, and operator-defined target mechanics. Net-debt evidence traces facilities, interest, leases, guarantees, restricted cash, factoring, and contingent items without choosing contractual treatment.

Rows include assertion ID, entity, period, source fact, calculation, units, denominator, reconciliation status, evidence for and against, uncertainty, owner, reviewer, and escalation. Conflicting versions remain separate. Mathematical closure is not accounting or deal acceptance.
