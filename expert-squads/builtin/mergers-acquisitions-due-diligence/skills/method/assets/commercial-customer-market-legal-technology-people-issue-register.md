# Cross-functional issue and counterevidence register

## Reusable evidence contract markers

- artifact_id: stable artifact or row identity
- source_id_locator: exact authoritative source locator
- source_version_date: immutable source version and applicable date
- qualified_reviewer: named discipline reviewer with decision authority
- units_and_denominator: value, unit, currency, population and denominator or not-applicable rationale
- assumptions_uncertainty: authorized assumptions, unknowns, confidence and reason
- decision_not_made: explicit professional decisions this artifact does not make
- outcome_unknown: unresolved outcome stated without inference
- stop_escalation: exact hold point, reason, escalation owner and required review

## Evidence governance

Every record must carry a stable artifact or row ID; exact source locator, version, and date; the authorized cutoff or effective date; owner and named qualified reviewer; applicability, entity, jurisdiction, period, currency, unit, and denominator where relevant; operator-approved assumptions; uncertainty and confidence with reasons; privacy, privilege, confidentiality, and license state; current evidence status; decision_not_made; outcome_unknown; and explicit stop/escalation conditions. Missing fields remain visibly unknown and are never inferred. Evidence is restricted to the authorized transaction perimeter and immutable source snapshot.

- artifact_id: MADD-XFUNC-001
- owner: named commercial, legal, technology, security, or people workstream owner
- qualified_reviewer: deal sponsor and relevant qualified discipline specialist
- decision_not_made: no legal conclusion, synergy, valuation, regulatory determination, employment decision, security acceptance, or recommendation
- outcome_unknown: financial and operational consequences remain unknown until review

Assign a stable issue ID and one atomic assertion. Identify category, entity, period, source fact, management statement, calculation, operator materiality rule, potential consequence phrased as a question, supporting evidence, counterevidence, missing evidence, and reviewer. Customer concentration preserves cohort, gross or net revenue basis, churn definition, related parties, currency, period, and denominator. Market claims preserve study sponsor, method, date, geography, and comparability. Operational indicators trace to source systems and transformations.

Legal entries capture clause and request mappings while routing interpretation to counsel. Technology entries inventory architecture, dependencies, obsolescence, service levels, incidents, security evidence, licenses, and integration constraints without probing systems. People entries prefer authorized aggregates and route employment, benefits, incentive, immigration, works-council, and privacy questions to specialists.

Link related assertions without merging contradictions. Cross-reference financial adjustments only by stable IDs. A severity label requires an authorized rubric; otherwise use unrated. Keep requests and owners visible. Stop on unauthorized personal or privileged data, unclear perimeter, unsafe technical access, or required legal interpretation.
