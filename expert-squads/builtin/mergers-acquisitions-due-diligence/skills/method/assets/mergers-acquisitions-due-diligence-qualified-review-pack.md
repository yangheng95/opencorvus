# Mergers and acquisitions due-diligence qualified review pack

## Reusable evidence contract markers

- artifact_id: stable artifact or row identity
- source_id_locator: exact authoritative source locator
- source_version_date: immutable source version and applicable date
- qualified_reviewer: named discipline reviewer with decision authority
- units_and_denominator: value, unit, currency, population and denominator or not-applicable rationale
- assumptions_uncertainty: authorized assumptions, unknowns, confidence and reason
- decision_not_made: explicit professional decisions this artifact does not make
- outcome_unknown: unresolved outcome stated without inference
- stop_escalation: exact hold point, reason, escalation owner and required review

## Evidence governance

Every record must carry a stable artifact or row ID; exact source locator, version, and date; the authorized cutoff or effective date; owner and named qualified reviewer; applicability, entity, jurisdiction, period, currency, unit, and denominator where relevant; operator-approved assumptions; uncertainty and confidence with reasons; privacy, privilege, confidentiality, and license state; current evidence status; decision_not_made; outcome_unknown; and explicit stop/escalation conditions. Missing fields remain visibly unknown and are never inferred. Evidence is restricted to the authorized transaction perimeter and immutable source snapshot.

- artifact_id: MADD-REVIEW-001
- owner: M&A diligence review owner
- qualified_reviewer: deal sponsor or transaction committee plus finance, tax, counsel, technology, security, and people specialists
- decision_not_made: no valuation, pricing, negotiation, legal sufficiency, filing, disclosure, bid, sign, approve, or go-no-go decision
- outcome_unknown: completion, price, remedies, integration outcome, and liability remain unknown

Confirm all four roots used the same baseline, cutoff, VDR snapshot, request version, currency and accounting basis, and materiality source. Reconcile artifact IDs, document IDs, Bates or stable locators, entities, periods, amounts, currencies, denominators, and family relationships. Preserve conflicting documents, representations, calculations, and reviewer positions separately; never choose a preferred version without authorized disposition.

Trace every issue to evidence, counterevidence, missing requests, financial bridges, privilege or privacy limits, uncertainty, owner, and reviewer. Separate observed facts, calculations, allegations, hypotheses, and decisions. Record excluded scope and post-cutoff changes. Verify no worker accessed remote systems, contacted parties, invented thresholds, interpreted law, valued the target, or recommended a decision.

Output an evidence index, perimeter and gap summary, cross-workstream trace table, unresolved contradictions, reviewer questions, and sign-off slots. A disposition records reviewer identity, discipline, date, source version, status, rationale locator, and follow-up owner. Completion means ready for human consideration, never that diligence is complete or advisable.
