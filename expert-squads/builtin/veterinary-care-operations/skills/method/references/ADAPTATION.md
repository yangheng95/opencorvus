# Bounded adaptation

## Retained

- Keep the controlled clinical record distinct from the client-facing summary.
- Trace client wording to current veterinarian-signed source fields.
- Preserve restriction, follow-up, clinic contact, delivery, and acknowledgment evidence.
- Use plain language only when the clinical meaning remains unchanged.

## Modified

These concepts are rewritten into a read-only veterinary operations method spanning episode identity, care-pathway records, diagnostics/orders, medication/procedure/anesthesia traces, inventory/cold chain, biosecurity, and qualified review. All facts require source/version/author evidence.

## Excluded

No inferred clinical facts; hard-coded symptoms, timelines, doses, or missed-dose advice; direct instructions; diagnosis; triage; treatment; prescribing; administration; discharge; client contact; system operation; or example patient output is retained. No upstream scripts or integrations are used.
