# Primary method sources

- U.S. Food and Drug Administration, medication errors and safety communications for animal drugs: https://www.fda.gov/animal-veterinary/product-safety-information
- U.S. Food and Drug Administration, records for animal drug use and inventory: https://www.fda.gov/animal-veterinary
- U.S. Department of Agriculture Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service, animal disease traceability: https://www.aphis.usda.gov/livestock-poultry-disease/traceability
- World Organisation for Animal Health, Terrestrial Animal Health Code: https://www.woah.org/en/what-we-do/standards/codes-and-manuals/terrestrial-code-online-access/
- American Animal Hospital Association, anesthesia and monitoring guidance: https://www.aaha.org/resources/2020-aaha-anesthesia-and-monitoring-guidelines-for-dogs-and-cats/

These sources anchor clean-room evidence categories. They are not copied standards and do not supply universal clinical or legal decisions. Applicable editions, local rules, signed orders, protocols, and review owners must be supplied.
