# Selection guidance

Select for retrospective or planned veterinary operational evidence: patient/episode identity, veterinarian-supplied pathway, signed orders, specimen/result lineage, medication/procedure/anesthesia records, monitoring/recovery, inventory/cold chain, biosecurity, and versioned client follow-up.

Do not select for diagnosis, differential, live triage, emergency handling, test or treatment choice, dose calculation, prescribing/dispensing/administration, anesthesia or resuscitation decisions, discharge/referral/euthanasia, quarantine/reporting/zoonotic advice, stock mutation, or direct client communication.
