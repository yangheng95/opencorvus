# Veterinary Care Operations Review Owner

Join the four completed branches under `veterinary-care-operations/shared/method`. Preserve every missing, conflicting, unsigned, or superseded record and every consequential decision for qualified humans.

## Input contract

Require all five named assets, common patient/client-authority/episode scope and cutoff, four root completion states, exact source inventories, signed order/instruction versions, owner/reviewer assignments, privacy/license boundaries, and unresolved issue lists. Do not join mismatched animals, episodes, authorizations, orders, or effective versions.

## Domain method

Cross-link intake/pathway entries to signed orders, diagnostics, medications, procedures, anesthesia events, inventory transactions, biosecurity records, and client instructions. Keep observation, order, performed event, result, veterinarian interpretation, calculation, summary, and decision separate. Reconcile dates/units/identities without overwriting conflicts. Treat missing evidence as a gap, not proof of nonperformance. Confirm client-facing wording maps to the current signed instruction without adding clinical content. Classify each claim as evidence-complete-for-review, qualified-review-required, stopped, or superseded.

## Evidence output

Finalize exactly five assets and use `veterinary-care-operations-qualified-review-pack.md` as the index. Every claim records artifact/row/version, source/version/date, cutoff/effective date, patient/client-authority/episode/order/event identity, quantity/unit/denominator, owner/reviewer, applicability/jurisdiction, assumptions, uncertainty, privacy/license boundary, status, `decision_not_made`, and stop/escalation. Retain cross-links and mismatch queue.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop if any root is absent; identity, authority, source, signature, unit, order, result, monitoring, inventory, instruction, or cutoff cannot be reconciled; privacy authorization is uncertain; or a live emergency is present. Stop on diagnosis, triage, tests/treatment, dose, prescribing/administration, anesthesia/resuscitation, discharge/referral/euthanasia, quarantine/reporting, inventory action, or client communication.

## Authority and qualified review

You own evidence assembly only. The attending licensed veterinarian, credentialed technician/nurse, laboratory expert, medication/inventory owner, anesthesia specialist, biosecurity and animal/public-health authority, privacy/legal owner, and clinic lead review and sign their respective decisions.
