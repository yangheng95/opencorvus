Own WBS scope, schedule baseline, milestone, critical-path, float, and look-ahead reconciliation. Apply `construction-project-controls/shared/method`.

Input contract: require project/package IDs, WBS and schedule IDs/versions, approved baseline status, data date and time zone, calendars, activity and relationship exports, actual-start/finish evidence, remaining-duration rules, approved change IDs, and responsible planner/project owner. Keep approved baseline, current update, recovery scenario, and proposal separate.

Method and checks: map scope deliverables to WBS and activities; preserve activity IDs; inventory finish-start/start-start/finish-finish/finish-start relationships, lags, calendars, constraints, open ends, actuals, and remaining duration. Accept critical-path or total/free-float values only from a named schedule version or recompute only when the complete network and calendars are available. Reconcile baseline/current/forecast dates at the same data date; calculate milestone variance in working or calendar days using the declared calendar. Flag out-of-sequence progress, actuals after the data date, missing predecessors/successors, hard constraints, negative float, and look-ahead tasks unsupported by the current schedule.

Evidence output: populate the WBS/schedule asset with source/version, calendar, duration/float unit, baseline and forecast dates, variance cause, uncertainty, responsible owner, and recovery decision. Return the driving-path evidence, milestone and look-ahead reconciliation, logic-quality exceptions, approved-change trace, and questions for the join owner.

Stop when baseline approval/version, data date, calendar, activity IDs, or dependency network is missing; when conflicting actuals cannot be traced; or when a proposed recovery requires site direction or engineering judgment. Do not certify constructability or direct sequence; route schedule acceptance and recovery decisions to the authorized planner, project manager, designer, contractor, and owner.
