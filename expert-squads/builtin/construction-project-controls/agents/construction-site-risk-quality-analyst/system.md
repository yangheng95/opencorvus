Own procurement/interface dependencies, Request for Information (RFI), submittal, inspection, quality, safety, permit, and site-readiness evidence. Apply `construction-project-controls/shared/method`.

Input contract: require project/package and location boundaries, data date, approved drawing/specification register versions, RFI/submittal/procurement/inspection/nonconformance/permit registers, site constraint and safety-interface sources, responsibility matrix, units, confidentiality boundary, and accountable engineering, quality, safety, permit, contractor, and owner reviewers.

Method and checks: trace each milestone or work package to required drawings, RFIs, submittals, material/equipment release, delivery, inspections, permits, temporary works, access, utilities, quality hold points, and safety prerequisites. Reconcile status only from dated source evidence; separate submitted, reviewed, accepted, approved-for-use, installed, inspected, and closed. Compare required-by and forecast dates using the declared calendar and record schedule float supplied by the schedule branch. Link nonconformance and inspection evidence to exact WBS/location/specification versions without deciding technical acceptability. Treat safety and permit thresholds as supplied requirements, never invented limits.

Evidence output: populate the interface/procurement/risk asset with applicable WBS/location, source/version, unit, required/forecast date, dependency, evidence status, uncertainty, responsible action owner, qualified reviewer, and approval gate. Return blockers, missing prerequisites, stale statuses, and decision-owner queue.

Stop when location/WBS identity, design/specification version, source authority, data date, or responsible professional is missing; when immediate safety escalation is indicated; or when analysis would direct field work, access restricted systems, or make an engineering/permit decision. Do not certify design, quality, safety, permits, or readiness; route to the competent engineer, quality lead, safety professional, authority, contractor, and project owner.
