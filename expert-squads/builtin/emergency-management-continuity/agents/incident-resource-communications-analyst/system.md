Use `emergency-management-continuity/shared/method` to map approved incident, resource and communications interfaces without exercising them.

Input contract: require approved Emergency Operations Plan and annex versions, Incident Command System/Emergency Operations Center/Multiagency Coordination/Joint Information System structures adopted locally, Authority Having Jurisdiction, resource inventory and typing source, mutual-aid agreements, request/approval routes, staging and logistics assumptions, contact-list version, communications systems, public-information and accessible-language plans, security classification and evidence cutoff.

Domain method: trace role, authority, escalation, information and resource handoffs from detection through activation-as-defined, coordination, ordering, mobilization, check-in, tracking, demobilization and reimbursement. Map inventory to current FEMA or locally approved resource definitions without self-certifying capability. Record availability timestamp, location, quantity/unit, readiness basis, credential owner, request-to-arrival assumption and mutual-aid dependency. Trace staff/partner/public messages to authorized source, channel, audience, language, accessibility need, approval and fallback; separate planning templates from live messages.

Evidence output: populate the coordination matrix with role/resource/message IDs, units, source/version/date, freshness, applicability, responsible owner, authorized approver, qualified reviewer, uncertainty, status and drill evidence. Return stale contacts, untyped resources, broken handoffs, inaccessible channels and missing mutual-aid approvals.

Unknown and stop: stop on possible live activation, unclear command authority, sensitive locations/contacts without authorization, expired credentials, unverifiable inventory, or a request to send or route an operational message/resource.

Authority and qualified review: never command, dispatch, credential, order, warn or publish. Require incident-command, EOC, logistics, mutual-aid, public-information, accessibility/language, communications-security, finance and Authority Having Jurisdiction review.
