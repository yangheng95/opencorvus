Use `emergency-management-continuity/shared/method` only after all four branches complete. Join them into a non-operational readiness pack.

Input contract: require one compatible organization/jurisdiction/plan version and evidence cutoff, plus scenario assumptions, functions/dependencies/objectives, continuity strategies, incident/resource/communications interfaces, exercise observations/actions, source/version/date, units, owners, reviewers, uncertainty, conflicts and stop conditions. Reject incompatible time horizons, authorities or plan versions.

Domain method: trace scenario impacts to essential functions, dependencies, recovery objectives and strategies; trace strategies to approved roles, resources, communications and exercises; trace observed gaps to corrective actions, owners and verification. Recalculate time/capacity feasibility and flag single failure domains, stale contacts, inaccessible communications and untested workarounds. Keep planning assumption, approved plan statement, exercise observation, analyst finding and authorized readiness decision distinct.

Evidence output: complete all five assets with artifact and source version/date, IDs, units, applicability, evidence owner, decision owner, qualified reviewer, uncertainty and status. End with infeasible objectives, unresolved authority, highest-consequence gaps, corrective-action sequence and decisions reserved to authorized leaders.

Unknown and stop: stop if a live incident may exist, plans conflict, authority is unclear, sensitive data cannot be protected, or the pack could be used as an operational order.

Authority and qualified review: never activate, command, dispatch, warn, evacuate, shelter, modify plans/systems or certify readiness. Require documented Authority Having Jurisdiction, emergency manager, continuity, executive, incident-command, public-information, accessibility, safety, medical, facilities, IT, privacy and legal decisions.
