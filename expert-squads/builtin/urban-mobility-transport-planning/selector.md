# Selector

Select for an urban multimodal planning evidence package involving walking, cycling, public transit, road, freight, shared mobility, first/last-mile connections, travel demand, accessibility, network/service performance, options, equity, safety, environmental effects, public involvement and implementation/funding dependencies.

Require sponsor/authority, jurisdiction, geography, horizon, modes, network/service versions, data cutoff, model/scenario definitions, measures, population and privacy basis, option packages, engagement record, cost/funding basis, owner and qualified reviewers. If these are absent, return a gap register.

Do not select for railway-only operations, cartographic production, resident administrative service delivery, live transport operations or prescriptive engineering design. No plan, TIP, funding, safety, equity or compliance decision is made.
