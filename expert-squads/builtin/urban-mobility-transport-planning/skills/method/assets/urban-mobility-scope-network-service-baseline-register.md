# Urban mobility scope, network and service baseline register

## Control header

- Template ID: `UMT-BAS-TEMPLATE-001`
- Purpose: Freeze geography, horizon, modes, facilities, services and authoritative baseline data.
- Quantity/unit: every value records unit, denominator, geography, population, time period, price year and comparison basis as applicable.
- Source/version/date: immutable data/document/model locator, version, effective or collection date and cutoff.
- Owner: named sponsoring-authority evidence owner.
- Qualified reviewer: transportation planner/modeler/engineer and equity/accessibility/community or legal/environmental reviewer as applicable.
- Applicability: jurisdiction, study geography, base year/horizon, mode, network/service, model and scenario.
- Assumptions: authorized local assumptions with source; never use upstream example values.
- Uncertainty: sampling, measurement, model, forecast, scenario, geographic and participation limits.
- Privacy/license: classification, suppression, permitted use and third-party terms.
- Status: draft, evidence-present, contradicted, unresolved, or qualified-review-complete.
- Decision not made: no option selection, design/safety approval, compliance, plan/TIP adoption, funding or public commitment.
- Stop/escalation: missing boundary, lineage, comparability, validation, privacy control or public authority stops work.

## Completion procedure

Use separate rows for different geographies, populations, modes, periods, network/service versions, models, scenarios, options and measures. Preserve observed values, model inputs, modeled outputs, assumptions and stakeholder statements as distinct evidence classes. Link every result to data and model versions. Do not copy a generic target or upstream threshold.

Cross-check all five assets. Detect incompatible baselines, unreconciled counts, stale GTFS/service, missing calibration or validation, different accessibility definitions, inconsistent denominators, omitted modes, double-counted benefits, hidden burdens, incomplete engagement and uncertain cost/funding bases. Preserve conflicting results and participation gaps.

## Required review questions

1. Are sponsor, jurisdiction, geography, horizon and decision explicit?
2. Are network, service, data and model versions aligned?
3. Does each measure state unit, denominator, population, period and comparator?
4. Are calibration, validation, sensitivity and forecast limits visible?
5. Are benefits and burdens disaggregated with privacy controls?
6. Does engagement evidence show method, participation limits and response traceability?
7. Are option selection, engineering approval, plan adoption, funding and public commitment reserved to human authorities?

## Controlled closeout

The owner attests only to evidence provenance and completeness. MPO/public authorities and qualified professionals record official decisions in authoritative systems. Never connect to live routing, dispatch or signal systems; contact the public; promise an outcome; expose restricted mobility data; or claim community consent. Preserve source updates and superseded scenarios as versioned evidence.
