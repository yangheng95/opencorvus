# Urban Mobility Transport Planning

An evidence-first urban multimodal planning squad. Four zero-dependency specialists establish network/service and data baselines, examine demand and accessibility modeling, compare multimodal options, and trace equity, safety and engagement evidence. An explicit planning owner joins them without choosing an option or adopting a plan.

The package projects one local Skill, `urban-mobility-transport-planning/shared/method`, to the scheduler and every worker and owns exactly five controlled assets. It is a bounded MIT adaptation of Urban Design Skills' multimodal structure plus clean-room public-planning governance. Exact source, license, retained concepts, exclusions and modifications are recorded in `skills/method/references/source-provenance.md`.

Use for a named jurisdiction, geography, horizon and authorized decision. It cannot perform live routing/dispatch/control, guarantee demand, approve design or compliance, adopt plans, commit funds or make public promises.
