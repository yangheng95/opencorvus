# Primary method sources

- DICOM Standard, current published parts and editions: https://www.dicomstandard.org/current
- U.S. Food and Drug Administration, Medical X-ray Imaging: https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/medical-imaging/medical-x-ray-imaging
- U.S. Food and Drug Administration, guidance for industry and FDA staff on medical X-ray imaging devices and facility quality: https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents
- International Atomic Energy Agency, quality assurance programme for diagnostic radiology: https://www.iaea.org/resources/rpop/health-professionals/radiology/quality-assurance

These are method anchors, not copied standards or automatic legal requirements. The facility must provide the applicable edition, jurisdictional rule, tolerance, protocol, and decision owner.
