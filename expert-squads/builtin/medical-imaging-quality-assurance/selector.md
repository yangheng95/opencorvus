# Selection guidance

Select this squad when the requested output is a source-bound medical-imaging QA evidence pack covering equipment/configuration, protocol baselines, phantom technical QC, minimized DICOM transfer/display lineage, dose-index context, nonconformance, service, retest, or CAPA trends.

Do not select it for clinical diagnosis, image/exam acceptance, patient-specific dose or risk judgment, acquisition or rescan decisions, scanner/PACS/RIS/workstation operation, protocol/display/routing change, de-identification execution, return-to-service, accreditation, or regulatory conclusions. Those require authorized systems and qualified human roles.
