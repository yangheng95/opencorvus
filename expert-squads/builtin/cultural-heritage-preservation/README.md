# Cultural Heritage Preservation

Cultural Heritage Preservation prepares a bounded, source-grounded preservation plan through three independent professional views: significance and provenance, condition and preventive risk, and digital preservation and access. `heritage-conservation-plan-owner` is the explicit join node and cannot run until all three reports are complete.

Every object, structure, site, collection unit, image, and record keeps a stable identifier, location, source, version/date, responsible owner, qualified reviewer, applicability boundary, uncertainty, and status. Verified archives, professional inference, oral sources, and community-held knowledge remain visibly distinct. Condition observations are location-specific and risks use documented cause-event-effect scenarios rather than invented universal thresholds.

The package prepares evidence and options only. It never treats, cleans, samples, moves, excavates, authenticates, restitutes, deaccessions, changes environmental controls, releases restricted knowledge, or claims legal, cultural, engineering, safety, or conservation approval.
