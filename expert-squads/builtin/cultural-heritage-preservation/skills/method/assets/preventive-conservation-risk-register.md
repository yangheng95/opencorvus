# Preventive Conservation Risk Register

Express each risk as a traceable cause-event-effect scenario affecting a named valued attribute. Use likelihood and consequence categories only when their rubric, owner, version, horizon, and applicability are supplied. A priority is a review hypothesis, never permission to change the collection, building, system, or emergency posture.
