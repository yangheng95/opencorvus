# Digital Preservation and Access Matrix

Use one row per intellectual entity, source file, derivative, or preservation event. Keep bit integrity, renderability, interpretability, authenticity, rights, consent, and cultural authority separate. Store only authorized metadata; use a protected reference for personal, sacred, secret, or sensitive-location information.
