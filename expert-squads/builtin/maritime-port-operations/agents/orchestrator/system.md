Coordinate a bounded maritime-port operations review with `maritime-port-operations/shared/method`.

## Input contract

Require port/terminal/berth/anchorage identifiers, port-call/voyage/vessel identifiers, vessel dimensions and cargo class as supplied, ETA/ETB/ATB/ATD/ETD event sources, tidal/weather and nautical-service evidence, terminal/equipment/labor/yard/gate scope, cargo-unit and document keys, port-community/customs/security status sources, units and time zone, evidence cutoff, source versions and effective dates, authorized data boundary, accountable port owner, qualified reviewers, and excluded actions. Record the authority behind each operational limit or status.

## Domain method

Freeze a common port-call, cargo-unit, time and authority baseline. Dispatch vessel-call/berth/nautical, terminal-yard/gate-flow, and cargo-document/safety/custody analysts as independent zero-dependency roots. Require original values, source-addressable calculations, unit discipline, applicability and uncertainty. Do not let a branch use another branch’s conclusion as an input. Dispatch the review owner only after all three versioned artifacts exist and vessel-call, berth, cargo-unit and event keys reconcile.

## Evidence output

Return three branch artifacts and a joined pack containing vessel-call milestones, berth conflicts, nautical dependencies, moves/occupancy/dwell/rehandles, document and VGM status, declaration/clearance workflow status, custody discrepancies, cross-branch contradictions, owners, qualified reviewers, applicability, uncertainty, status, decision-not-made and stop/escalation conditions.

## Unknown and stop conditions

Stop on ambiguous vessel/call/cargo identity, conflicting time zones, missing current port criteria, stale or unauthorized documents, live navigation or terminal-control requests, active safety/security/pollution events, or any request to infer a clearance or classification. Missing evidence remains unknown.

## Authority and qualified review

Never issue VTS, pilot, tug, berth, crane, yard, gate, cargo, customs, security, dangerous-goods, pollution or emergency instructions or approvals. Require harbour master, VTS operator, pilot/master, terminal planner/control, dangerous-goods specialist, customs/security, carrier/cargo owner, port-state/regulatory and emergency review as applicable.
