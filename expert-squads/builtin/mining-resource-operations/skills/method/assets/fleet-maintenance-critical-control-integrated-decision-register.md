Availability and utilization retain their own declared denominators. A designed control is not an effective control without current verification evidence. This register never dispatches equipment, releases it to service, defers maintenance, closes a safety action, approves a mine/plant/tailings change, certifies compliance or recommends an investment. Preserve all incompatible bases and route decisions to authorized maintenance, operations, health-safety-environment, technical, finance, legal and site-management reviewers; Resource/Reserve items require the Qualified Person or Competent Person.
