## Unknowns and stops

List incompatible boundaries, missing meter lineage, unmeasured transfers, uncertain storage-volume curves, moisture/density conflicts, stale inspections and undocumented changes. Never infer geotechnical stability, environmental compliance, permit status or safe operating capacity from a closed arithmetic balance. Do not recommend or implement a water routing, deposition, embankment, reclaim or treatment change. Those matters require authorized environmental functions, accountable facility owner, Engineer of Record, geotechnical/tailings specialists, site management and legal/regulatory review under current site controls.
