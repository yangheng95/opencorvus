## Applicability and review boundary

This is a clean-room evidence-preparation method, not a reporting code, engineering standard, operating procedure or legal interpretation. Applicability must be set per site, jurisdiction, commodity, lifecycle stage and decision. Resource/Reserve statements and disclosure require the applicable Qualified Person or Competent Person; mine, blast, geotechnical and tailings changes require authorized technical functions; safety, environment, finance, legal and investment conclusions require their respective accountable reviewers. Source currency, local adoption and conflicts remain explicit uncertainties in every output.
