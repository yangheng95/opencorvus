# Selection guidance

Select this squad when the requested deliverable is a traceable, non-operational review across mining evidence domains: geological sampling and QA/QC, model and resource assumptions, plan-to-actual and grade-control reconciliation, stockpile movements, metallurgical accounting, recovery, water and tailings dependencies, fleet availability/utilization, maintenance exposure, and critical-control verification.

Do not select it to produce or approve a Resource or Reserve statement, public technical disclosure, cut-off or economic viability decision, mine-plan authorization, blast design, ground-support instruction, tailings change, equipment dispatch, permit or compliance conclusion, safety release, clinical or legal opinion, investment recommendation, or autonomous control. If the request depends on those acts, the scheduler must narrow the task to evidence preparation and name the qualified reviewer who must decide.

Before dispatch, require a named site and commodity, orebody or operational boundary, model/plan/reconciliation period, data cut-off, unit conventions, dry/wet basis, measurement reference points, reporting code if any, and accountable owner. Missing or conflicting bases are stop conditions, not invitations to estimate.
