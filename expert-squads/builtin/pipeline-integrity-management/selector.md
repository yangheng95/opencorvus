# Selection guidance

Select for a bounded regulated pipeline integrity evidence review involving segment/configuration lineage, threat integration, inspection or assessment runs, indication/anomaly correlation, excavation, repair, remediation, or management of change.

Do not select for petroleum well integrity, process-unit safety, standalone materials failure analysis, energy planning, live leak or rupture response, pipeline control, pressure change, excavation/repair authorization, return-to-service, or regulatory filing. Those require authorized systems and qualified human roles.
