# Omnichannel Distribution method

Preserve campaign truth while adapting format, length, tone, call to action, disclosures, and asset roles to current channel evidence. UTM means Urchin Tracking Module and names link-attribution parameters. Record attribution limitations and rights status. Never use browser actions or manual narration as a substitute for a configured publishing connector.
