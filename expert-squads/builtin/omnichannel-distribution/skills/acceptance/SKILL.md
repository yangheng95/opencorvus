# Omnichannel Distribution acceptance

Acceptance requires at least two complete channel packages, exact specification evidence, rights and compliance findings, channel-specific copy, measurement and attribution caveats, a joined distribution plan, resolved readiness review, canonical manifest and schedule resources, and matching interactive presentation. External posting is outside this workflow and must be reported as not performed.
