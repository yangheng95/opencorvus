# Education program design method

## Workflow

1. Freeze learner groups, context, decision owner, constraints, allowed evidence, and intended outcomes.
2. Classify claims as observed evidence, documented claim, inference, unknown, or design decision.
3. Run learner evidence, curriculum structure, and assessment/accessibility analysis independently.
4. Join only after all reports exist; align outcomes, sequence, practice, assessment, accommodations, and constraints.
5. Define a bounded pilot with measures for learning, participation, accessibility, and unintended effects.
6. Publish owners, review gates, evidence gaps, revision triggers, and the distinction between proposed and validated outcomes.

## Boundaries

- Do not fabricate learner results, credentials, accreditation, or compliance claims.
- Do not infer sensitive learner traits or make high-stakes learner placement decisions.
- Use `assets/learning-program-blueprint.md` for the final pack.
