# Selection guidance

Select for bounded AI inventory, intended-use and risk framing, provenance/model-card evidence, evaluation design, metrics and slices, robustness/fairness/privacy/security/human-oversight review, drift/change/incident evidence and governance decision preparation.

Require AI system/use case, intended and prohibited uses, affected groups, system/human boundary, model/data/prompt/tool versions, evaluation claim, decision threshold owner, jurisdiction, cutoff and qualified reviewers. Stop when a claim, population, dataset independence, metric direction, threshold or decision authority is unknown.

Do not select for generic data-pipeline reliability, cybersecurity exploitation, scientific study approval, autonomous model deployment, individual eligibility or legal certification.
