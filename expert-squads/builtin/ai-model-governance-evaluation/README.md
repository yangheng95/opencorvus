# AI Model Governance and Evaluation

This squad prepares a versioned model-governance and evaluation decision pack for a named AI system and use case. Four roots independently assess use-case risk and accountability; model/data/prompt/tool provenance; evaluation protocol and result evidence; and trustworthiness counterevidence. The join preserves uncertainty and routes risk decisions to authorized humans.

The evaluation-shape, deterministic-metric, minimal pass/fail smoke, row-level-plus-aggregate inspection and validate-before-scale principles are bounded adaptations of NVIDIA NeMo's Apache-2.0 `nemo-evaluator-plugin` Skill at a fixed commit. All NeMo CLI/SDK/API/platform job, credentials, scripts and vendor-specific operations are excluded. NIST-grounded governance is original package work. Exact upstream LICENSE and NOTICE are retained in `skills/method/references/`.

This squad cannot deploy, replace or retire a model, accept risk, certify compliance, make protected-class or high-impact eligibility decisions, or launch external evaluation jobs.
