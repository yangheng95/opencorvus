## Findings

1. Failed GUI job: [107050503689](https://github.com/yangheng95/opencorvus/actions/runs/35820194375/job/107050503689), `overlay (darwin-x64) / compile (darwin-x64)`, step `Package GUI installers`. Compiler output reports `Finished release profile` and the built `opencorvus-overlay` executable. The executable stage completes at 05:05:52 UTC in 288.45 seconds.
2. `.app` creation and ad-hoc signing continue successfully. At 05:05:57.737 UTC Tauri starts `bundle_dmg.sh` for `OpenCorvus_0.1.13_x64.dmg`; at 05:06:22.128 it reports script failure. The enclosing bundle stage exits 1 after 29.18 seconds. This is a DMG (Disk Image) packaging failure, not a Rust or frontend compilation error.
3. The runner is `macos-15-intel`, macOS 15.7.9 / 24G830, image 20260824.0482.1. Published v0.1.12 job 106666593158 used exactly that OS/image and successfully bundled its app/DMG. The Overlay build/bundler/workflow and dependency versions did not change between v0.1.12 and 0778b617; the lockfile changed only release-family versions. Tauri CLI is locked to 2.11.4.
4. Same-run ARM64 job 107050503685 successfully finishes app/DMG packaging in 31.51 seconds. Its macOS 26.6.2 runner is not an identical reproduction of Intel. Both successful ARM64 and preceding successful Intel logs contain the same skipped-notarization warning, so that warning does not establish the cause of this failure.
5. The precise inner error is missing from the retained job log. Tauri's [DMG owner](https://github.com/tauri-apps/tauri/blob/tauri-cli-v2.11.4/crates/tauri-bundler/src/bundle/macos/dmg/mod.rs) calls `output_ok()`. Its [CommandExt implementation](https://github.com/tauri-apps/tauri/blob/tauri-cli-v2.11.4/crates/tauri-bundler/src/utils/mod.rs) captures child stdout/stderr, emits them only at debug level, and replaces a nonzero exit with a generic program-name error. Our `packages/overlay/script/build.ts:151` invokes the bundler without verbose logging. Thus the surrounding Bun ShellError repeats only the generic error, not the failing `hdiutil`/filesystem operation. The sole check annotation is also just exit code 1.
6. The failed-job retention path contains native first-run diagnostics only; no such artifact exists for this job, and the compiled Intel app/DMG intermediate was not uploaded after bundle failure. Runner cleanup ended a `diskimages-help` process, but its presence does not prove a busy-disk, timeout or mount failure. No such cause is asserted.
7. A prior v0.1.8 Intel job had the same outer error and succeeded on a same-source job retry. That establishes recurrence and a prior recovery outcome, not the underlying cause of the present failure.
