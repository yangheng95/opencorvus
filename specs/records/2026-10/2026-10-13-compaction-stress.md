## Research and decisions

Sources read on 2026-10-13; these motivate experiments, not assumed OpenCorvus results:

- [OpenAI compaction](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/compaction): native compacted windows include opaque state and retained items. Preserve canonical returned state if a provider-native path is ever implemented; do not equate a natural-language summary with it. This task retains the current portable streaming mechanism, rather than adding a parallel non-streaming provider path.
- [Anthropic context engineering](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents): bounded high-signal context plus retrievable external evidence; evaluate recall before aggressive compression.
- [Lost in the Middle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172): vary important fact positions, not just total history length.
- [LongMemEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10813): score extraction, updates, temporal ordering, cross-message reasoning and abstention separately. Keep probe questions out of summarization prompts to avoid answer-conditioned retention.
- [LongLLMLingua](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06839): prompt compression addresses a different token-selection problem; do not remove instruction/provenance tokens solely for a compression ratio.
- [AgentFold](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24699): test repeated consolidation and selective retrieval; its trained agent results do not establish quality of this untrained harness.

Decision: first repair deterministic loss at the existing transcript/reader boundary, then tune the single compaction prompt from real failures. Keep original messages as authority, bounded retrieval as evidence access and visible assistant summary as continuation. No synthetic user instructions, shadow memory, fallback summary or regex instruction routing.

### Additional research and its consequences

Read on 2026-10-13; reported research outcomes are not OpenCorvus measurements:

- [Governance Decay / ConstraintRot, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.22528v2) compares full, compacted, absent and pinned policies and grades action arguments. It separates ordinary organization-specific restrictions from strong model priors. Its pinning stress tests still fail under impersonated recent authority; therefore pinning is not a proof of perfect instruction preservation. Apply the experimental separation and authority-conflict tests, not an unreviewed rule extractor or parallel policy store.
- [The Compaction Cliff](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.22752v1) studies repeated retention and typed preservation. Its guarantee depends on classifier recall and a feasible budget; the paper also identifies unequal token budgets in one behavioral comparison. This motivates at least five rounds and matched context budgets, with retrieval, overhead and attribution measured separately. Do not import headline percentages as product results.
- [What to Keep, What to Forget](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.08032v1) frames compaction as retaining future task utility under a resource budget and emphasizes uncertainty about future queries. Our inference: keep canonical evidence recoverable and test held-out needs after repeated compression; prose similarity or compression ratio alone cannot establish fidelity.
- [REMORY](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11287v1) learns soft memory vectors to supplement a textual checkpoint, and evaluates source attribution and subsequent agent behavior. It requires a trained memory network and access to actor features/input vectors; it is not an API-only prompt setting. Its frontier reference bars are explicitly not controlled comparisons. Treat it as a reason to evaluate native opaque state separately, not as a feature this repository already implements.

Current-corpus gap confirmed from runner source: deployment denial is in the permanently retained anchor. The existing hostile Tool/diagnostic cases prove resistance to overriding that visible restriction, while repeated region correction proves update retention. They do **not** yet prove that a later permission revocation or new organization rule survives five compressions, nor that a model's reported permission predicts its actual next Tool arguments. Next benchmark requirements: place genuine policy updates in the replaced middle; distinguish later user corrections from lookalike Tool text; test allowed as well as restricted actions; capture every emitted action against an isolated harmless execution surface; pair full-history and compacted runs at matched budgets and retain per-round attribution.
