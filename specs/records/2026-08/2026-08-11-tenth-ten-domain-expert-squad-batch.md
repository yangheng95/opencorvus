## Non-overlap and authority boundaries

- Radiation Therapy Physics Quality Assurance owns source-bound commissioning, reference dosimetry, machine-quality, treatment-planning-system, patient-specific-quality, change, incident, and independent-audit evidence. It does not create treatment plans, prescribe dose, operate a machine, authorize beam-on, change a device, release a system, or replace a qualified medical physicist and radiation oncology team.
- Medical Device Postmarket Surveillance owns complaint, installed-base, event, vigilance, trend, Post-Market Clinical Follow-up (PMCF), Real-World Evidence (RWE), field-action, Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA), and risk-file trace evidence. It does not decide causality, seriousness, reportability, recall, compliance, submission, treatment, or field action.
- Clinical Biostatistics and Data Monitoring owns estimand, Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP), population, derivation, model, missing-data, multiplicity, sensitivity, interim, blinding, and monitoring evidence. It does not choose unapproved methods or thresholds, unblind data, recommend stop/continue, certify results, sign an analysis, or make a clinical decision.
- Internal Audit Control Assurance owns charter-bound universe/risk, control objective, design, walkthrough, population/sample, operating-effectiveness, exception, finding, remediation, and closure evidence. It does not issue an audit opinion, determine material weakness/fraud, change a control, accept risk, or replace Internal Audit leadership, management, legal counsel, or external auditors.
- Mergers and Acquisitions Due Diligence owns authorized deal perimeter, Virtual Data Room (VDR) completeness, source-bound issue extraction, financial-quality reconciliation, commercial, legal, technology, people, and counterevidence. It does not value a company, negotiate, contact a party, decide materiality without an operator rule, make legal conclusions, file, sign, or recommend go/no-go.
- Advertising Measurement and Brand Safety owns campaign taxonomy, metric contracts, event lineage, delivery/viewability/invalid-traffic reconciliation, suitability evidence, attribution, incrementality, experiment, and claim trace. It does not change buying platforms, budgets, bids, creative, targeting, blocklists, publication, or make legal/compliance determinations.
- Records and E-Discovery Operations owns matter authority, retention/hold sources, custodian/system inventory, collection provenance, processing, deduplication, family relationships, search/review evidence, privilege flags, production mapping, and disposition questions. It does not collect from live systems, delete, release a hold, decide privilege/responsiveness/admissibility/spoliation, produce records, or provide legal advice.
- Fire Protection Engineering Assurance owns adopted-basis identity, passive and active system evidence, interface/testing records, modeling provenance, inspection findings, impairment, and qualified-review routing. It does not declare code compliance, design a system, set engineering thresholds, isolate/reset equipment, order evacuation, authorize emergency action, or replace the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) and registered fire-protection professionals.
- Power Grid Protection and Reliability Assurance owns topology/baseline, protection-zone/device, study, setting, coordination, breaker/communication, disturbance, misoperation, outage, and reliability-definition evidence. It does not access or change relays/SCADA/EMS, approve settings or switching, trip/reclose/energize, declare misoperation/root cause, or replace protection engineers, system operators, asset owners, cybersecurity, and regulators.
- Oceanographic Observation Data Assurance owns platform/instrument/deployment/profile identity, calibration provenance, coordinate/time/depth/pressure semantics, raw-adjusted-derived separation, Quality Assurance of Real-Time Oceanographic Data (QARTOD) test provenance, format metadata, and cross-platform validation. It does not change sensors, overwrite flags, delete or publish data, operate a platform, issue forecasts/warnings/navigation advice, or make marine safety/compliance conclusions.
