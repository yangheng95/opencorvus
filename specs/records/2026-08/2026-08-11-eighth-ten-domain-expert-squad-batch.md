## Non-overlap and operating boundaries

### One Person Company operating system

This is the company-level state and reconciliation owner for an owner-operated business. It does not replace `marketing-growth`, `sales-strategy`, `finance-operations`, `customer-success`, `product-management`, `ecommerce-merchandising`, `tax-compliance`, or `commercial-legal`. It freezes one operating profile, reconciles offer, demand, revenue, cash, obligations, delivery, capacity, customer state, automation, approvals, and evidence cutoffs, then routes specialized questions to the existing domain Squad or qualified professional.

No output sends, publishes, pays, refunds, signs, files, deploys, deletes, changes permissions, contacts a customer, or represents self-review as independent separation of duties. Legal, tax, accounting, financing, insurance, privacy, employment, and regulated conclusions remain with qualified humans. External actions require the owner’s explicit approval and an `outcome_unknown` state must be reconciled before retry.

### Infrastructure, environmental, enterprise, financial, and transport domains

- Pipeline Integrity owns regulated pipeline segment, threat, assessment, anomaly, excavation, repair, and change evidence; it does not replace petroleum well integrity, process safety, materials failure analysis, or energy planning.
- Meteorological Assurance owns observation metadata/QC, forecast issue/valid/lead-time provenance, alignment, and verification; it does not issue forecasts or warnings and does not replace climate adaptation, GIS, emergency command, or aviation operations.
- Hazardous Waste owns waste-stream, generator-site, accumulation, manifest, and final-disposition evidence; it does not replace chemical process safety, wastewater, sustainability reporting, logistics, or customs.
- Identity Access Governance owns identity lifecycle, accounts, entitlements, role/SoD evidence, and certification; it does not provision access or replace HR, cybersecurity assurance, or privacy operations.
- Backup Recovery Assurance owns workload/data-copy recoverability evidence and isolated restore validation; it does not execute recovery or replace continuity management, cloud architecture, cybersecurity, or data engineering.
- Securities Post-Trade owns trade capture through clearing, custody, DVP/RVP, settlement, fails, and reconciliation; it does not trade, move assets, or replace finance operations, payments fraud, forensic accounting, or equity research.
- Air Traffic Management Safety owns airspace/facility/procedure configuration, demand/capacity evidence, hazards/controls, occurrences, and change assurance; it does not issue live clearances or replace aircraft maintenance, satellite operations, rail, or urban mobility.
- Cloud FinOps owns technology cost/usage ingestion, allocation, unit economics, forecast, commitment, anomaly, and governance evidence; it does not buy commitments, change resources, book entries, set budgets, or replace cloud architecture or finance operations.
- Service Reliability Incident Operations owns service-level indicators/objectives, error-budget evidence, observability/alert quality, incident evidence coordination, handoff, and post-incident learning; it does not execute production mitigation, declare severity, send external communications, attribute security incidents, or replace cybersecurity, cloud architecture, or data engineering.

## OPC commercial configuration contract

The one source of truth is `skills/method/references/opc-config.schema.json`, Draft 2020-12. Four instances live under `skills/method/examples/`: `micro-saas.json`, `consulting.json`, `creator-media.json`, and `digital-product-commerce.json`.

Required top-level fields are `schema_version`, `profile_id`, `profile_version`, `business_model`, `company`, `owner`, `localization`, `operating_state`, `review_cadence`, `data_sources`, `secret_refs`, `offer_portfolio`, `customer_channels`, `pipeline`, `delivery_capacity`, `cash_controls`, `metric_definitions`, `workflows`, `approval_policy`, `observability`, `retention_policy_ref`, `continuity`, `uncertainty`, `applicability`, and `stop_conditions`.

- `automation_mode` defaults to `observe_and_draft`; `external_write`, `auto_approve`, and `auto_retry` default to false. Any bounded retry must be explicitly idempotent, reversible, budgeted, approved, and reconciled.
- Time zone is an explicit IANA identifier plus `tzdb_version`. Functional and transaction currencies are explicit three-letter codes with exchange-rate source/time when conversion is used. Jurisdiction is never inferred from locale, currency, address, or company name.
- Data sources carry system-of-record identity, locator/import mechanism, authorization reference, refresh cadence, freshness, reconciliation key, data class, and owner. Secrets are provider-neutral references only; plaintext `secret`, `token`, `password`, and `api_key` properties are rejected by schema and test.
- Metrics carry business definition, formula version, numerator, denominator, unit, source, cutoff, cadence, owner, and uncertainty. No profile hard-codes an industry target or claims a benchmark is universally valid.
- Approval policy explicitly covers public claims, price/discount, contract, payment/refund, procurement, tax filing, data export/deletion, production deployment, permissions/secrets, and bulk contact. Same-person prepare/review is recorded as `self_review`, never independent review or separation of duties.
- Observability records event/correlation IDs, workflow/input versions, source cutoff, approval reference, action class, external effect, output locator, duration, cost, result, retry, error, and `outcome_unknown`.

The four examples are deliberately different:

- Micro-SaaS binds billing/subscription/entitlement, product analytics, support, deployment/uptime, and accounting exports; price, refund, deployment, migration, entitlement, terms/privacy, and customer-data actions require approval.
- Consulting binds lead/opportunity, proposal/contract, time/capacity, deliverable, invoice/payment/expense, and client-communication evidence; proposals, scope/price changes, signatures, subcontractors, deliverables, invoices, and refunds remain external approvals.
- Creator/Media binds content/rights, editorial calendar, audience analytics, newsletter/customer, sponsor/affiliate, and revenue/payment evidence; publication, sponsored claims, rights, disclosures, audience export, offer/price, and refunds remain approvals.
- Digital Product/Commerce binds catalog/SKU/rights, storefront orders, payment/refund, entitlement/download/fulfillment, optional inventory/vendor, support/returns, accounting, and tax-configuration evidence; it reconciles order/payment/fulfillment before any retry and never double-charges, double-refunds, or double-fulfills.
