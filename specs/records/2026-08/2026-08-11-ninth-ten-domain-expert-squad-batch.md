## Non-overlap and authority boundaries

- Clinical Genomics reviews variant evidence identity, source versions, criteria provenance, conflicts, and uncertainty; it does not diagnose, classify a patient variant, recommend care, sign a report, or replace a qualified molecular laboratory/genetics professional.
- Transfusion Assurance owns component/specimen/order/compatibility/issue/transfusion/reaction evidence continuity; it does not determine compatibility, select or release a component, transfuse, diagnose a reaction, advise a patient, or write a blood-bank system.
- Medical Device Human Factors owns use specification, critical-task, use-error, formative, summative, and traceability evidence; it does not certify compliance, approve a protocol, recruit or expose participants, change a device, submit to a regulator, or declare residual risk acceptable.
- Dam Safety owns facility configuration, inspection, instrumentation, behavior, potential-failure-mode, and control evidence; it does not operate gates, set alarm thresholds, declare safety, issue warnings, order evacuation, or approve engineering work.
- Bridge Integrity owns bridge identity, element condition, load/scour/fatigue evidence, repair/action and independent quality review; it does not rate, post, close, reopen, permit loads, operate traffic control, or approve repair.
- Marine Vessel Assurance owns vessel/statutory/class source separation, survey, maintenance, defect, repair and verification evidence; it does not declare seaworthiness/class/certificate validity, operate equipment, authorize sailing, issue a survey, or accept a defect.
- Corporate Governance Secretariat prepares source-bound meeting/entity/consent evidence and action calendars; it does not give legal advice, determine quorum/authority/validity, sign minutes/consents, file, contact directors or regulators, or replace corporate counsel and the authorized secretary.
- Corporate Treasury owns cash-position, forecast, funding, payment, settlement, bank and reconciliation evidence; it does not move money, approve a payment, trade, borrow, hedge, open accounts, set limits, book entries, or make investment/accounting/legal conclusions.
- Student Financial Aid owns source-versioned applicant, verification, eligibility-input, cost, packaging, award, disbursement and reconciliation evidence; it does not decide eligibility, change an institutional record, calculate from invented rules, originate/disburse/return funds, contact a student, or make a regulatory determination.
- Digital Accessibility owns criterion-to-evidence coverage, automated and manual observations, assistive-technology evidence, remediation trace and exception questions; it does not certify conformance, silently change production, infer disability, replace disabled-user testing, or make legal conclusions.
