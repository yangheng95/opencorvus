# Material origin and release semantics

This benchmark's author created the supplied synthetic metering/operating material. It describes no real utility, person or institution and carries no claimed licence from an external data supplier. Use is confined to the supplied evaluation/research context; no additional redistribution licence is asserted here. There are four leaf stations A1/A2/B1/B2 and additive zones ZA=A1+A2, ZB=B1+B2.

The canonical quantity is litres (L). Original observation rows can use L or cubic metres (m3), including different units for revisions of the same day. Exact rational conversion uses 1000 L/m3 before checking integer litres. A daily event is [UTC00:00,next UTC00:00); its end timestamp is the business coordinate. The fixed public epoch is 2022-01-01T00:00:00Z, one tick per hour. Temperature covariates use tenths of a degree Celsius, converted by the reference predictor to Celsius.

Raw `observations.csv` and `covariate-vintages.csv` retain actual release timestamps, business timestamps, revision IDs and quality flags. Static series/hierarchy/availability/units define identities and knowledge roles, not future outcomes. An origin receives only original rows released by its cutoff. Observed quantities must also precede the origin; known-future calendars, issued forecasts and operating notices may extend forward if already released. Highest released revision wins even when missing; do not revive an older valid value. Event/release/revision and original row units remain the provenance keys. Terminal labels are later revision2 observations, supplied only to evaluation/authorized selection/report stages.

Public development stops at 2024-08-01T09:00:00Z. The six 2023 exploration origins are public exploratory material, not blind tests and not permission to expose their later revisions to earlier forecasts. All36 inner origins are later than the development cutoff. Twelve outer origins occur in2025, monthly on the15th except June20. Each has three earlier inner origins. Forecasts start with the first complete UTC day after cutoff, with14 explicit end ticks. Issued weather covers only the first13 of those days; the last forecast weather value is genuinely unavailable. Do not import the next issue or realized weather.

Original archive rows, public material/units/release contract and the assigned target grids are the data authority. The generator and full canonical/terminal archive are not forecast inputs. The reporter receives full accepted results after numerical execution and may discuss observed failures; that permission cannot retroactively authorize training on future labels. This note is frozen explanatory material, not a second scheduling or data configuration.
