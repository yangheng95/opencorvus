# Frozen method specification

This is an original candidate study on the original synthetic public materials described in PROVENANCE.md. No private outer data or reference trajectories have been accessed. submission.json is the single method-pool and program declaration. Public development may inform design; formal inner/outer source and configurations must remain those sealed before the first private prediction. The report program is part of that seal.

## Scientific question and hypotheses
Can legal weather, calendar seasonality and operating-state information improve 14-day hierarchical mean/distribution forecasts over a recent weekday mean? Does removing weather from both point fitting and uncertainty reconstruction change that benefit? We do not require either extension or weather mechanism to win. Paired differences are descriptive, without treating dates, hierarchy nodes or overlapping origins as independent replicates.

## Data and permission
UTC date intervals are represented by their next-midnight end ticks. A raw row locator is packet-relative CSV filename and 1-based line plus its full business identity, release, revision, original value/unit and exact canonical value. observations.csv decimal values are read with Fraction, multiplied by the packet unit multiplier and checked to be integer L, before any float modeling. No historical global-unit assumption is used. At EVERY actual and pseudo-origin, eligible release<=origin rows are projected again; observations and observed variables also require end<origin. Highest revision wins, even if missing. Missing observations are excluded explicitly from fitting, never replaced with an older revision. The current packet is not treated as a snapshot at a past pseudo-origin. Registry rows need not be used in a fit; actual sample/feature refs identify those used.

Calendar weekday and annual phase are deterministic from UTC date; holiday comes from the latest legal plan, with explicit default0 if absent/missing. For operating state the explicit missing prior is open=1 and transfer=0. These are estimated/model states, not invented published facts. Observed historical temperature uses legal actual readings. Future temperature uses ONLY legal issued forecasts, corrected by an issue-lead bias estimated from legal historical forecast/actual pairs; this distinction is recorded in each feature. Temperature is converted from C_tenth to C. Missing historical temperature uses a legal seasonal climatology. Missing future temperature uses the mean anomaly of the last three available preceding TARGET-date issued forecasts over legal climatology, added to target-date climatology; when no preceding issued target forecast exists the climatology itself is used. For each fit origin, bias learning reconstructs the highest forecast revision released at historical issue-day09Z for leads1..13, and matches it to the highest actual temperature released at the fit origin. Missing highest forecasts are omitted as missing. Each lead correction is sum(forecast-minus-actual errors)/(n+14), a zero-bias14-day prior. No actual after the fit origin is used. Each pair and its issue cutoff are recorded. Missing-target extrapolation uses these bias-corrected preceding forecasts. Climatology is ridge-fit on legal actual temperature within the same365-day window: intercept/sin/cos, penalties(.001,1,1). In particular day14 never reads the next issue or future actual.

## Declared pool and finite search
- seasonal-8 (baseline): unweighted average of at most8 latest valid observations of the same weekday within365 days. At least2 are needed. No weather, holiday or operating adjustment. Its empirical residuals are uncentered.
- weather-operations (extension): separate per-leaf ridge weighted least squares on up to365 days, weights 2^(-age_days/90). Features: intercept, annual sine/cosine with365.25-day phase, Monday through Saturday indicators (Sunday reference), holiday, temperature/10C. A1 has an additive published transfer-in feature; A2 multiplies normal features by published open exposure and adds a closed-level feature. The ridge penalty is.5 except intercept.001. Coefficients minimize sum(weight*(y-X beta)^2)+sum(penalty*beta^2). The recent28-day raw residual sum divided by(n_recent+14) is added as a shrunk local intercept. At least28 valid training rows per leaf are needed. Location is clipped at0.
- without-weather (real ablation): same regression, window, shrinkage, operating features and calibration as the extension, with actual/forecast temperature and climate fitting entirely removed in main AND all pseudo-origin paths. Annual calendar features remain; the causal effect of weather is not identified by this observational comparison.

The numerical pool is exactly the three configurations in submission.json, selection IDs include baseline, extension and the real ablation, outer IDs all three. No additional unpublished search is encoded. Initial constants were chosen before the first public model run. Public cold-002 found raw issued-weather regression inferior to its no-weather ablation; before any private run the final design added explicit issue-lead forecast-bias correction and included the ablation in the selection pool. Both changes and the original negative results are retained in specs/development.md. No private feedback was used. The local level is regularized to respond to an unannounced level shift AFTER observations arrive, not to foresee it.

Operating caveat: open=0 is a structural zero-exposure prior for normal A2 demand; the closed-level coefficient can learn a nonzero level from legal closed observations. No preclosure variation identifies that coefficient. Similarly transfer coefficient is ridge-shrunk to0 until a transfer-on regime is observed. Public development has no state variation. This is an explicit modeling assumption, not a known volume from an announcement or a causal claim. Uncertainty under a never-observed closure is underidentified: A2 residual spread is scaled by exposure; a closed zero-prior forecast can be overconfident. A1's initial transfer volume is unknown. These vulnerabilities must be reported, not hidden by annual averages.

## Distribution and calibration
For each method/origin refit the SAME configuration at origins56,84,112 days earlier, reconstructing its own legal observations and features. Forecast the corresponding14 complete future days. Residual labels are the HIGHEST revisions released at the CURRENT actual origin, including missing. They are allowed calibration labels but are NOT the labels used in the past fits. Keep only complete four-leaf dates (at least14 vectors; normally42). diagnostics records both lineages and all residuals.

The baseline uses raw residuals, so its distribution mean includes estimated bias. Regression/ablation subtract each leaf's residual sample mean to maintain the fitted conditional-mean target. For each future date, add each centered/raw paired residual vector to the nonnegative point location, truncate leaf samples to0, and form each aggregate by summing the paired leaf samples with registered weights. A2 regression residual spread is multiplied by its operating exposure. The reported mean is the sample expectation of this empirical predictive distribution; near zero truncation may shift it from location. Parent means add exactly within protocol tolerance. Quantiles use NumPy's linear interpolation at(p*(n-1)); aggregates have their own sample quantiles and need not equal sums of child quantiles. Pairing represents empirical same-date dependence, not a validated joint temporal/path distribution. A42-vector empirical tail and historical regime assumption do not provide guaranteed95% coverage; no conformal or distribution-free guarantee is claimed.

## Fixed model selection
select consumes only the supplied three complete registered inner frames, legal released labels and fixed positive exact scales. Per node/day MSE is((mean-y)/scale)^2; leaf and aggregate layers each have weight1/2, equally divided within layer, dates and inner origins. Python Fraction preserves JSON decimal values via str. Minimize the complete exact three-inner mean; exact ties choose Unicode codepoint method-ID order. Output the original ID only; no refit/weight/parameter is transferred. All methods, including ablation, execute on every official outer. Only registered inner loss chooses deployment; an ablation in the declared selection pool may win without changing its configuration.

## Research and decision quantities
For median plus three central intervals use contract WIS: (.5*absolute_median_error + sum(alpha/2*interval_score))/(3.5*scale), alpha=.5,.2,.05. Compare all methods and selected strategy on full common grids and per-node/per-origin; retain paired differences. Coverage and width are unscaled marginal intervals at50/80/95%, inclusive endpoints. Formal selected action is raw q.5/.75/.9 for shortage cost1/3/9 with surplus cost1. Decision ledger preserves action, truth, shortage/surplus and cost components; mean-action costs are a separate diagnostic. There is no capacity, inventory, transfer optimization, joint tail, path reliability or real-world institution certification.

Quality cannot be self-certified without evaluator's fixed external reference. Candidate baseline is not an authority for the external reference. Candidate score is never computed. A clean six-cut development run is not the formal12-outer/36-inner qualification. Report reconstructs metrics and its figures from the delivered numerical results, consumes actual fit diagnostics/provenance and emits all failures/limitations rather than inventing private truth.
