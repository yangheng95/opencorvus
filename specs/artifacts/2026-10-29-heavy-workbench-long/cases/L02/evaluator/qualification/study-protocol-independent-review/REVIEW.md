## Exact metrics and scientific meaning

For each node/target with positive fixed scale s, point loss is ((mean−truth)/s)^2, supplementary scaled MAE is |mean−truth|/s, and interval loss is WIS/s. WIS=[.5|median−truth|+Σ(alpha/2)IS_alpha]/3.5 for alpha=.5,.2,.05, with IS=(upper−lower)+(2/alpha)(lower−truth)_++(2/alpha)(truth−upper)_+. Do not divide by the sum of weights or substitute the mean for the median. These formulas and averaging over fixed positive node/time weights follow the standard interval-score construction; the [original WIS paper](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008618) provides the method, and its [correction](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010592) is why copied illustration numbers are not used as an oracle.

Reviewer exact hand calculations confirm single MSE4/WIS6/7; hierarchical means12/20/32 with truth10/20/30 yield MSE3/WIS13/14 under unit scales and half-per-layer weights; all-zero degenerate quantiles give single WIS10. Under/over penalties were also exercised at truth1 and25, yielding37/7 and75/7 for the same intervals. Means target a different functional from medians: in a .9-at0/.1-at10 distribution, mean1 has MSE9 while median0 gives10. Therefore mean coherence, not quantile additivity or mean=median, is appropriate for this scoring protocol. Hierarchical means have linear aggregation semantics; this does not certify a coherent joint predictive path ([hierarchical forecast text](https://otexts.com/fpp3/hierarchical-forecasting.html)).

Nonunit anchors are necessary. With sA2/sB4/sZ6, exact scores are scaled MSE11/36, MAE5/12, WIS19/84; with the author's2/5/10 they are27/100,7/20,5/28. Unit-only checks cannot detect a wrong scale power or an omitted interval scale. Both nonunit triples actually passed independent real scoring CLIs. All these are numerical fixtures, not forecast quality or full study success.

The real scorer validates complete unique node/target keys, JSON numeric types (bool excluded), nonnegative finite bounded values, monotone declared quantiles and mean aggregation within public tolerance. It computes the full fixed grid before averaging; no missing forecast row is scored as a shorter easy sample. An actual complete two-target grid returns6rows; deleting one row gives prediction_incomplete/exit2. The score context remains evaluator-trusted: this primitive does not authenticate its origin/grid/scales against material registration. That must be done by the forthcoming runner, not attributed to this local function.
