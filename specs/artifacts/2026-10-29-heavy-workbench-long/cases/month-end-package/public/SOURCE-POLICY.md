# Public-source resolution policy for the April CFO draft

Finish the calculations supported by the inputs; explicitly escalate missing company evidence. Do not silently choose a source because its total is closest to the GL. Source-calculated amounts and original GL observations are distinct, even when both are correct calculations of different records.

- Use the original April TB's signed amounts and division/account identities. The March template provides structure and prior balances, not April truth. Restore net income/expense, clearing and overall proof semantics.
- Dedicated April prepaid schedules supply source-calculated vendor observations and GL reconciliations. Review their coverage text, descriptions and denominators; identify contradictions concretely. The combined prepaid workbook is another source version, with its actual periods retained. Do not infer approval, silently correct a contract term or make missing/invalid legacy checks zero.
- Count each signed legal transaction once. Distinguish transaction period from total year-to-date activity. Investigate source-backed components of cross-period bridges, keeping causal posting history and cutoff approval unknown where absent. A matching amount alone does not establish a posted journal.
- The two payroll copies contain overlapping amounts and differing business annotations. Count amounts once, preserve relevant unique instructions and disclose conflicting labels. Show supported accrual and partial salary/employee-expense bridges separately from a claim that the GL is wrong.
- A bonus source's fixed reported total and salary observation are not a live rollforward. Preserve their historical role, make current headlines refresh, and describe the unapproved estimate/compensation policy honestly.
- An AR amount matching one division does not establish the GA-0015 entity crosswalk. Preserve that source identity, both GL scopes and the missing mapping.
- Missing AP detail and bank-statement evidence remain unavailable. Present the known GL control, any source checks and proposed reclassification separately; provide a specific CFO request. Funding subsidiary split, mapping and company policies must not be invented.

No private expert workbook or original44-item rubric is authoritative for unavailable supplier amounts, unapproved ledger changes, duplicate subtotals or unsupported company judgments. They are excluded from candidate context and numerical expectations. This is a clarified local CFO-review comparison, not an official GDPval score or signed financial statement.
