# Complete original operational and observation law

The three `instances/*` directories contain separate complete authoritative inputs. `profile.json` specifies integer costs/capacity/cash, `weeks.csv` uses explicit week IDs, `suppliers.csv` uses supplier IDs, `latent-law.csv` gives exact label priors and the good-lot likelihood for each supplier, and `opening-receivables.csv` contains already-earned opening receivables. CSV row order has no business meaning. All inputs are purpose-built synthetic originals, not measured suppliers. Do not merge the three priors or opening cash states.

At the beginning of week0 initialize the declared cash and zero material. At every subsequent week beginning, receive original normal orders due now into that supplier's quarantine slot and collect only receivables due now. Every supplier has at most one retained quarantine pack; one normal order buys one pack and arrives next week. The total normal order rate is at most one pack per week. Do not place an order if the retained slot will already contain a pack on arrival. A retained pack is unobserved; purchase, nonselection, delayed inspection or lack of a stock receipt is not a failed/successful quality observation.

Laboratory stage: skip or inspect one actually available pack. Inspection costs1, removes the pack, then yields exactly2 good units on `good` or0 on `reject`. Both outcomes have strictly positive probability. Even before current production consumes material, capacity4 must accommodate the good outcome; do not admit an inspection whose successful outcome would exceed that peak. Normal stock and cash cannot be represented by expected yield. The result, chosen action and original key are one durable receipt. All aliases with identical per-supplier likelihood rows are observationally equivalent for every adaptive policy. Conditional on a class, successive results are independent; use exact Bayesian conditioning on the complete paid-inspection history, not on an imagined future class.

Operations stage sees the actual laboratory result and its post-state. Choose an integer number of immediate certified good units and at most one new normal supplier order. Good material after certified purchase must fit capacity4 and cover all current demand. Production/shipment then consumes exactly the demanded units, charges production cost, and creates the original sale receivable at week+2. Sales are not current cash. New normal orders pay their pack cost now and must arrive before the eight-week horizon ends. All payments reduce settled cash; every intermediate stage requires cash≥−15. No loan, sale of raw stock, retrospective cancellation, unauthorized destruction or retroactive receivable reassignment is supplied.

At ordinary week end, remaining good units incur the declared holding charge. Retained quarantine packs persist unaltered and have no holding charge. On final week7, no incoming order may remain. Automatically dispose all remaining good units and retained packs at the declared unit/pack disposal charges, clear them, and require settled cash≥0. Do not also charge ordinary end-horizon holding. Report remaining sales receivables at their absolute due weeks8/9, without collecting them in the current horizon. Exact expected cost is all normal/certified/production/laboratory/holding/disposal expenses, not sales revenue or ending receivables.

`model.py` is the public single exact primitive for loading these tables, Bayesian conditioning, full-prefix packet reconstruction and physical transitions. It contains no planner, selected future, grader or reference. Errors have explicit `ContractError.code` identities. The raw tables and above equations remain the business specification; using the helper does not itself prove a policy, an optimum, a physical observation, an executed durable recovery or an independent review.
