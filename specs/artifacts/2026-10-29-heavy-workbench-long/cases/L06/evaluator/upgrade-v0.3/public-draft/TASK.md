# Staged factory operations: policy, execution, recovery and handoff

This is a synthetic limited manufacturing business. Read the original raw tables, OPERATIONS.md and tree-contract.json. Build a complete causal purchasing/production policy for all finite public-support paths, an executable current-observation actor, exact business ledger, usable report/figures and reproducible handoff. Chinese or English and any correct language/method are allowed. This development draft is not yet frozen.

At each week observe all due historical receipts/arrivals, the current demand/supplier capacity/plant hours and the complete prior committed history. Then place new orders, receive lead0 purchases, produce, deliver all current demand, check pre-consumption/pre-dispatch storage peaks, and settle actual costs/holding/cash. No future actual path or future event is given to the actor. All possible support paths are public; that does not reveal which future is selected. Same complete observation prefix requires the same complete action, independently of a path label. Different observations may lawfully lead to the same action.

Integer pack orders, supplier MOQ/freight/lead times, BOM, route restrictions/shared hours, both warehouse peaks, delayed sales receipts, credit and terminal rules are simultaneous business requirements. Historical orders remain irrevocable; a later supply restriction does not cancel their arrival. Post-week inventory/cash/queues derive from the original committed prefix. Do not edit prior actions, spend a late receivable early or repair an invalid state by declaring another inventory. Missing facts/errors need explicit diagnosis, never an invented approval or guarantee.

Deliver policy.json with format l06-tree-policy/1 and decisions for every public scenario. Each decision object has order_lots[week][supplier][raw], truck_on[week][supplier], production[week][plant][product]; nonnegative integer quantities, binary trucks. Declaration of raw/finished inventory is optional and cannot override actual business. Raw CSV explicit indices determine array order, row order is not meaning. The offline policy must be physically feasible and nonanticipative throughout, not only initially.

Deliver all actor source/assets/dependencies and submission.json with format l06-online-submission/1 and argv, a nonempty array of strings. Runtime provides fixed readonly /project and /public, no network,1 CPU; source may implement any correct method. Actor exchanges one JSON line per current week on stdin/stdout. No credentials, Provider, evaluator, reference policy or other projects are inputs. No minimum duration or total token/wall budget; work until complete, preserve true failures and do not wait to create hours.

Compare the policy to an actually executable current-needs spot alternative. The pre-solve quality goal is expected cost at least15% lower than this canonical alternative, with all physical/information/actual execution requirements passing. A weak candidate-supplied baseline cannot replace it. An optional perfect-information relaxation must be labelled and bounded honestly; solver FEASIBLE/time limit/native bound is not exact optimality or an independent rational proof. No old0.2 lower bound applies.

Provide full-path weekly ledger and source mapping, cost components/probabilities/receivables and crisis/credit advice. Show actual figures. Distinguish finite business validity, source execution/recovery, optimization quality and unsupported real-world certification. A numerically correct table cannot excuse a false guarantee, fictitious approval or claim that simulated weeks prove agent working hours.
