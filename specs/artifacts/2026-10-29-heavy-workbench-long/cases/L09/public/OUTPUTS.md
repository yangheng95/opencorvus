# Complete course product

The delivery entry is delivery-index.json with keys course,report,forms,handover_checkpoint,handover_packet,handover_instructions,student_pack,teacher_pack,functional_grades,figures,figure_data,answer_programme,source_archive,evidence. The first six and answer_programme/source_archive/evidence are safe relative paths (archive/evidence can be directories); packs/figures/data are lists of safe relative paths. Machine course/score/checkpoint schemas are fixed, while teaching documents and visual file names/formats are free and indexed here. An equally complete PDF/HTML/table package is valid; no scoring by a privileged Markdown filename. Include all referenced actual evidence, raw packets or a directly runnable packet constructor, declared answer sources, glossary and worked inputs/query fragments, rather than referring to evaluator-side files the replacement teacher cannot access.

course.json: six sessions with id,hours,lab_id,objectives,prerequisites; total30h and every session5h. Twenty-four diagnosis records refer to original learner_id/observed_task/observed_counterexample, suggested support route and evidence-limited rationale. Observations are not invented stable abilities. The course may use any complete readable presentation; a source/asset manifest indexes its handouts and teacher answers.

Student pack: six lab handouts with observable objectives, prerequisites/input facts, runnable steps/expected state and typed failure interpretation, worked counterexample, meaningful scaffold/challenge and retrieval/reflection/transfer task. Teacher pack: answer programs, actual source-dependent results, lesson timings totaling5h/session, misconception/remediation based on diagnostics and actual trial, and explanation/functional grading distinctions.

Assessment pack: paired pre/post forms covering D/S/R/A with changed surface data, answers and explicit0/partial/full evidence anchors; isomorphic content rationale/remaining limits. No demand for statistical improvement. Rehearsal report records actual constructed operator/student commands, source/version, observed failure, actual material revision and valid rerun; no invented human operator or measured outcome. Capture original errors rather than overwriting them.

Replacement teacher: clean whole-run instructions and a separate W4 run from an actual W3 schema1 checkpoint, with original raw packet, origin/source/run receipt context and clear stop/retry expectations. A final database alone is insufficient. Code, inputs, expected ledger and audit must reproduce. Offer text/table alternatives to figures and keyboard-friendly CLI instructions; do not claim untested accessibility compliance.

Generated evidence: actual diagnostic-observation and functional-work figures with units/identity, companion data tables and interpretation; no fabricated before-after learning chart. Machine code/score results are checked by the evaluator. Prose, complete JSON, tables/formulas or alternative accessible formats are equally valid if they supply the same substantive information. Reference filenames are examples, not privileged grading keys.
