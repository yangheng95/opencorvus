# Original teaching commission
Deliver six five-hour sessions (30 learner contact/study hours) for the24 fictional profiles. Four fixed ability domains: exact data/identity(D), relational current-state reasoning(S), durable incremental engineering(R), scoped access/audit(A). The capstone is a tenant-safe, revision-correct, restartable release ledger with an evidence-based incident handover.
Provide student handouts, runnable teacher answers, real differentiated routes, comparable pre/post forms with answer/partial-credit anchors, actual rehearsal/repair records and a replacement-teacher pack. Hardware is one CPU/offline Python+SQLite; no cloud keys, paid accounts or live people. Any implementation language/SQL formulation with the contract is valid.
Scripts and diagnoses are constructed material. No claims of observed learning improvement, psychometric validity or real student identity are supported.30h is curriculum time, not agent effort. Failure or uncertainty must be taught and graded from evidence, not renamed as success.
