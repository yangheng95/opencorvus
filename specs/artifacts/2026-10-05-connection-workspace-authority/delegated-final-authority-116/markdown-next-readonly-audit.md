## Official primary grammar / concrete distinguishers

CommonMark0.31.2 §6.2 requires closing ** to be right-flanking; the preceding character cannot be whitespace, and if preceding punctuation then the following character must be whitespace or punctuation. Its §2.1 punctuation definition includes Unicode punctuation/symbol categories. Full-width colon U+FF1A and ideographic full stop U+3002 are punctuation; a following ordinary Han letter is not.

Therefore conceptual `**标签：**正文` and `**标题。**中文` are not valid strong spans under that closing rule, even though an author may intend bold. `**标签**：正文`, `**标签：** 正文` and end-of-line `**标签：**` have different lawful delimiter boundaries. Intraword strong itself is allowed with ** (officialexample396); do not incorrectly declare all adjacent Chinese text unsupported or solve it with an ASCII-word test. Escaped delimiters/code spans intentionally remain literal. These examples are normative reasoning, not an execution of current116 raw text.

Primary source: [CommonMark0.31.2 emphasis and strong emphasis](https://spec.commonmark.org/0.31.2/#emphasis-and-strong-emphasis), [character classes](https://spec.commonmark.org/0.31.2/#characters-and-lines). Installed current Marked GFM regex uses Unicode punctuation-aware delimiter rules; application sets no alternate CJK grammar. Standards-consistent literal stars are not by themselves a parser bug.
