# Warehouse handbook

Current operations guide for the demonstration stockroom.

## Opening notes

Use the north counter for new arrivals. Leave delivery paperwork in the labeled tray.

## Handoff checklist

1. Confirm the shipment and its location.
2. Record the time for the receiving team.
3. Send the handoff note to the duty lead.

## End of shift

Return the scanner to its dock and leave the desk ready for the next team.
